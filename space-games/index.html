What are space games?

Space games take place among the stars. The classic type is the space shooter, where you fly a ship and blast waves of aliens; there are also asteroid dodgers, rocket-building games, planet merge puzzles, alien invasion defense and astronaut adventures.

What makes a good one?

A good space game uses its setting: floaty physics, a sense of scale and enemies that move in interesting patterns. Upgrades for your ship give longer runs a goal.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

Beginners can start with planet merge puzzles or rocket launch games, which are slow-paced. If you like action, try the space shooters - they are some of the purest arcade games here.
