What are strategy and tower defense games?

Strategy games reward planning more than reflexes. In tower defense you place and upgrade towers along a path so that waves of enemies never reach your base. Real-time strategy (RTS) games have you gather resources, build units and push across a battlefield, while war and conquest games are about taking territory one region at a time.

What makes a good one?

A good strategy game gives you real choices: different towers or units that are each strong against something, and enough information to see why a plan failed. Waves that grow steadily, upgrade trees with trade-offs and a speed-up button for slow rounds all make a big difference.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

If you are new, start with a classic tower defense game - the path is fixed, so you can focus on where to put towers - and play the first levels on the easiest setting. Experienced players will get more out of RTS and conquest games, where you manage an economy and an army at the same time. Most strategy games save progress between levels in your browser.

Strategy & Tower Defense Games: questions and answers

What is a tower defense game?

A strategy game where enemies walk along a route towards your base and you build towers beside it to stop them. Between waves you earn coins to build more towers or upgrade the ones you have.

Are these strategy games free?

Yes. All 39 games in this category are free to play online without a download or account; some show short ads from the game provider.

Can I play strategy games on a tablet?

26 of the 39 games are marked touch-friendly. Tower defense works very well with taps; bigger RTS maps are easier with a mouse.
