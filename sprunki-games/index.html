What are Sprunki games?

Sprunki started as a fan-made music mixer where you drag sound-making characters onto a stage to build a song. The characters became a trend of their own, and now there are Sprunki puzzle, memory, coloring, driving, shooter and horror games as well as music mixers.

What makes a good one?

A good Sprunki game keeps the music front and centre - characters that each add a sound, loops that fit together - or uses the colorful cast well in a simple puzzle or memory game. Horror versions work best when the creepy twist is a surprise.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

Creative players should start with the music and beat-making games. Coloring and memory games are great for younger kids, while the horror and survival versions are for players who like a jump scare. These are unofficial fan games.
