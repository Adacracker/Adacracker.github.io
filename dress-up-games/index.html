What are dress-up games?

Dress-up games give you a character and a wardrobe and let you put together a look. Fashion games add a theme or a challenge - a red carpet, a winter party, a magazine cover - while makeover games walk you through skincare, makeup, hair and nails step by step. There are also doll and avatar makers where you design a character from scratch.

What makes a good one?

A good dress-up game has lots of clothes and accessories that mix well, a large preview of your character and an easy way to undo a choice. The best ones let you save or screenshot the final look and do not rush you with timers.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

Younger players or first-timers usually like simple outfit pickers and doll makers: tap an item to wear it. If you enjoy a goal, try the makeover games with several steps or the style challenges that score your look against a theme. Most of these games are made for touch screens as well as a mouse.

Dress Up Games: questions and answers

Are these dress up games free?

Yes. All 89 dress-up and fashion games are free to play in the browser without a download or account.

Do dress up games work on tablets and phones?

Most do: 74 of the 89 games are marked touch-friendly, and you only need to tap or drag clothes onto the character.

Can I save my outfit?

That depends on the game. Some have a photo or save button at the end; otherwise a screenshot is the easiest way to keep your favourite look.

Are there princess dress up games?

Yes, and we also have a separate Princess Games collection with royal outfits and fairy tale themes.
