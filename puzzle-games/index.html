What are puzzle games?

A puzzle game gives you a problem with clear rules and lets you solve it at your own pace. Here that means match-3 and tile-matching boards, block and Tetris-style puzzles, colour sorting, merge games, jigsaws, hidden-object scenes, draw-a-line physics puzzles and pure logic levels.

What makes a good one?

The best puzzle games introduce one new idea at a time and then twist it, so every level feels fair but fresh. Look for an undo button or hints, levels that can be finished in a few minutes and no harsh timers unless you want them.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

For a relaxing start, try matching, sorting or jigsaw games: you can play with one finger and there is no wrong move that ends the game. If you like a challenge, go for logic puzzles, physics puzzles and block games with limited moves - they get properly tricky after the first twenty levels. Puzzles are the easiest games to pause, so they fit a coffee break well.

Puzzle Games: questions and answers

Are these puzzle games free?

Yes, all 442 puzzle games are free to play in the browser with no download. Some show an ad between levels or offer an optional ad for a hint.

Which puzzle games work on phones?

288 of the 442 puzzle games are marked touch-friendly by their providers, and many play in portrait mode. Each game page tells you which way to hold your phone.

Do puzzle games save my progress?

Many level-based puzzles remember the levels you have finished in your browser's storage on this device. Clearing your browser data or switching devices starts you from the beginning.

Where did the mahjong and solitaire games go?

Mahjong, solitaire, chess and other classic table games are now in the Board & Cards category.
