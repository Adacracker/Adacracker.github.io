What are Christmas games?

Christmas games are ordinary game types wearing their holiday best: match-3 boards made of ornaments and candy canes, Santa delivering presents in a runner, gingerbread cookie makers, festive mahjong and solitaire, tree-decorating and dress-up games, and snowball fights. Because they cover every genre, there is something for every age in this collection.

What makes a good one?

A good Christmas game does more than paint snow on an old idea. Look for themed levels that actually use the holidays - wrapping gifts, decorating, sledding - a cosy soundtrack you will not want to mute, and difficulty that suits a relaxed family evening.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

Playing with young children? Pick decorating, colouring, dress-up or simple matching games that only need taps. For older kids and adults, the Christmas match-3, mahjong and solitaire games have hundreds of levels, and the runners and shooters are a good test of reflexes. Most of these games work on tablets, which makes them easy to pass around.
