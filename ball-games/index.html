What are ball games?

This collection is for every game built around a ball. That includes real sports - basketball, soccer, golf, bowling, pool - but also arcade games where you steer a rolling ball down a track, bounce one up a tower, aim a pinball or guide a marble through a maze.

What makes a good one?

Ball games are all about physics. A good one has a ball that rolls and bounces the way you expect, a clear aim or power indicator and levels that make you think about angles.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

For quick fun, pick a one-tap bounce game or a rolling-ball runner. If you want a skill to master, try mini golf, pool or basketball shooting, where small changes in angle and power matter. Most ball games are easy to play with a finger on a phone.
