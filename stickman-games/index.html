What are stickman games?

Stickman games use the simplest possible hero - a few lines and a circle - and spend everything else on movement and physics. Popular types are ragdoll fighting, stick war and army games, archery and spear duels, swinging-rope games, parkour runs and stickman versions of almost every other genre.

What makes a good one?

Because the art is minimal, a stickman game lives or dies by how it moves. Look for snappy animation, physics that react to every hit and controls that let you pull off a trick you planned. The minimal graphics also mean they run smoothly on older phones and laptops.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

Hook-swinging and one-tap parkour games are the easiest way in. If you like strategy, try a stick army battle where you grow a crowd; if you like a challenge, try ragdoll fighters and archery duels, where angle and power take practice.
