What are Tung Tung Sahur games?

Tung Tung Tung Sahur is one of the best-known Italian Brainrot characters: a wooden figure with a bat whose name comes from the drum call used to wake people for the pre-dawn meal during Ramadan. In games he is usually the chaser in escape and night-survival games, but you will also find slap games, runners, parkour, snake.io arenas and silly face games starring him.

What makes a good one?

A good Tung Tung Sahur game builds tension with sound - the tung-tung knocking getting closer - and gives you clear ways to escape: hiding spots, keys to collect and short levels. In action games, look for upgrades and different characters to unlock so each run feels new.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

Want a scare? Pick a night escape or survival game and play with sound on. For something lighter, try a slap, runner or parkour game. Younger players may prefer the face and clicker games. These are fan-made games by independent developers.
