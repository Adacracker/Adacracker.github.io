What are zombie games?

Zombie games share one enemy - slow (or not so slow) crowds of the undead - but play very differently. There are zombie shooters, tower and base defense games, survival games where you scavenge and craft, driving games where you plough through the apocalypse, and silly zombie puzzles and merge games.

What makes a good one?

A good zombie game builds pressure: the crowd grows, ammo runs low and you have to decide what to protect. Upgrades between waves and a clear sense of how many zombies are left keep the tension fun instead of frustrating.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

New players should start with zombie defense or merge games, where you plan between waves, or driving games where the car does the hard work. If you want real pressure, try the survival shooters with endless waves. Some games here have cartoon gore, so check the description before handing one to a young child.
