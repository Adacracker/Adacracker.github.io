Su Dark Hood

Descrizione di GameMonetize (in inglese)

Dark Hood is a cute shadow-platform puzzler. Guide a tiny hooded blob through 70 dark levels, collect the stars and coins and reach the magic portal. A cozy adventure about a little creature who loves the darkness.
