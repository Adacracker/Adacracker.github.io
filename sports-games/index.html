What are sports games?

Sports games turn real sports into quick browser games. Some are faithful simulations with teams and tournaments; most are arcade versions that boil a sport down to one skill - a penalty kick, a free throw, a putt - so a round takes seconds. You will find soccer, basketball, golf, pool, bowling, tennis, hockey, cricket, darts, archery, fishing and winter sports.

What makes a good one?

The best sports games have one control that is easy to learn and hard to master, such as swiping with the right power or releasing at the right moment. Physics that behave the way you expect and a clear meter for aim and power make every miss feel like your fault, not the game's.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

Start with single-skill games like penalty shoot-outs, basketball shots or mini golf: they explain themselves in one try. If you want more depth, pick games with a career, league or tournament mode, or head-to-head games where you can play a friend on the same keyboard.
