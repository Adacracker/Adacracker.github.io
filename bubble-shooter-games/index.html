What are bubble shooter games?

In a bubble shooter you fire coloured bubbles at a cluster at the top of the screen. Connect three or more of the same colour and they pop; any bubbles left hanging without support fall too, which is where the big scores come from. Some versions let the cluster creep down over time, others give you a set number of shots per level.

What makes a good one?

A good bubble shooter has an aiming line, predictable wall bounces and a preview of the next bubble so you can plan. Special bubbles such as bombs and rainbow bubbles add variety without making it random.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

Beginners should aim for bubbles holding up large groups rather than the nearest match - dropping a whole cluster is worth far more. Experienced players can practise bank shots off the walls. Bubble shooters are great on phones: drag to aim, release to fire.

Bubble Shooter Games: questions and answers

How do bubble shooter games work?

You shoot coloured bubbles at a cluster. Three or more of the same colour pop, and bubbles that are no longer attached fall and score bonus points.

Are bubble shooter games free?

Yes, all 12 bubble shooters here are free in your browser.

Can I play bubble shooters on my phone?

Yes - 3 of the 12 games are marked touch-friendly; you drag to aim and let go to shoot.
