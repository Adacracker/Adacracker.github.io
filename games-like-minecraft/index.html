What are games like Minecraft?

Minecraft made blocky worlds famous: everything is built from cubes you can break and place. Games in this collection borrow that look or idea. Some are sandboxes where you mine resources and craft tools, others are parkour courses through block worlds, 'noob vs pro' adventures, mining idle games or blocky shooters.

What makes a good one?

The best blocky games give you freedom - several ways to solve a problem or build something - and a clear goal for the first few minutes, so you are not lost in an empty world. Saving your world in the browser is a big plus for building games.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

If you just want to build, choose a sandbox or crafting game and start in creative or peaceful mode if offered. For quick fun, pick a block parkour or noob adventure with short levels. Most 3D blocky games use W A S D and the mouse; check the Phones line for touch support.

Top picks and why

  • Blocktrail 2048 - Collect blocks, grow your number trail and chase down weaker rivals to steal their points. (touch-friendly)
  • Mega Noob Sky Obby: 50 Stages - In this 3D platformer adventure, your mission is to guide your Noob through 50 unique stages filled with moving obstacles, winding spirals, and giant balancing spheres. (touch-friendly)
  • Block Tower Smash - Hold to crash your ball through the rings, let go to bounce, and never touch the dark blocks or your ball breaks. (touch-friendly)
  • Idle Craft 3D - Rebuild your home in Idle Craft 3D where you recruit workers and summon elders to manage fatigue. (touch-friendly)

Games like Minecraft: questions and answers

Is this the real Minecraft?

No. These are free browser games inspired by Minecraft's blocky style, made by other developers. None of them needs a launcher or a Minecraft account.

Do I need to download anything?

No. All 86 games run directly in your browser.

Can I play blocky games on a phone?

53 of the 86 games are marked touch-friendly; large 3D sandboxes are easier on a computer.
