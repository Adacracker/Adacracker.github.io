What are idle and clicker games?

Idle and clicker games are about numbers going up. You start by tapping or clicking to earn coins, spend them on helpers and machines that earn for you, and before long your empire runs by itself. Tycoon games add a business theme - a factory, a mine, a supermarket - and many let progress continue while the tab is closed.

What makes a good one?

A good idle game always has a next goal just in reach and upgrades that change the speed of the game, not just the numbers. A clear display of income per second and a reset or 'prestige' system for long-term players give it staying power.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

Everyone can play these, so choose by theme. Want something you can leave in a tab? Pick a tycoon with offline earnings. Like to be busy? Choose an active clicker or a merge idle game where tapping still matters. Progress is usually saved in your browser on this device.

Idle & Clicker Games: questions and answers

What is an idle game?

A game that keeps making progress with little or no input from you. You come back, collect what you earned and spend it on upgrades.

Do idle games keep running when I close the tab?

Some calculate offline earnings when you return, others pause. It depends on the game - the description often mentions offline income.

Are idle games free?

Yes, all 113 games here are free. Some offer optional ads for a temporary boost.
