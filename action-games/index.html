What are action games?

Action games are about doing, not planning: you move, dodge and strike in real time and the game answers right away. In this category you will find fighting games, stickman brawlers, survival games where waves of monsters or zombies close in, superhero runs and hack-and-slash adventures. Shooters have their own Shooting category.

What makes a good one?

A good action game feels responsive - your character reacts the moment you press a key or tap the screen. It also teaches you its enemies: the best ones show an attack coming, so when you lose you know why. Short levels and quick restarts matter too, because you will fail a lot before you get good.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

New to action games? Start with one-button or auto-attack games and survival games with upgrades between rounds - they get harder slowly. Experienced players should look for fighting games with combos, boss rushes or ragdoll physics brawlers, where timing and practice really pay off. Are these action games free?

Yes. All 216 action games are free to play in your browser. They are supplied by game distributors and some show a short ad before or between rounds.

Do action games work on mobile?

131 of the 216 games here are marked touch-friendly by their providers, usually with an on-screen joystick or tap controls. The rest are made for a keyboard and mouse.

What controls do action games use?

On a computer most use the arrow keys or W A S D to move and a key such as space, J or a mouse click to attack. Each game page lists the controls we found in its instructions.

Are there violent games here?

Some games have cartoon combat, monsters or zombies. We leave out explicit adult content, but if you are choosing for a young child, read the description on the game page first.
