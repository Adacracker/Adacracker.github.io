What are cooking games?

Cooking games come in two styles. Recipe games guide you step by step - mix, bake, decorate - and are relaxed and creative. Restaurant and time-management games give you a queue of customers and a timer: take orders, cook, serve and upgrade your kitchen to keep up.

What makes a good one?

Good recipe games have lots of ingredients and decorations and clear steps. Good restaurant games ramp up slowly, show each customer's patience and let you upgrade equipment so you feel yourself getting faster.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

Younger players and anyone who wants to relax should pick recipe and decorating games. If you like a challenge, try restaurant games - they are a great test of multitasking.
