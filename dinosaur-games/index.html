What are dinosaur games?

Dinosaur games come in many shapes: runners where a dino jumps over cacti, simulators where you play as a T-Rex, shooters and hunting games, merge games that evolve stronger dinos, and puzzles and colouring games for younger fans.

What makes a good one?

A good dinosaur game makes the dinosaurs feel big and powerful, with sound and weight to every step. In runners and merge games, a steady stream of new dinosaurs to unlock is the main fun.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

Little dino fans will like runners, merge games and colouring. Older players can try the 3D dinosaur simulators, which are best with a keyboard and mouse.

Top picks and why

  • Dino Squad Battle Mission immerses you in a pulse-pounding journey as a lone dinosaur fighting for survival. (touch-friendly)
  • Dinosaur Dig - Explore different locations, uncover hidden bones, carefully clean each discovery, and assemble complete dinosaur skeletons. (touch-friendly)
  • Cadillacs: Dino Riot - Choose Kira or Bo and fight to stop a dinosaur-smuggling convoy in a colourful 2D rescue adventure. (best on a computer)
  • Diggosaur is a cozy digging game with little dinosaurs. (best on a computer)
