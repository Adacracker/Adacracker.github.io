What are coloring and drawing games?

This collection mixes two kinds of creative games. Colouring games give you a picture - often by numbers or as pixel art - and let you fill it in. Drawing games either give you a free canvas or turn your lines into part of the puzzle, such as drawing a path or a shape to solve a level.

What makes a good one?

A good colouring game has a wide palette, a zoom function and a fill tool that stays inside the lines. In drawing puzzles, the line should do exactly what you drew, so solutions feel clever rather than lucky.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

For relaxing, choose colour-by-number or pixel art - there is no wrong answer. For a challenge, choose draw-a-line puzzles. Colouring games are especially nice on a tablet.
