What are 2 player games?

Two-player games let two people play on one device at the same time - no second computer or online account needed. Usually the screen is shared or split in half: one player uses the left side of the keyboard and the other the right side, or each person takes one side of a tablet. Some games also offer a single-player mode against the computer.

What makes a good one?

The best two-player games are easy to explain in one sentence, have rounds short enough to say 'best of five', and give the losing player a chance to come back. Controls that do not overlap on the keyboard are important, so nobody presses the other person's keys.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

With a younger child or someone who rarely plays, start with head soccer, air hockey or tank duels: one round shows how it works. Competitive players will enjoy fighting games, races and sports games with power-ups. On a laptop, two players fit best when one uses W A S D and the other the arrow keys.

2 Player Games: questions and answers

Can I play 2 player games on one keyboard?

Yes. Most games in this collection put player one on W A S D (or nearby letter keys) and player two on the arrow keys. Check the game's start screen for the exact keys.

Can two people play on a tablet or phone?

Some can: 54 of the 94 games are marked touch-friendly, and many of those give each player one half of the screen.

Are 2 player games online?

No, these are local games - both players sit at the same device. For online play against strangers, try our .io games.

Are they free?
