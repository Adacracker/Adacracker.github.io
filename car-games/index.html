What are car and racing games?

Car games cover everything with an engine. Racing games pit you against other drivers or the clock, drift games score you for sliding through corners, stunt games launch you off ramps, and driving simulators let you steer trucks, buses or tractors through a job. Parking games and motorbike and bicycle stunt games are here too.

What makes a good one?

Good handling is everything: the car should feel heavy enough to slide but never random. A camera that keeps the road ahead in view, a garage to upgrade or unlock vehicles, and short tracks you can retry quickly keep a racing game fun for longer.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

Beginners will enjoy parking games and side-view stunt games, which only need two or three buttons, and endless highway games where the steering is simple. If you are after speed, try 3D racing and drift games with upgrades and time trials. On a computer the arrow keys or W A S D steer; on phones most games show pedals and arrows on screen.

Cars & Racing Games: questions and answers

Are these car games free to play?

What are the controls for racing games?

Usually arrow keys or W A S D to steer, accelerate and brake, often with space for handbrake or nitro. The Quick facts box on each game page lists what we found in its instructions.

Can I play racing games on my phone?

95 of the 199 games are marked touch-friendly. Look for 'Yes, touch-friendly' in the Quick facts on a game page.
