What are brain and quiz games?

Brain games test a thinking skill directly: general knowledge in trivia and flag quizzes, speed and accuracy in math games, vocabulary in word puzzles, fingers in typing games and reasoning in logic puzzles and riddles. Several are designed for school-age children and work as light practice.

What makes a good one?

A good brain game gives instant feedback - right or wrong, and why - and gets harder in small steps. Short rounds and a score you can try to beat the next day make it a habit rather than a chore.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

Children and beginners can start with picture quizzes, counting and simple word games. Adults who want a challenge should try logic puzzles, riddles and timed math. A few minutes a day is enough to notice yourself getting faster.
