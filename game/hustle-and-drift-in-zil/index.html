About Hustle & Drift in ZIL

Buckle up: Hustle & Drift in ZIL is a free driving game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

In this game, you need to deliver cargo while keeping it firmly on the truck. But delivering safely isn't enough—you've got to do it with style! Slide your ZIL into drifts to rack up more cash for every delivery.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Hyper Cars Stunt Racing and Demolition Derpy, give Hustle & Drift in ZIL a try: they share the “car” and “drift” tags.

How to play Hustle & Drift in ZIL

Arrow keys and/or WASD – Drive / Vehicle control.

Mouse – Camera look / Look around.
