About Chaindrop

Quick fun ahead. Chaindrop is a free arcade game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the arrow keys, the space bar and the mouse.

Aim, drop, and watch one ball ricochet through a field of glowing pegs. Every peg it clips raises your multiplier, so one long bounce beats a dozen short ones. Clear each round's target before your balls run out, then pick one of three upgrades (bigger balls, bouncier shells, bombs, gold pegs, low gravity) and face a tougher board. Gold pegs boost the multiplier, bombs blast nearby pegs, and green pegs give free balls.

Why you might like it: Fans of Bongo Bounce and Marble Maze may like this one too, since it shares their “arcade” and “ball” tags.

How to play Chaindrop

Mouse: move to aim, click to drop. Touch: hold and drag to aim, release to drop. Keyboard: Left / Right arrows to aim, Space to drop, M to mute.
