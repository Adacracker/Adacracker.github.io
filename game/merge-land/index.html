About Merge Land

Merge Land loads straight into this page as a free puzzle game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with touch.

Discover countless objects to merge, from delicious plates of food to tropical treasures like pebbles and shells. Merge, build or restore run-down buildings, unlock vibrant tropical locations, meet new characters and help them transform their world with your special touch.

Why you might like it: Merge Land has a lot in common with Dessert Merge and Hot Pot Chef - they share the “food” tag.

How to play Merge Land

Move and merge identical pieces on the board to complete tasks. Earn experience points to unlock new levels with more buildings to restore. Progress through levels, earn rewards and use special items like the Joker to merge with any piece and speed up your journey.
