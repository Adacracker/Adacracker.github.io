About Match 3: Forest Crew

Match 3: Forest Crew is an exciting logic puzzle where you need to feed hungry animals after their long winter hibernation. The critters are picky, and everyone wants something special. Luckily, there is enough food for everyone! Feed each animal and wake up the next sleepyhead from their warm winter den. In the game you will be able to: Relax to pleasant music and fun effects. Meet a whole crowd of cute and fluffy animals. Train your mindfulness and concentration. Solve diverse and unique levels.
