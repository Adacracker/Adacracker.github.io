About Drone Wars

Fast hands help here. Drone Wars is a free action game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the mouse and taps.

In Drone Wars, your mission is to skillfully guide your combat drone through arenas teeming with enemy drones. Mastering the intuitive controls, outmaneuvering your adversaries, and proving yourself as the premier drone pilot are paramount to success. The primary objective is to survive relentless waves of enemy drones that continuously appear within the arena.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Card Clash : Battle Arena and Last Ring, give Drone Wars a try: they share the “arcade” and “attack” tags.

How to play Drone Wars

Welcome, pilot! In Drone Wars, your mission is to command your combat drone through intense arenas, battling waves of enemy drones. The game features intuitive click-to-move controls: simply click your mouse on PC or tap the screen on mobile to direct your drone.
