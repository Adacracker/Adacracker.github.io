About Splash Rivals

Splash Rivals loads straight into this page as a free shooting game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with W A S D, the space bar and the mouse.

Splash Rivals is an online multiplayer paint shooter where two teams of four battle to cover the arena in their colour. Spray the ground, splash your rivals and swim through your teams paint to refill your paint tank and recover health. Choose from seven weapons, customise your character and powers, and team up with friends or create private matches for up to eight players!
