About Jewel Loop Color Sort

Puzzle time. You can play Jewel Loop Color Sort, a puzzle game from our Puzzle category, free in your browser. You can play it on a computer or a touch screen. It is played with the space bar, the mouse and taps. On a phone, hold it upright: the game is made for portrait mode.

Sort colorful jewels around a looping conveyor and fill matching spaces to reveal sparkling pictures. Plan each move around limited belt capacity and a ticking clock, use helpful boosters, and explore 1,478 puzzle levels. Progress is saved automatically.
