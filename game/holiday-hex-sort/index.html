About Holiday Hex Sort

Holiday Hex Sort loads straight into this page as a free puzzle game. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the mouse, touch and dragging.

In Holiday Hex Sort, drag and drop hex tile stacks onto the board. Merge tiles to form stacks of 10 and collect them. As levels progress, more tile types appear. If there are 10 similar tiles on a stack, they will be collected. Clear all tiles by making stacks of 10 before time runs out !

Why you might like it: Holiday Hex Sort has a lot in common with Winter Hexa Stack and Winter Memory - they share the “christmas” and “holiday” tags.

How to play Holiday Hex Sort

Use your mouse or touchpad to drag and drop hex tile stacks onto the board. Merge tiles to create stacks of 10 and clear them. Complete the board before time runs out!
