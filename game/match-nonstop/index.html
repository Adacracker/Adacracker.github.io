About Match Nonstop

Puzzle time. Match Nonstop is a puzzle game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the mouse and taps.

Tap mahjong tiles on the screen to place them into the bottom slots. Three identical tiles will be removed automatically. Tiles are stacked in different positions, so observe carefully to find matching patterns. You can use helper tools such as shuffle and undo to get out of trouble, and clear all tiles to win.

Why you might like it: Match Nonstop has a lot in common with Castle Story Match3! and Color Coin Merge - they share the “match3” tag.
