About Boom Boss

Become the Boom Boss! Place dynamite on voxel buildings, press BOOM and watch them crumble in a satisfying physics-based demolition. Then vacuum up the rubble, earn coins and upgrade your crew. Blow up houses, barns, windmills, towers and harbour warehouses across sunny suburbs, farms and the docks. Chain-explode red barrels, use MEGA TNT on big jobs and beat your best clean-up time for three stars.

How to play

Tap or click the building to place dynamite, then press BOOM. Hold and drag (or move the mouse) over the rubble to vacuum it up. Drag beside the building to rotate the camera, pinch or use the mouse wheel to zoom.
