About Double Boy Jump

Adventure awaits. Double Boy Jump loads straight into this page as a free adventure game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the mouse and touch.

Keep both characters out of danger as you cross gaps collect coins and aim for a higher score. Unlock character skins and explore themed modes such as Snow Dash Face to Face Cloud Ride Monster Escape and Escort Quest. With simple one-touch controls and physics-based teamwork every jump is a fresh test of timing. Play with a mouse on desktop or touch controls on mobile.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Try Again and SkyHop — 3D Parkour Adventure, give Double Boy Jump a try: they share the “jumping” and “platform” tags.
