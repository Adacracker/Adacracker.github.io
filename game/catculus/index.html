About Catculus

Fancy a brain-teaser? Catculus is a puzzle game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the mouse and touch.

Catculus is an addictive and strategic puzzle game that combines the love of cute cats with the stimulating challenge of mental math. Dive into a vibrant retro-pixelated universe where your goal is to select adjacent tiles to reach a target number. Designed for fans of brain teasers and mathematics, Catculus offers an accessible yet deeply engaging gameplay experience that will test your calculation speed and strategic thinking.

Why you might like it: Fans of CatDoku and Cat arrows: Cozy Puzzle may like this one too, since it shares their “cat” and “challenge” tags.

How to play Catculus

The Goal of the Game.

The main objective is to select a series of adjacent tiles on the grid whose sum equals the Target Number displayed in the center of the screen.

Gameplay.

1.The Target Number: At the start of each round, a Target Number appears in the center of the grid.

2.Selecting Tiles:

Click or touch a tile to begin your selection.

Continue to select adjacent tiles (horizontally or vertically) to add their values together.

The current sum of your selection is displayed in real-time.
