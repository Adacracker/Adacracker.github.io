About Paint & Escape

Quick fun ahead. Paint & Escape loads straight into this page as a free arcade game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

Paint & Escape is a neon stealth-action game. Sneak past the guards, paint your graffiti masterpiece on the glowing spot, then escape before they catch you! Every level is a newly built house, school, museum or arcade, and the guards get smarter as you go. Watch out for security cameras, guard dogs and armored guards. Splat guards with paint bombs, taunt them with dances and collect spray cans for 3 stars. Unlock upgrades, outfits and hats, and come back for daily gifts, quests and trophies.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Keyboard Escape Block Speed +1 and Urban Jump, give Paint & Escape a try: they share the “arcade” and “action” tags.

How to play Paint & Escape

WASD or Arrow keys move SPACE or E F throw a paint bomb at a nearby guard T dance taunt ESC pause Mobile drag anywhere to move tap the on-screen bomb and dance buttons.
