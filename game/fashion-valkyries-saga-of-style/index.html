About Fashion Valkyries Saga of Style

Time to get creative. Fashion Valkyries Saga of Style is a dress-up game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the mouse and touch.

Immerse yourself in the magical world of the North to meet the northern valkyrie warriors and create relevant fashionable images for brave girls. Choose beautiful fur outfits, shining armor, and unusual accessories inspired by northern myths. Experiment with ethnic hairstyles and militant makeup. Try to assemble for each girl the perfect heroic image worthy of the harsh northern warriors!

Why you might like it: Fashion Valkyries Saga of Style has a lot in common with Spooky Halloween Makeover and Besties Halloween Fashionista - they share the “girls” and “makeup” tags.

How to play Fashion Valkyries Saga of Style

In this game you will be able to create very unusual fashionable images for two brave Valkyrie girls. At the end of the game, you can save your result as a PNG-image. Depending on the gaming device, either a click of a computer mouse or a simple touch on touchscreens is used to control it.
