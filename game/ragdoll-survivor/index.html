About Ragdoll Survivor

A stick fighter is stuck in the middle of a war zone and has to fight to stay alive. Punch, kick, block, dash and grab your way past enemy soldiers, knife fighters, riot shields and tanks, while bombs, rockets, saw blades and lava fall from the sky. Play 100 missions across 10 chapters, each ending with a boss fight. Fill the SUPER bar to go into RAGE mode. Earn coins to unlock new fighters and gear, and try Boss Rush, Endless and daily missions. Plays on computer, phone and gamepad.

How to play

Desktop A D - move W - jump S - duck F - punch G - smash C - block Shift - dash E - grab R - SUPER Mobile On-screen buttons hold the phone sideways Gamepad supported Tip block when a soldier shows the red.
