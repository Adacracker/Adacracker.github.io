About Abyss Muncher

Abyss Muncher is a free simulation game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with W A S D, the space bar and the mouse.

Dive into a colorful underwater roguelite where every meal makes you stronger. Hunt smaller creatures, dodge dangerous predators, and evolve your own build with mutations, skills, and relics. Explore changing ocean maps, unlock new creatures, and invest your rewards in permanent upgrades for the next dive.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Zaruvane The Amber Oasis and Cat Clicker, give Abyss Muncher a try: they share the “animal” and “survival” tags.

How to play Abyss Muncher

Desktop: WASD to swim, Space to use your innate skill, and J/K/L for acquired skills. Use the mouse to choose upgrades and navigate menus. Mobile: drag to swim and tap the on-screen skill buttons. Eat smaller creatures, avoid stronger predators, and choose evolutions as you grow. Follow the guided first dive to learn the basics.
