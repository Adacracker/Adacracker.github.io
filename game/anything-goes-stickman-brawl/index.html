About Anything Goes: Stickman Brawl

Ready for some action? Anything Goes: Stickman Brawl is an action game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the arrow keys, the space bar and taps. It can also be played by two people on one device.

Anything Goes: Stickman Brawl is a 3D fighting game with five stickmen and five martial arts: Rocco (street fighting), Mei (kung fu), Javier (boxing), Dandara (capoeira) and Junke (wrestling). Chain combos, land special moves and grab bats, chairs or bottles to fight in 15 stages full of hazards like lava, trains and traffic. Play Story, Arena, Tower, Challenges, 1 Player with AUTO difficulty or 2 Players on one keyboard.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Alien Rocket : Cosmic Escape and Maze Warriors, give Anything Goes: Stickman Brawl a try: they share the “2players” and “stickman” tags.

How to play Anything Goes: Stickman Brawl

Player 1 (keyboard)

A / D: move, W or Space: jump, S: crouch.

J: light punch, U: heavy punch.

K: light kick, I: heavy kick.

L: grab, pick up or throw items.

Left Shift: block (right as a hit lands: Combo Break)

H: dodge (hold H + W/A/S/D: move freely, double-tap: run)

J + K: Energy Super (full bar), U + I: Guard Break.

P: pause.

Player 2 (keyboard)

Arrow keys: move, Numpad 8: jump.

Numpad 1 / 4: light / heavy punch.

Numpad 2 / 5.
