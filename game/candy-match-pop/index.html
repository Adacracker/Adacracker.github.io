About Candy Match Pop

Fancy a brain-teaser? Candy Match Pop is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. It runs on desktop and mobile alike. Controls use the mouse, swipes and taps. On a phone, hold it upright: the game is made for portrait mode.

Candy Match Pop is a colorful single-player match-3 puzzle adventure with 80 levels across four chapters. Swap neighboring candies to match three or more, trigger satisfying cascades, and create line blasts, bombs, and rainbow candies. Complete score and collection goals, clear obstacles, collect keys, and protect special candies in move-limited and timed challenges. Use boosters to tackle tricky boards, earn stars and coins, and explore daily rewards, missions, and unlockable themes. Play with a mouse or touch controls on desktop and mobile. Supports English and Chinese, with progress saved in your browser.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Slime Buffet and Fruit Box Sort, give Candy Match Pop a try: they share the “puzzle” and “candy” tags.

How to play Candy Match Pop

Desktop Click one candy and then an adjacent candy or drag a candy horizontally or vertically to swap Mobile Tap two adjacent candies or swipe to swap Match 3 or more of the same candy and complete the level goals before moves or time run out Select.
