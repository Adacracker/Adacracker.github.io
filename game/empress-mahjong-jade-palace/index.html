About Empress Mahjong: Jade Palace

A table classic, no table needed: Empress Mahjong: Jade Palace loads straight into this page as a free board or card game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it.

Enter the Jade Palace and enjoy a relaxing Mahjong Solitaire adventure with the elegant Empress. Match identical free tiles, clear beautifully layered boards, and complete 90 handcrafted levels. Use hints when needed, rely on automatic shuffling when no matches remain, and replay levels to set faster best times.

Why you might like it: Empress Mahjong: Jade Palace has a lot in common with Mahjong Christmas Holiday and KrisMas Mahjong 2 - they share the “matching” and “tiles” tags.

How to play Empress Mahjong: Jade Palace

Select two identical free tiles to remove them. A tile is free when no tile covers it and at least one side is open. Clear every tile to complete the level. Use Hint to reveal an available pair. If no valid pair remains, the board reshuffles automatically. The timer counts upward, so replay levels to improve your best time.
