About SWAT: Squad Tactics

Lock and load: SWAT: Squad Tactics is a free shooting game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use W A S D, swipes and taps.

Lead a tactical squad through intense top-down shooter missions. Rescue hostages, clear dangerous rooms, outsmart armed enemies, and restore order in chaotic locations. Choose your gear, move fast, aim carefully, and use smart tactics to survive every operation.

Why you might like it: Fans of GORILGO FPS : Online Shooter may like this one too, since it shares their “attack” tag.

How to play SWAT: Squad Tactics

On PC, use WASD to move and LMB to aim, shoot, and interact during missions. On mobile, swipe to move your squad and tap to aim, shoot, select actions, or use objects. Clear each area, defeat enemies, rescue hostages, and complete the mission.
