About Cosmic Ace

You can play Cosmic Ace, an action game from our Action category, free in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

Cosmic Ace is a fast 3D space combat survival game. Pilot your jet through escalating invasion waves, aim with the mouse, burn nitro to boost, collect nanocores for mid-run upgrades, and unlock aircraft and weapons in the hangar. How long can you survive as invasion pressure rises?

Why you might like it: Fans of War Space and Voxhalla may like this one too, since it shares their “survival” tag.

How to play Cosmic Ace

Desktop: Move the mouse to aim. Use WASD or arrow keys to fly. Hold Shift (or the boost key) for nitro. Defeat waves, pick upgrades when prompted, and survive as long as you can.

Mobile: Use the on-screen joystick to move and the boost button for nitro. Aim by dragging. Tap Play to start.
