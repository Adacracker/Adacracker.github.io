About Archer vs Monsters

Got a minute? You can play Archer vs Monsters, an arcade game from our Arcade & Casual category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the arrow keys, the space bar and taps.

Archer vs Monsters is a casual archery arcade where timing is everything.

Monsters rotate in different ways: some spin at a steady pace, others lurch forward in chaotic bursts, some sharply reverse direction on every hit. Study their behavior and pick the perfect moment to strike.

Every arrow you land sticks into the monster and claims its spot. Hit an occupied zone and the raid is over — so the more arrows you fire, the tighter things get. That last arrow always hits different!

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Halloween Horror Massacre and Pumpkin Ninja, give Archer vs Monsters a try: they share the “arcade” and “monster” tags.

How to play Archer vs Monsters

Tap the screen — the archer shoots an arrow at the monster.

The monster rotates: each one has its own speed and movement style. Some spin steadily, others lurch forward in sudden bursts, some reverse direction on every hit.

Your goal: deal as many hits as the monster has HP. Every arrow that lands sticks in and takes up space. If a new arrow hits one that's already there — the raid is over!
