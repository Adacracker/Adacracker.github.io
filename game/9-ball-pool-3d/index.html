About 9 Ball Pool 3D

Warm up first: 9 Ball Pool 3D loads straight into this page as a free sports game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the mouse, taps and dragging.

Challenge computer opponents, compete in a three-round championship, beat the clock, or sharpen your skills in the practice room. Choose between isometric and top-down camera views, use the rising power meter for precise shots, and follow authentic 9-ball rules: strike the lowest-numbered ball first and legally pocket the 9-ball to win. Collect daily rewards, earn club coins, and master every angle.

Why you might like it: 9 Ball Pool 3D has a lot in common with Netherton Volleyball and Snowball Kickup - they share the “ball” and “8 ball pool” tags.

How to play 9 Ball Pool 3D

Move the mouse to aim your cue Left click once to lock your aim and start the power meter Left click again to shoot On mobile drag to aim then tap once to lock your aim and tap again to shoot Hit the lowest-numbered ball first.
