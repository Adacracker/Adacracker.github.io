About Car Sort

Car Sort is a puzzle game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the space bar, the mouse and taps.

Car Sort is a colorful parking puzzle game. Tap a garage to send its front cars onto the road, then match cars with garages of the same color. Plan your moves, keep an eye on road capacity, and clear increasingly challenging levels. Use helpful boosters when you need more space or a fresh arrangement. Play with a mouse on desktop or tap on mobile. Progress is saved in the current browser.

Why you might like it: Car Sort has a lot in common with Brain Jam and Bus Jam Sort Escape - they share the “logic” and “car” tags.

How to play Car Sort

Desktop Left-click garages and interface buttons Mobile Tap garages and interface buttons Sort cars into matching garages and keep the road from filling up.
