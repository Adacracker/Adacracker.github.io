About Pixel Destroyer

Quick fun ahead. Pixel Destroyer is an arcade game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the mouse, touch and dragging.

Pixel Destroyer is an exciting physics-based arcade puzzle game where your goal is to destroy colorful pixel shapes using bouncing balls.

Dive into a world of pixel destruction! In this game, you’ll need to use your wits and precision. Each level features a unique shape made of pixel blocks. Your objective is to destroy them all using a limited number of shots. The balls follow the laws of physics, bouncing off walls and obstacles, allowing you to reach even the most hard-to-access blocks.

Why you might like it: Fans of Xmas War Multiplayer and Happy Brothers may like this one too, since it shares their “arcade” and “christmas” tags.

How to play Pixel Destroyer

Destroy all pixel blocks on the screen to complete the level.

Touch the screen and drag to aim.

Release your finger or the mouse button to fire a burst of balls.

Use the walls for ricochets to reach protected areas.

You have a limited number of moves for each level.

If you run out of moves, you lose.
