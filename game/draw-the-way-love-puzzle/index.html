About Draw the Way: Love Puzzle

Draw a safe path and guide the hero through dangerous levels to reach the woman waiting for him. Plan every curve, avoid obstacles and watch your drawing come to life! Each level presents a fun new challenge where clever thinking and the right path will bring them together.

How to play

Press and drag to draw a path for the character. Guide the line around obstacles, then release to make him follow it. Reach the woman safely to complete the level. If your path fails, try again and draw a better route!
