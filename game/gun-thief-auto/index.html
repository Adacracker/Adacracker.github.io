About Gun Thief Auto

Simulation fans, take note: Gun Thief Auto loads straight into this page as a free simulation game. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

Game Overview Gun Theif Auto is an open-world voxel action game featuring a 6x6 urban grid. Explore freely, complete mission contracts, and evade police in sandbox or mission modes. Key Features Modes: Sandbox free roam and structured missions with a dynamic police wanted system. World: A massive 6x6 city grid with accessible rooftops and diverse districts. Vehicles & Arsenal: Over 15 vehicle classes (sports cars, police cruisers) and weapons including handguns and RPGs. Characters: Over 100 dynamic NPC types, including civilians, police, and rival gangs.
