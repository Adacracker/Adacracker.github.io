About Sea Survivor

You can play Sea Survivor, an action game from our Action category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the arrow keys, the mouse and dragging.

Survive endless waves of enemy ships in an epic three-D top-down sea battle. Fire powerful cannons, collect loot, upgrade your ship, and use smart strategy to become the ultimate sea warrior.

Why you might like it: It sits in our Action games alongside Monster Duelist and Playdough School, so if you like those, try this one.

How to play Sea Survivor

Controls.

PC: Use the mouse to drag and steer your ship, or use W, A, and D / Arrow Keys to move.

Mobile: Use the on-screen joystick to control your ship.
