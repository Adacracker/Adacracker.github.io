About Tactical Penguin

Strategy fans, take note: Tactical Penguin loads straight into this page as a free strategy game. It works on phones, tablets and computers.

Defend your penguin colony's fish supply from marauding pelicans in Tactical Penguin. Channel strategic precision and timing to launch missile-equipped penguins, navigating blizzards and shifting winds. Experience relentless, escalating challenges as you safeguard food and survival. Dive into this engaging twist on classic missile defense gameplay!

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Hot Barrel Cats and Going Right, give Tactical Penguin a try: they share the “animal” tag.
