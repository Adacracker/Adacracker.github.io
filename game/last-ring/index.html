About Last Ring

The ring is closing and the monsters keep coming. Survive five minutes inside a shrinking arena where you only move and your weapons fire on their own. Dodge through the horde, grab the gems monsters drop, and pick a new skill every time you level up. Pair a weapon with its matching passive to evolve it, defeat two bosses, and unlock harder rings.

How to play

Desktop: move with WASD or the arrow keys, or hold the mouse button and drag. Press P or Esc to pause. Mobile: drag anywhere on the screen to move. Attacks are automatic. Stay inside the ring, collect gems to level up, and survive for five minutes.
