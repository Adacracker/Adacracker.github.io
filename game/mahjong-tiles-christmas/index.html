About Mahjong Tiles Christmas

A table classic, no table needed: you can play Mahjong Tiles Christmas, a board or card game from our Board & Cards category, free in your browser. You can play it on a computer or a touch screen. Controls use the mouse, taps and touch. On a phone, turn it sideways for the wide landscape screen.

Mahjong Tiles Christmas is winter theme game. Collect the pairs of identical tiles by clicking or tapping them. You can collect only those tiles which are open from either left or right side. Collect all the tiles to complete a level. Complete all the levels to win the game.
