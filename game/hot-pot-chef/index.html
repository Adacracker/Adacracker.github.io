About Hot Pot Chef

Hot Pot Chef is a puzzle game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with taps.

Hotpot Shef is a relaxing food sorting puzzle and match-3 game where you sort tasty ingredients, match 3 identical items, complete food orders, and clear the hotpot. Choose ingredients carefully, organize the tray, solve each sorting puzzle, and complete every order in this fun casual cooking game.

Why you might like it: Hot Pot Chef has a lot in common with Snack Stack and Dessert Merge - they share the “matching” and “food” tags.

How to play Hot Pot Chef

Tap ingredients in the hotpot and move them to the tray. Match 3 identical ingredients to collect them and complete food orders. Ingredients from different layers are available, so choose the right items, manage the tray, avoid unnecessary ingredients, and plan each move carefully. Complete all orders, clear the hotpot, and finish the level!
