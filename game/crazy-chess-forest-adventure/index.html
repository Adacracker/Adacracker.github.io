About Crazy Chess: Forest Adventure

Crazy Chess: Forest Adventure is a fast-paced fantasy chess battle where classic chess meets HP-based combat, unique abilities, wild movement, power-ups, and dramatic K.O. battles. Play in 2 Player mode with a friend or challenge the computer across multiple difficulty levels. Move units, attack enemies, manage HP, trigger powerful events, and defeat the enemy boss. Explore a colorful forest battlefield, master unique units, and experience chess like never before!

How to play

Choose a game mode: 2 PLAYER to play locally with a friend, or VS COMPUTER to challenge the AI with different difficulty levels.

Select a piece and tap a highlighted valid move. Each piece has unique movement and HP-based combat. Attack enemies, use abilities and power-ups, survive battle events, and defeat the enemy boss to win.

Use Settings to change theme, day/night mode, music, and sound. Desktop: Mouse controls. Mobile/Tablet: Tap pieces and move locations.
