About Royal Jelly Jam

Puzzle time. Royal Jelly Jam is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. You play it with dragging.

Royal Jelly Jam is a cozy Color Block Jam–style puzzle: slide each colored jelly block into the gate of its own color to melt it away, and clear the whole board before the timer runs out.

Why you might like it: Royal Jelly Jam has a lot in common with Royal Smash Puzzle Game - they share the “block” and “puzzleblock” tags.

How to play Royal Jelly Jam

Drag a colored block toward the gate of the same color it slides across the board and melts through the matching gate Clear every block before time runs out to finish the level.
