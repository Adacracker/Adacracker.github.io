About Tropical Tiles Mahjong

Escape to a tropical Mahjong paradise filled with fruits, flowers, sea creatures, and sunny beach tiles! Match free pairs, clear relaxing layouts, and progress through an endless island adventure. Collect Golden Tiles during the Mermaid Spell event, build winning streaks for bigger multipliers, and climb the daily leaderboard. Unlock a reward chest every 10 layouts and use helpful boosters like Hint, Shuffle, Undo, Torch, and Freeze. A relaxing puzzle escape for Mahjong fans!

How to play

Match two identical tropical-themed tiles to remove them from the board. A tile is free when no tile is on top and at least one side is open. Clear all tiles before time runs out to complete the layout. Use Hint, Shuffle, Undo, Torch, or Freeze when needed. Collect Golden Tiles during the Mermaid Spell event, build your winning streak, and climb the leaderboard. Complete 10 layouts to unlock a chest filled with booster rewards.
