About Ninja: Sword Dash

You are a swift stickman ninja armed with a sword. Choose a direction, launch into a lightning-fast dash, and eliminate opponents before they can stop you.

Each arena is a short test of planning and reflexes. Plot your path, avoid danger, and find the right sequence of strikes to leave no enemy standing.

How to play

On desktop, hold the left mouse button, point in the direction you want to move, and release to launch the character into a sword dash.

On a phone or tablet, swipe across the screen in the desired direction. Aim the hero directly at enemies, avoid danger, and choose the right sequence of attacks.
