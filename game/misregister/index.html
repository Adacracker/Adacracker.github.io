About Misregister

Got a minute? Misregister is a free arcade game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use touch and dragging.

MISREGISTER: a four-page seam proof pressed on one cuboid. Ink lands on the page facing you, but after every shot the press turns to a page you did not pick and the ink keeps pressing down on all four. Three or more of one ink pop, even across pages, and anything left hanging falls. Drift adds a row to all four pages on a timer; one bubble over the trim rail spoils the edition. A pop scores 10, a fall 15, and 25 for each extra page one pop crossed. Drag to aim on desktop and phones.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Paint & Escape and Keyboard Escape Block Speed +1, give Misregister a try: they share the “arcade” tag.

How to play Misregister

Pointer keyboard and touch Drag to aim release to fire a shot that reaches a corner carries onto the next page A D turn the block and the two on-screen TURN pads do the same on touch HUD keys pause mute and END pull the sheet bank the score.
