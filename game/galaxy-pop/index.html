About Galaxy Pop

Puzzle time. Galaxy Pop is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the space bar, the mouse and taps.

Galaxy Pop is a cosmic bubble shooter in outer space. Aim your blaster and shoot colorful bubbles, match three or more of the same color to pop them, and watch the bubbles below fall. Shots are limited, so aim carefully and bounce off the walls to reach tricky spots. Play 1000 levels, some with special goals: rescue the aliens, collect the UFOs or collect the eggs. Stuck? Use boosters: Color Pop clears one color, Hook drops a bubble and everything below it, Blast destroys the bubbles around your tap. Complete daily quests, spin the lucky wheel and open the hourly reward chest.

Why you might like it: Fans of Spellbound Orbs and Candy Match Pop may like this one too, since it shares their “puzzle” and “match 3” tags.

How to play Galaxy Pop

Touch and drag or hold the mouse button to aim release to shoot.
