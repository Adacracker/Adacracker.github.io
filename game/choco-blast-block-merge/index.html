How to play Choco Blast : Block Merge

How to Play:

Drag and drop chocolate blocks of various shapes onto the 8x8 grid.

Fill complete lines horizontally or vertically to clear them and score points.

Create combos by clearing multiple lines in a single placement or clearing lines consecutively for huge point multipliers!

Game Over triggers when there is no room left on the grid for the remaining chocolate blocks—but don't worry, you can always watch a quick ad to Revive and keep your high-score streak alive!
