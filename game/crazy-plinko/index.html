About Crazy Plinko

Ready to run the show? Crazy Plinko is a free simulation game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it.

At first it feels random. Then patterns appear. With each upgrade you influence gravity, angles and timing. The board stops being chaos and becomes a system you can master. But are your choices smart enough to succeed?

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Babel Tower and WW1 Battle Simulator, give Crazy Plinko a try: they share the “strategy” tag.
