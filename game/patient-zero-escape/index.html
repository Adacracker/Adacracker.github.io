About Patient Zero: Escape

You can play Patient Zero: Escape, a puzzle game from our Puzzle category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the mouse, taps and dragging.

Patient Zero: Escape is a relaxing block-placement puzzle game set in a stylized 3D hospital. You are patient Zero — every patient order in your form is a puzzle board you must clear. Pick up the shapes, fit them into the empty slots, and fill every cell to cure the ward and move on.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Prism Blast 3D and Halloween Tile Tumble, give Patient Zero: Escape a try: they share the “block” and “sliding-puzzle” tags.

How to play Patient Zero: Escape

How to play:

Drag (or tap) a piece to move it; tap/click to rotate where offered.

Fill the board completely to complete the level.

Use P or Escape to pause, and the pause menu to return to the menu or adjust sound.
