About Goose Cup

Welcome to Goose Cup — the ultimate battle of football fans!

Choose your favorite country and start juggling. Every touch earns support points for your team’s shared score.

Join thousands of players, climb the global leaderboard, beat rival countries, and prove your fans are the strongest in the world!

Stop just watching football. Enter Goose Cup, support your team, and lead it to victory!

How to play

The goal is to keep the ball in the air as long as possible without letting it fall.

Controls:

Left-click, tap the screen, or press the Spacebar on your keyboard to kick the ball.

The kick registers only when the ball is within the player's active zone: head, chest, knees, or feet.

Gameplay:

Time your kicks: a perfect hit doubles your points!

Stay alert: the longer you juggle, the faster the ball flies.

Every successful kick adds to your country's score on the global leaderboard.
