About Scopa

A table classic, no table needed: you can play Scopa, a board or card game from our Board & Cards category, free in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It can also be played by two people on one device.

Play Scopa, one of Italy’s most beloved card games, and test your strategy, memory, and quick thinking. Challenge your opponent, capture cards, and score points by collecting coins, the settebello, primiera, and decisive Scopas. Every move matters, so choose your cards carefully. With simple rules and smooth gameplay, Scopa is perfect for both experienced players and newcomers. Enjoy this Italian classic anytime, directly on your device!

Why you might like it: Fans of Ludo Quick: Dice Board Games and Domino Peak may like this one too, since it shares their “board” and “multiplayer” tags.

How to play Scopa

Scopa is played in turns. Each player plays one card from their hand. You can capture one or more cards from the table if their total value matches the card played. If you cannot capture, your card stays on the table. Capturing all table cards in one move makes a Scopa and earns an extra point. At the end, points are awarded for most cards, most coins, the settebello, primiera, and Scopas. Play wisely and outscore your opponent!
