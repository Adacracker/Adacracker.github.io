About Sky Ace: Stunt Plane Islands

Start your engine. Sky Ace: Stunt Plane Islands is a free driving game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the space bar, the mouse and dragging.

Take the controls of a bright stunt plane and fly free over palm islands, a red canyon, snowy peaks and a city at sunset. Thread glowing rings, pop balloons, chain loops and barrel rolls into huge combos, race rival pilots and land on a runway or the deck of a carrier. Earn stars and coins across 28 missions to unlock five planes with their own handling and eight paint jobs.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Combat Landing, give Sky Ace: Stunt Plane Islands a try: they share the “airplane” and “flight” tags.
