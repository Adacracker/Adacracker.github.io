About Park Fever

Park Fever loads straight into this page as a free driving game. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the arrow keys and taps.

Can you clear the traffic jam? Move vehicles in the right order, free the buses, and pick up waiting passengers. Match colors, plan every move, and enjoy a fun puzzle that combines parking strategy with passenger management!

Why you might like it: Fans of Bus Mania: Parking Puzzle and Arrow Parking may like this one too, since it shares their “car” and “parking” tags.

How to play Park Fever

Tap a vehicle to drive it out of the traffic jam. Vehicles can only leave if the path in the direction of their arrow is completely clear. Move them to the boarding area and pick up passengers in the queue. Match each vehicle with the correct passengers color and clear the entire parking lot to complete the level.
