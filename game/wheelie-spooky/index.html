About Wheelie Spooky

Wheelie Spooky is a driving game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the space bar and touch.

Ride through haunted roads in Wheelie Spooky! Balance your bike, perform tricks, collect coins, and unlock spooky costumes and bikes.
