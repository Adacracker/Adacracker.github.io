About Bee Colony

Ready for some action? Bee Colony is a free action game that runs right in your browser. You can play it on a computer or a touch screen. Controls use the space bar, the mouse and dragging. On a phone, turn it sideways for the wide landscape screen.

Bag Defense is an action-packed adventure where strategy and skill are the key to survival! Carefully choose and manage your weapons, armor, and supplies to fend off relentless waves of monsters and defend your territory. With limited backpack space, every decision counts as you craft the ultimate gear setup to face increasingly powerful foes. Progress through thrilling chapters, conquer evolving challenges, and prove yourself as the ultimate defender against the monster invasion!
