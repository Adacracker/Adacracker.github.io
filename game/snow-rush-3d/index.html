About Snow Rush 3D

Got a minute? You can play Snow Rush 3D, an arcade game from our Arcade & Casual category, free in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the arrow keys, the space bar and taps. It can also be played by two people on one device.

Snow Rush 3D is a fun sledding game that tests speed and reflexes for winter sports enthusiasts, playable in both 1 and 2 players! Try to reach the longest distance by overcoming obstacles and ramps on a challenging snow-covered track, or challenge your friend! Snowmen, obstacles and tricky turns await you along the way. How far can you go and how many gifts can you collect?
