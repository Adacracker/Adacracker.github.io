About Pixel Jam Bus Puzzle: Color Sort

Pixel Jam Bus Puzzle: Color Sort is a puzzle game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the space bar, the mouse and taps.

Pixel Jam Bus Puzzle is a colorful sorting puzzle where tiny passengers form pixel pictures. Send vehicles into limited parking spaces, match their colors to waiting passengers, and clear the picture. Plan your moves around vehicle capacity and available space. Explore 1,000 levels, use Clear or Shuffle to tackle tricky arrangements, and upgrade pickup speed. Play with mouse or touch controls, with progress saved in your browser.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Sandly: Color Flow and Words and Bubbles, give Pixel Jam Bus Puzzle: Color Sort a try: they share the “sorting” tag.

How to play Pixel Jam Bus Puzzle: Color Sort

Desktop: Left-click vehicles and on-screen buttons. Mobile and tablet: Tap vehicles and buttons. Choose an available vehicle, collect passengers of the same color, and let full vehicles leave to free a parking space. Clear the pixel picture without filling every parking space.
