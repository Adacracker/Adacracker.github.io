About Trap Step

Adventure awaits. Trap Step loads straight into this page as a free adventure game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the arrow keys, the space bar and taps.

A troll platformer where nothing is what it seems. 40 levels across 8 doors: floors that vanish, spikes that lie, invisible bridges, gravity that flips, bounce pads, rising lava and walls that chase you. Every death leaves a mark, so you learn each trap and then outsmart it. Stuck? Skip a level after 5 tries.

Why you might like it: Trap Step has a lot in common with VEX 3 Xmas and Definitely Not a Platformer - they share the “platformer” and “jumping” tags.

How to play Trap Step

Arrow keys or A/D to move, W/Up/Space to jump, R to retry. On mobile, slide your thumb on the left pad to move and tap the right button to jump.
