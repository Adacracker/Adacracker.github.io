About Grand Pet Connect

Puzzle time. You can play Grand Pet Connect, a puzzle game from our Puzzle category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the space bar and taps.

Grand Pet Connect is a cute and relaxing tile-matching puzzle filled with adorable animals! Pick pet tiles, collect sets of three identical ones, and watch them disappear. Clear the layered board, plan your moves carefully, and enjoy increasingly challenging levels with your furry and feathered friends!

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Goose Rescue: Screw Sort and Eggxel-Pop, give Grand Pet Connect a try: they share the “animal” and “relaxing” tags.

How to play Grand Pet Connect

Tap an available pet tile to move it into the tray. Match three identical tiles in the tray to remove them and free up space. Clearing tiles reveals new ones underneath, giving you more matching options. Keep making sets of three until the entire board is cleared. Be careful — if the tray fills up before you can make a match, you lose the level. Use helpful boosters to undo moves, shuffle the tiles, or get some help when you're stuck.
