About Grim Spell

Survive an endless monster horde as a lone wizard in a cursed land! Your spells automatically target nearby enemies—just move, dodge, and stay alive. Collect XP gems, level up, and choose 1 of 3 random upgrades. Unlock 6 powerful spells and boost damage, speed, health, crit chance, pickup range, and more. Fight goblins, skeletons, orcs, ghosts, and deadly bosses. Earn gold to unlock permanent upgrades and test your skills in Boss Attack mode!

How to play

WASD / Arrow keys (ZQSD on AZERTY) = Move.

Spells = Cast automatically at the nearest enemy.

1 / 2 / 3 or A / D + Enter = Choose an upgrade on level up.

P = Pause.

Enter / Space = Confirm in menus.

Mouse = Click buttons.

Mobile: drag anywhere on the screen to move, tap the gear (top right) to pause.
