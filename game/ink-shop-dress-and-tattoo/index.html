About Ink Shop: Dress & Tattoo

Simulation fans, take note: Ink Shop: Dress & Tattoo loads straight into this page as a free simulation game. It runs on desktop and mobile alike. On a phone, hold it upright: the game is made for portrait mode.

Welcome to Ink Shop: Dress & Tattoo, a unique and immersive game that lets you unleash your inner tattoo artist. This isn't just any game; it's your gateway to a world where art meets relaxation, and where your creativity can truly shine.
