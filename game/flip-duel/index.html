About Flip Duel

Pack your bags: Flip Duel is a free adventure game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the arrow keys, W A S D and taps. It can also be played by two people on one device.

Ready to find out how stylishly you can land a backflip, or how spectacularly you can fail one? Flip Duel is a fast-paced parkour flipping game built around timing, skill combos, challenging landing zones, and hilarious ragdoll crashes.

Play through the full parkour adventure in 1 Player mode, challenge a friend in a local 2 Player flip duel, or enter FreeStyle and keep moving through obstacles for as long as your life bar allows.

Why you might like it: Fans of +1 Speed Keyboard Lucky Escape and Steal Animal Eggs Online may like this one too, since it shares their “2players” tag.

How to play Flip Duel

Player 1: WASD – Character control and skill selection.

Player 2: ARROW KEYS – Character control and skill selection.

Touch Controls: Tap the screen to control your character and perform flips.
