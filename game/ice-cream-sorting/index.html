About Ice Cream Sorting

Puzzle time. Ice Cream Sorting loads straight into this page as a free puzzle game. It runs on desktop and mobile alike. You play it with dragging. On a phone, hold it upright: the game is made for portrait mode.

Sort delicious ice creams by moving them between shelves and match 3 identical treats to clear them. Reveal hidden desserts, plan your moves carefully, and solve hundreds of relaxing yet challenging sorting puzzles. Unlock exciting achievements as you progress, use the Shuffle booster to overcome tricky situations, and pause the timer by watching a rewarded ad whenever you need extra thinking time. Enjoy colorful graphics, satisfying gameplay, and switch your preferred language anytime from the Settings menu.
