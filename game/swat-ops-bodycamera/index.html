About SWAT Ops: Bodycamera

SWAT OPS: Body Camera is a first-person tactical shooter seen through an operator's body cam: lens distortion, camera shake and a gritty retro look. Drop into three-minute Deathmatch or 4v4 Team Deathmatch against bots on four maps, from a derelict foundry to a frontier village. Earn coins in every match to open cases, unlock 100+ weapon skins and charms, and climb the season battle pass. Plays in the browser on desktop and mobile.

How to play

Goal: get the most kills before the three-minute timer ends. First to 15 kills wins Deathmatch; first team to 30 wins Team Deathmatch.

Desktop: WASD move, mouse look, LMB shoot, RMB aim, R reload, Q/wheel switch weapon, Shift sprint, Ctrl crouch, Space jump, Tab/Esc PDA and scoreboard.

Mobile: left stick move, drag right side to look; buttons for fire, aim, reload, jump, crouch, weapon switch, PDA.
