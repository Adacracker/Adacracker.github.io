About Frosty Ball

Adventure awaits. Frosty Ball loads straight into this page as a free adventure game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the arrow keys, the space bar and taps.

Frosty Ball is a fast-paced physics platformer set across icy peaks and cosmic frost! Roll your way through challenging obstacles, collect golden coins, leap over sharp spikes, and unlock 10 adorable snowball characters. Each world brings exciting new mechanics like anti-gravity pads, portals, swinging frost maces, and deadly laser beams. Can you earn 3 stars on every stage?

Why you might like it: Frosty Ball has a lot in common with Santa Christmas Delivery and Santa Adventure - they share the “winter” and “ball” tags.

Desktop Controls Move Left Right A D or Left Arrow Right Arrow Jump Spacebar W or Up Arrow Pause P or Esc Mobile Touch Controls Tap the on-screen Left Right buttons to move Tap the Spacebar button to jump Tap the Pause icon in the top-rig.
