About Cakefoot

Got a minute? Cakefoot loads straight into this page as a free arcade game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the mouse and touch.

Cakefoot is a precision avoider game where you use a single button to walk your cake as far as you can along a line, while obstacles like spikes, bombs, fire, and fish try to eat you. Easy to learn - press the button to walk forward, and let go to walk backward - brutally hard to defeat. 22 levels between you and the end of the line. You're live against the world with real time in-game leaderboards. Do you have what it cakes to put your name on top?

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Needle in a Haystack, give Cakefoot a try: they share the “speedrun” tag.

How to play Cakefoot

Hold left-click, any key, gamepad button, or touch to walk forward, and let go to walk backward.

Beat levels and collect coins to unlock emojis. Beat the game and collect all coins to unlock more, including visual effects, and a faster but heavier cake on the third difficulty.

Click your name on the title screen to edit your name and emoji.

Turn off shaders from the pause menu if the framerate is dropping.
