About Berryflow

Berryflow is a puzzle game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the mouse, taps and touch.

Drag through berries of one color and the juice pours into a glass. There are fewer glasses than colors, so mixing is unavoidable: pour top layers from glass to glass, drop a fizz tablet to foam away a layer, add syrup to blend colors, and bring every mocktail to one pure color. 70 levels across five worlds: chameleon berries, fuses, color bombs, ice and an order belt. No timers, one-hand play on phone and desktop. Every drink is a non-alcoholic berry mocktail.

Why you might like it: Fans of Goods Master and Color Coin Merge may like this one too, since it shares their “logic” and “sorting” tags.

How to play Berryflow

Mouse or touch. Drag through 3+ adjacent berries of one color to pour their juice into the selected glass; longer chains give more juice. Tap a glass to choose where the juice goes; above it you see the target color and how much still fits. Drag from glass to glass to pour the top layer (only onto the same color or an empty glass). Fizz removes the top layer; syrup blends the two top layers. A glass is done when full with one color. Finish every target glass within the move limit.
