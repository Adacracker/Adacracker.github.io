About Night Lake

Night Lake loads straight into this page as a free simulation game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the space bar, the mouse and touch.

A calm night on a quiet lake: row your little boat by lantern light, cast your float and wait for the bite. Night Lake is a relaxing fishing game with a touch of mystery. Catch perch, pike, carp and giant catfish, sell your catch at the dock and upgrade your rods, reels, lines and baits. Explore a growing collection of lakes, from a moonlit forest lake to a jungle bayou, frozen shores and a ruined mill pond, and discover the strange, legendary creatures said to live in the deep.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Cellar and Ink Shop: Dress & Tattoo, give Night Lake a try: they share the “relaxing” and “upgrade” tags.

How to play Night Lake

Desktop: W/S to row, A/D to turn. Hold Left Click on the water to aim and release to cast. Click or press Space when the float goes under, then hold to reel in; keep the tension out of the red and use A/D to lean the rod. E opens the dock shop at the dock, P pauses. Mobile: left stick to row, right stick to aim, cast, strike and reel.
