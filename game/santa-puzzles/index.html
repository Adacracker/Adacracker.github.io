About Santa Puzzles

Santa Puzzles is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. It runs on desktop and mobile alike. You play it with the mouse, touch and dragging. On a phone, turn it sideways for the wide landscape screen.

In this game called Santa Puzzles you have 12 puzzles with pictures from Santa Claus characters. Assemble them by changing to place the pieces. To change drag parts up to another position. Completed a level, go to the next. In each level you have limited time for which need to complete it.

Why you might like it: Santa Puzzles has a lot in common with Jewel Christmas Mania and Winter Memory - they share the “christmas” and “winter” tags.
