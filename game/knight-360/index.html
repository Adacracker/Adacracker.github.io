About Knight 360

Ready for some action? Knight 360 loads straight into this page as a free action game. It works on phones, tablets and computers. On a phone, hold it upright: the game is made for portrait mode.

In Knight 360, become a valiant knight battling eerie monsters with strategic spin attacks. Navigate intricate 3D environments and fend off skeletons and ghosts with skillful combat. Each level challenges your wits and bravery as enemy tactics change, creating an escalating test of survival and cunning.
