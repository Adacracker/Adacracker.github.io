About Cooking Match

Cooking Match loads straight into this page as a free puzzle game. You can play it on a computer or a touch screen. On a phone, hold it upright: the game is made for portrait mode.

Dive into a vibrant culinary adventure with Cooking Match, where strategic moves and rapid thinking turn ingredients into delectable dishes. Conquer match-3 puzzles, create larger clusters for bonus points, and race against time in a colorful world full of unique cooking challenges. Perfectly designed for endless family fun!

Why you might like it: Cooking Match has a lot in common with Bubble Rush and Galaxy Pop - they share the “bubble shooter” tag.

Cooking Match: FAQ

Is Cooking Match free?

Yes. Cooking Match is free to play in your browser on Arcade Cabin. It is supplied by GamePix, and the provider may show ads before or during the game.

Can I play Cooking Match on a tablet?

Yes. GamePix lists Cooking Match as mobile-friendly, so it works with touch on phones and tablets as well as on computers. It is made for portrait mode, so hold your phone upright.

Do I need to download Cooking Match?

No. Cooking Match runs in your browser right on this page - press Play to start. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

Who provides Cooking Match?

Cooking Match is distributed by GamePix, one of the three game providers whose catalogs Arcade Cabin uses. The game belongs to its developer, who is not named in GamePix's catalog feed.
