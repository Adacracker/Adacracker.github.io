About Pixel Tetrix Trails

Fancy a brain-teaser? Pixel Tetrix Trails is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the mouse, touch and dragging.

Join a cheerful artist and rebuild colorful pixel-art pictures from glossy Tetrix-style pieces. Drag every block into its correct place, reveal the completed artwork, and finish before time runs out. Explore 90 handcrafted levels with satisfying placement feedback, cozy studio visuals, and increasingly challenging shapes.

Why you might like it: Pixel Tetrix Trails has a lot in common with Cooking Block Fever and TideRoll: Block Puzzle - they share the “block” and “tetris” tags.

How to play Pixel Tetrix Trails

Drag a piece using the mouse or touchscreen and move it over the picture. Valid positions show a positive highlight, while invalid positions show a warning highlight. Release the piece when it fits correctly. Complete every cell before the timer reaches zero. Use Hint to reveal the correct location of one unplaced piece.
