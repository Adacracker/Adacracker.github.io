About Elemental Challenge

Fancy a brain-teaser? Elemental Challenge loads straight into this page as a free puzzle game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the arrow keys.

Elemental Challenge is an addictive logic puzzle game where you combine elemental pieces to create complete elements. Plan every move carefully, discover unique elemental mechanics, and find the perfect way to merge each piece. One wrong move can leave you stuck as puzzles become increasingly complex. Test your logic, strategy, and problem-solving skills, master powerful combinations, and conquer every elemental challenge!

Why you might like it: Elemental Challenge has a lot in common with Find The Key and Snake Way - they share the “brain” and “logic” tags.
