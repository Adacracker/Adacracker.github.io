About Taxi Rush

Buckle up: you can play Taxi Rush, a driving game from our Cars & Racing category, free in your browser. It works on phones, tablets and computers. Controls use the mouse. On a phone, turn it sideways for the wide landscape screen.

Navigate dense traffic, avoid roadblocks, and manage unique passenger demands to earn rewards.
