About Island Mayhem: Tiki Blast 3D

Fancy a brain-teaser? Island Mayhem: Tiki Blast 3D is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the mouse, taps and touch.

Island Mayhem is a colorful 3D cannon shooting and physics puzzle game. Join Bonko, a brave little pirate monkey, and blast your way across tropical islands! Aim the cannon, set the power and fire to knock down tiki towers, wooden forts and stone walls. Every block, barrel and tiki moves with real physics, so one smart shot can bring the whole tower crashing down. Play 200 levels across 10 islands, from Tropical Isles and Volcanic Reef to Frosty Cove and Thunder Peaks, each with new twists like slippery ice, moving platforms, wind and low gravity. Defeat a giant tiki boss at the end of every island. Use 10 special cannonballs such as Blas.

Why you might like it: Island Mayhem: Tiki Blast 3D has a lot in common with Watermelon Stack 3D and Rope Chef - they share the “casual” and “physics” tags.
