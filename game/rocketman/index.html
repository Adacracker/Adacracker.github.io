About Rocketman

Ready for some action? Rocketman loads straight into this page as a free action game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the arrow keys, the space bar and the mouse.

Step right up! Load a fearless stunt kid into the circus cannon and fire them across the sky. Bounce on trampolines, pop balloons, fly through hoops and smash crate walls as you go. Grab coins to upgrade your gunpowder, aero suit, helmet, scope and rocket. Then fly farther through five areas: the Big Top, the Fairground, the City, the Sky and Space. Complete missions, unlock new looks and beat your record on every launch!

Why you might like it: Rocketman has a lot in common with Planet Blast: Rockets - they share the “rocket” tag.

How to play Rocketman

Aim: Drag to aim the cannon. On a keyboard, use the Arrow keys or W/S.

Launch: Press and hold to fill the power meter, then release to fire. Let go at the right moment for a PERFECT shot!

Rocket boost: While flying, hold the mouse button, your finger on the screen or Space to fire your rocket. Watch your fuel.

Upgrade: Spend your coins between launches to fly even farther.

Controls: Mouse / Touch, or Space / Enter + Arrow keys (W/S)
