About Dreams

Puzzle time. You can play Dreams, a puzzle game from our Puzzle category, free in your browser. It runs on desktop and mobile alike. Controls use taps. On a phone, hold it upright: the game is made for portrait mode.

In the realm of slumber, dreams manifest as delicate tiles, each holding a fragment of imagination, memory, or longing. As a Dream Collector, your mission is to weave these ephemeral pieces together, creating a tapestry of wonder. Only by assembling the perfect dream can you unlock hidden secrets and awaken the Dream Oracle.

Why you might like it: It sits in our Puzzle games alongside Winter Memory and Eggxel-Pop, so if you like those, try this one.

How to play Dreams

Rules:

You can jump to an adjacent tile if it’s within range.

Walls block your path—no jumping over them!

Simple tiles beckon from two tiles away.

Sticky tiles demand proximity—just one tile.

Canon tiles spin with each leap, challenging your strategy.

Win Condition:

Collect all dreams by stepping on the dream-containing tiles.

Reach the special “End Tile” (distinguished by a different color) to complete the level.
