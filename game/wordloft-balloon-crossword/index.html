About Wordloft: Balloon Crossword

Puzzle time. Wordloft: Balloon Crossword loads straight into this page as a free puzzle game. It is best played on a computer. Controls use taps.

Wordloft is a crossword where the letters float in on balloons. A bunch of balloons hangs below a mini crossword: pop the balloon with the right letter and it flies into the cell. Solve 100 crosswords in 10 themes — Home, Animals, Journey, Nature, City, Jobs, Sports, Music, Kitchen, Capitals — and travel across the map in a hot-air balloon. Earn stars, spend them on the Wind that clears the wrong balloons, and enjoy a calm word game with no timer.

Why you might like it: Fans of Connect the Dots - Color Lines and Sandly: Color Flow may like this one too, since it shares their “relaxing” and “family” tags.

How to play Wordloft: Balloon Crossword

Read the clue at the top. Tap the balloon with the letter that fits the highlighted cell — the letter flies into the crossword. Tap any cell to fill it out of order; tap a wrong (red) letter to remove it. Fewer mistakes mean more stars. Use the light bulb for a hint or the Wind to blow away the wrong balloons.
