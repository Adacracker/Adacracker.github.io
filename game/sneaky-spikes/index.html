About Sneaky Spikes

Pack your bags: Sneaky Spikes is a free adventure game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the arrow keys and the space bar.

Sneaky Spikes is a troll platformer drawn on notebook paper. Floors drop, spikes pop and doors bite, and a sarcastic narrator comments on every death. Finish a page and every one of your deaths replays at once in the Death Parade. 60 levels, stars to collect and a speedrun timer. How to play: Run and jump to the door at the end of each page. Expect the unexpected: watch for traps, learn from each death and try again.

Why you might like it: Fans of Lantern Wayfarner and Try Again may like this one too, since it shares their “platform” and “jump” tags.

How to play Sneaky Spikes

Desktop arrow keys or A D to move Space W or Up to jump P to pause Mobile on-screen arrow and jump buttons.
