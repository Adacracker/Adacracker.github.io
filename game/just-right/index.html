About Just Right

Just Right loads straight into this page as a free puzzle game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the mouse and taps.

Welcome to Just Right!

Every items has a perfect place... can you find it?

In Just Right, you'll sort, stack, match, and arrange objects until everything feels just right! Each puzzle is a fun little challenge that helps you spot patterns, think creatively, and enjoy the satisfying feeling of putting things in the perfect order.

Take your time, explore, and discover the joy of making every scene neat, cozy, and happy!

Why you might like it: Just Right has a lot in common with Arrows Flow and Detective Ava: Logic Files - they share the “logic” and “thinking” tags.
