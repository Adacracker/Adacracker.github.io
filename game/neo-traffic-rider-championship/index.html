About Neo Traffic Rider Championship

Buckle up: Neo Traffic Rider Championship is a free driving game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the arrow keys and the space bar.

Take the cockpit in Neo Traffic Rider Championship! Race seven rivals across six destinations, from coastal highways and rolling hills to city expressways and snowy mountain roads. Complete 18 championship events, tackle Rider Trials, and upgrade your sportbike’s engine and tyres. Find your passing line, build slipstream for a slingshot, and unleash boost at the right moment. Photorealistic artwork, recorded sportbike audio, and winding roads bring every ride to life. Want to keep riding? Try Unlimited Run on any track. Navigate increasingly dense traffic, complete rotating contracts, replenish boost, and chase your personal distance record.

Why you might like it: Fans of Race Through Traffic and Nitro Racing Gear may like this one too, since it shares their “traffic” and “bike” tags.
