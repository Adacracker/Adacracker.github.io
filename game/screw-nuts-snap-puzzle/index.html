About Screw Nuts Snap Puzzle

Fancy a brain-teaser? You can play Screw Nuts Snap Puzzle, a puzzle game from our Puzzle category, free in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the mouse, taps and touch.

Screw Nuts Snap Puzzle is a colorful puzzle game about matching screws and choosing the right order. Remove accessible screws from layered glass shapes and collect them in boxes of the same color. Each box holds three screws, while spare slots give you room to plan your next move. Clear the layers without filling the spare slots. Explore Unscrew mode and unlock challenges and cat-rescue puzzles as you progress. Use extra slots, box swaps, and a hammer to help with tricky boards. Play with a mouse or touch controls. Progress is saved in the current browser.

Why you might like it: Fans of Slime Buffet and Color Coin Merge may like this one too, since it shares their “logic” and “brain” tags.

How to play Screw Nuts Snap Puzzle

Desktop Left-click screws and buttons Mobile Tap screws and buttons Match three screws of the same color to fill a collection box In Unscrew mode select a screw then an empty hole to move it Use Pause to restart or return home.
