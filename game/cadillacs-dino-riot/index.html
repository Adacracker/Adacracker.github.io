About Cadillacs: Dino Riot

Adventure awaits. You can play Cadillacs: Dino Riot, an adventure game from our Adventure category, free in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

Choose Kira or Bo and fight to stop a dinosaur-smuggling convoy in a colourful 2D rescue adventure. Eight chapters lead through multiple combat zones, dusty roads and dangerous compounds. Chain punches, jump, grab and throw enemies, use found weapons and free caged dinosaurs. Checkpoints, illustrated scenery and an original funky soundtrack keep the adventure moving on desktop or mobile.
