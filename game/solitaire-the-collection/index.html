About Solitaire The Collection

Solitaire The Collection is a board or card game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it.

33 card game variants in one collection. Play familiar solitaires, discover new rules and pick the difficulty — from a calm deal to a real puzzle. Modes include Klondike with draw one or three; Spider with one, two or four suits; FreeCell; Pyramid; Vegas; Yukon, Russian Solitaire and Alaska; Scorpion; Forty Thieves and Josephine; Tri Peaks; Golf; Eight Off; Double Klondike and Easthaven; Spiderette and Simple Simon; Memory on a normal or large board. Take daily challenges, journeys with quests, collect keys, postcards and achievements. Customize card faces, backs and backgrounds, enable dark mode and left-handed layout. Hints, undo and auto.
