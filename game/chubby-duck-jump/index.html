About Chubby Duck Jump

You can play Chubby Duck Jump, an arcade game from our Arcade & Casual category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the space bar, the mouse and taps.

Chubby Duck Jump is a one-button charge-and-jump arcade game starring a chubby yellow duckling on a quest across an endless sky. Hold to charge the jump meter, release to leap to the next floating plank - charge too little and the duck splashes into the meadow below, hold too long and it overshoots into thin air. Every landing scores a point, every run is a new personal best, and perfect streaks light up the sky. Endless, 60-second runs, instant restart, one hand is all you need. Desktop and mobile, fully self-contained single file.

Why you might like it: Chubby Duck Jump has a lot in common with Slime Jump: Crown Quest and Dog Runaway - they share the “casual” and “arcade” tags.

How to play Chubby Duck Jump

Hold mouse tap SPACE to charge the jump - a ring meter shows the power Release to jump The longer the hold the longer the jump read the gap to the next plank and let go at the right moment Nothing else to learn.
