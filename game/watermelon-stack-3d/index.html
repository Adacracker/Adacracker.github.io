About Watermelon Stack 3D

Puzzle time. Watermelon Stack 3D is a puzzle game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the mouse, swipes and touch.

Aim, toss, and merge your way to a giant watermelon! Watermelon Stack 3D is a physics-based fruit merging game with colorful, squishy fruits. Rotate your view around the bowl, adjust your aim, and throw each fruit into the stack. Match two fruits of the same type to create a bigger one and increase your score. Discover 11 fruit types, from tiny blueberries to giant watermelons. Plan each throw carefully: if a fruit falls out of the bowl and touches the table, the game ends. How high can you score?
