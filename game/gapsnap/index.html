About GapSnap

Start your engine. GapSnap loads straight into this page as a free driving game. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the arrow keys, the space bar and the mouse.

You don't drive the road - you build it! Race your kart over floating voxel islands while the road breaks apart. Hold to snap a bridge across every gap, tap to throw up a ramp and jump onto shortcuts, grind on rails and grab coins and blocks. Power-ups: Gold Blocks (free bridges that pay coins) and the Mega Ramp (a huge flip jump to a bonus island). 6 worlds, missions, upgrades and 10 cars to unlock!

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Demolition Derpy and Car Eats Car 3D, give GapSnap a try: they share the “car” and “endless” tags.

How to play GapSnap

PC: steer with A/D or Left/Right arrows. HOLD Space or the left mouse button to build a bridge over a gap. TAP Space or press Up/W to build a ramp and jump. P or Esc pauses. Mobile: swipe left/right to steer, hold anywhere to build a bridge, tap or swipe up for a ramp.
