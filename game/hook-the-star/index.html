About Hook the Star

Fancy a brain-teaser? Hook the Star is a puzzle game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the space bar, the mouse and touch.

Hook the Star is a one-button physics game. Hold to grab the nearest hook with a rope, swing, and let go at the right moment to fly. Each room has up to five stars: hook them all to open the exit gate, then fly through it. Bounce off trampolines, fight side wind, ride a bungee rope and fire a harpoon in later rooms. 40 rooms, 6 speed and survival challenges, a speed bar with your best record, a cheering crowd and an outfit shop for your hero.

Why you might like it: Fans of Sandly: Color Flow and Bend the Dawn: Light Bending Puzzle may like this one too, since it shares their “physics” tag.

How to play Hook the Star

Hold the left mouse button, touch the screen or hold Space to shoot a rope at the nearest hook and swing. Release to let go and fly forward. Light every star in the room by hooking it, then fly through the open gate on the right to finish the room.
