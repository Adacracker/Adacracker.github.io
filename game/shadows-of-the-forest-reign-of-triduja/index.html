About Shadows of the Forest: Reign of Triduja

Shadows of the Forest: Reign of Triduja loads straight into this page as a free adventure game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with W A S D, the mouse and swipes.

Shadows of the Forest, reign of Triduja is a first-person casual horror game. Stranded after a vehicle breakdown, you are trapped in the eerie Realm of Triduya. Your only way out is to complete a mysterious Totem. Explore the surrounding woods to locate the hidden fragments and solve the puzzle. Stay alert—you aren’t alone in these woods.

Why you might like it: Shadows of the Forest: Reign of Triduja has a lot in common with Mall Escape: Backrooms Files and Dont Splash - they share the “horror” and “escape” tags.
