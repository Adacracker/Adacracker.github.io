About Hellforge: Demon Protocol

Hellforge: Demon Protocol is a free shooting game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the arrow keys, the space bar and the mouse.

Hellforge: Demon Protocol is a 2D side-scrolling run-and-gun campaign with 20 industrial missions and progressive difficulty. Fight patrol soldiers, shield troops, gatling brutes, mech hounds and berserkers. Survive 16 trap types, discover secret pickups, collect six weapons, armor, grenades and shields, and defeat ten animated bosses that return as elite enemies in later levels. Checkpoints, three lives, dodging and two weapon slots reward skillful play.
