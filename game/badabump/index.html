About Badabump

You can play Badabump, a puzzle game from our Puzzle category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the mouse, taps and dragging.

Hey there, help little dragon Badabump release as many fireballs as possible! Unfortunately, the little dragon has had a cold and now all the fireballs he spits are frozen. So, he needs your help because if you make them hit each other, you can release the fireballs and heal Badabump!! Come on, what are you waiting for? This game is a classic puzzle game where the only goal is to get the highest score possible! Enjoy fantastic 3D cartoon graphics and really fun sound effects! Come on, play now!
