About Mustang Strike

You can play Mustang Strike, a driving game from our Cars & Racing category, free in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the arrow keys, the space bar and the mouse.

Climb into a P-51 Mustang and take the fight to the occupied Gannet Islands. Dive on convoys racing along the island roads, knock out the anti-aircraft guns that guard them and dogfight the fighters that scramble to hunt you down. Ten missions at sunrise, noon or sunset, in clear skies or heavy cloud and gusting wind, with up to three stars each for speed and flying without damage. Easy mouse flying, plus full keyboard and touch controls.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Sky Ace: Stunt Plane Islands, give Mustang Strike a try: they share the “flight” tag.

How to play Mustang Strike

Mouse: click the game, then move to steer, left click to fire, wheel for throttle. Keyboard: arrow keys to fly, W/S throttle, A/D rudder, Space fire, L wing-leveller, M sound, P pause. Touch: on-screen buttons.
