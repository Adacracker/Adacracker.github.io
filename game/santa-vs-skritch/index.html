About Santa Vs Skritch

Everyone is busy celebrating Christmas and enjoying the holidays. But a critical showdown is happening somewhere in the woods!

Play against a friend or family member or challenge the AI to a duel. Do your best to kick the present past your opponent and score!

Will you help Santa or the Skritch? Come play now and show off your skills!

How to play

Play as Santa or Skritch. For Player 1, use the WASD keys to move around and B to kick. For Player 2, use the arrow keys to move around and K to kick. Try and score the most goals within the time limit.
