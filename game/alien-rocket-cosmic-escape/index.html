About Alien Rocket : Cosmic Escape

Ready for some action? Alien Rocket : Cosmic Escape is a free action game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the arrow keys and the space bar. It can also be played by two people on one device.

Alien Rocket: Cosmic Escape is a fast-paced space survival game where you pilot a futuristic alien rocket from Earth’s atmosphere into deep space.

Dodge dangerous asteroids and enemy UFOs, survive challenging boss battles, complete missions, collect Stardust, unlock achievements, and discover unique futuristic rocket skins.

As you climb higher, the environment changes from the atmosphere to the vastness of space, while the challenges become increasingly intense.

How to play Alien Rocket : Cosmic Escape

Single Player – PC.

W / A / S / D — Move the rocket.

Arrow Keys — Move the rocket.

P / ESC — Pause.

2 Player Split-Screen – PC.

Player 1: W / A / S / D.

Player 2: Arrow Keys.

Both players must survive as long as possible.
