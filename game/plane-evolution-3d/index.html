About Plane Evolution 3D

Quick fun ahead. Plane Evolution 3D is a free arcade game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the mouse, touch and dragging.

Plane Evolution 3D is a fast-paced 3D arcade flying game. Pilot an ever-growing collection of aircraft across 7 historical eras, from prehistoric gliders to futuristic cyberpunk jets. Steer with a simple mouse or finger move, fly through neon gates to boost your score, and dodge saw blades and spikes. Smash mystery cubes and glass gates for a shot at rare planes. Collect all 42 unique aircraft in this easy-to-pick-up, endlessly replayable flyer.

Why you might like it: Fans of Santa Run Challenge and Santa Claus Rush may like this one too, since it shares their “collect” and “running” tags.

How to play Plane Evolution 3D

Move your mouse (or drag your finger on touch devices) left and right to steer your plane — no clicking or buttons needed. Fly through green and multiplier gates to boost your score and evolve your plane. Avoid red gates, spinning saw blades, and spike platforms. Hit mystery cubes and glass gates for a chance to unlock a rare special aircraft.
