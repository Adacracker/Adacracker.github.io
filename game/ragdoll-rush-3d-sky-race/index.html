About Ragdoll Rush 3D: Sky Race

Quick fun ahead. Ragdoll Rush 3D: Sky Race is a free arcade game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

Run, jump and tumble through 25 obstacle courses across five colorful worlds! Race two computer-controlled rivals, dodge giant swinging hammers and use checkpoints to recover after a fall. Collect coins, unlock 15 outfits and upgrade your runner with a coin magnet, faster recovery and extra jump timing. Each upgrade has three tiers, and every course can be completed without upgrades. Improve your best times, earn achievements and collect a daily coin gift. Progress saves automatically. Play on desktop or mobile, in portrait or landscape, with 10 languages available.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Wall Rush 3D and Santa Christmas Run, give Ragdoll Rush 3D: Sky Race a try: they share the “jumping” and “running” tags.

How to play Ragdoll Rush 3D: Sky Race

Desktop WASD or arrow keys to move Space to jump Escape to pause Mobile drag the left joystick to move and tap the arrow button to jump Reach the finish to unlock more courses spend collected coins in the shop.
