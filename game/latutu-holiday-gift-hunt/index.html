About Latutu Holiday Gift Hunt

Latutu Holiday Gift Hunt is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the space bar, the mouse and taps.

Latutu’s Holiday Gift Hunt is a cheerful matching game where you search through a pile of cute holiday toys and gifts to find three identical items. Drag each match into your slots to create a trio and clear space for more. With its cozy holiday theme, adorable Latutu characters, and satisfying match-and-clear gameplay, this game offers a relaxing yet exciting experience for all ages. Perfect for short sessions or high-score chasing during the festive season.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Reindeer Match and Winter Memory, give Latutu Holiday Gift Hunt a try: they share the “christmas” and “matching” tags.

How to play Latutu Holiday Gift Hunt

Tap or click on any item to move it into one of your slots.

Match 3 identical items in the slots to clear them and score points.

Keep making trios to free up space—the game ends if all slots are full or time runs out.
