About Demolition Tycoon 3D

Start with a mini loader and build a demolition fleet, one building at a time. Hold to strike, break away sections and earn coins as your power grows with every hit. Upgrade power, speed, profit and critical hits, then save for bigger machines. Complete 80 contracts across seven districts, from a backyard to the harbor and alpine heights. Unlock seven machines, including the Titan crusher, and continue into tougher contracts beyond the campaign.

How to play

Mouse or touch: hold the demolition button or the building. Keyboard: hold Space to strike; Escape opens or closes settings. Clear each building, collect the reward and start the next contract. Buy upgrades and open Fleet to unlock and equip machines. Optional rewarded videos offer bonuses.
