About Nutty Rush

Nutty Rush is a cozy 3D platformer starring Pöfürdük, a brave little squirrel. The legendary giant nuts of the Great Forest are waking up, and the greedy eagle Grimtalon wants them all. Run, jump and spit nuts at grumpy foes across 5 colorful worlds: Nut Forest, Autumn Valley, Dark Cave, Snowy Mountains and City Rooftops. Team up with Sivri, a cheeky grey mouse, and ride on his back for extra speed. Zoom down twisty slides into ball pits, collect stars and giant nuts, and face big bosses: dodge their attacks, then hit them with nuts when they are open! Features: 25 handcrafted levels in 5 worlds, a boss fight at the end of every world, ride your mouse buddy Sivri, and your progress is saved automatically.

How to play

Move Arrow keys or WASD Jump Space press again in the air to double jump Throw a nut F Sprint Shift Gnaw E Ride or dismount Sivri Q Touch devices on-screen buttons.
