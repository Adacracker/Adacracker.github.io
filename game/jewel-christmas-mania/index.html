About Jewel Christmas Mania

Jewel Christmas Mania is a puzzle game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. You can play it on a computer or a touch screen. On a phone, turn it sideways for the wide landscape screen.

Try this Christmas puzzle game with a Christmas theme. Enjoy the holiday adventure full of snowmen, gingerbread, and Christmas balls. Be rewarded with combos and finish all Christmas levels.

Why you might like it: Fans of Candy Fever and Christmas Puzzle may like this one too, since it shares their “christmas” and “candy” tags.

How to play Jewel Christmas Mania

Swap and match 3 Christmas items of identical shape to remove them. Match 4 or more to create bombs and get more points! If you need help, get more boosters in the special rewards wheel. Gather enough points to win before you run out of moves or time.
