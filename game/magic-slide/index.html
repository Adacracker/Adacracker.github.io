About Magic Slide

Puzzle time. Magic Slide loads straight into this page as a free puzzle game. It is best played on a computer. It is played with dragging.

Slide blocks to complete horizontal lines!

New blocks rise from the bottom each time you move a block.

Don’t let the blocks reach the top!

Clear blocks to fill the pot gauge and reveal diamonds.

Aim for a high score!
