About American Truck Racing Simulator

Hit the highway in American Truck Racing Simulator, a pixel art arcade racer from BREYMANTECH 2026! Race powerful American trucks across 40 tracks in 8 USA cities, including Los Angeles, Las Vegas, New York, and Miami. Overtake rivals, reach checkpoints before time runs out, and finish in the required position to unlock the next track. Collect coins, unlock 7 trucks, and upgrade engine power, acceleration, handling, and nitro. Enjoy a more forgiving challenge from World 4 onward. Watch optional rewarded ads for bonus garage coins or double your race earnings!
