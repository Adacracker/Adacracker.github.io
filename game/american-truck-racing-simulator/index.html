About American Truck Racing Simulator

Start your engine. American Truck Racing Simulator is a free driving game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the arrow keys and the space bar.

Hit the highway in American Truck Racing Simulator, a pixel art arcade racer from BREYMANTECH 2026! Race powerful American trucks across 40 tracks in 8 USA cities, including Los Angeles, Las Vegas, New York, and Miami. Overtake rivals, reach checkpoints before time runs out, and finish in the required position to unlock the next track.
