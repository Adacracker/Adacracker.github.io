About Duckling Rush.io

Quick fun ahead. Duckling Rush.io loads straight into this page as a free arcade game. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

Swim around a big pond, scoop up lost ducklings, and steal them from rival mamas by cutting through their line. Dodge the hungry pike, use your QUACK burst to speed ahead and make rivals wobble, and finish each 90 second round with the longest conga line to win bread. Every duckling has a personality: sleepy, brown, punk, rubber and rare golden ones worth extra.

Why you might like it: Fans of Dog Runaway and Ocean Dash may like this one too, since it shares their “animal” and “casual” tags.

How to play Duckling Rush.io

Mouse or touch hold and drag to steer the duck always swims forward Tap the QUACK button bottom right for a speed burst and shockwave Keyboard arrow keys or WASD to steer Space to quack.
