About Animal Kart

A side-scrolling kart racer starring ten cartoon animals — a crowned lion, a war-bonnet elephant, a rhino in a police cap and friends. Race 60 tracks across jungle, desert, south pole and volcano, double-jumping hazards and slamming boost pads to beat the pack to the line. Every finish earns stars and coins to unlock new racers and tune their speed and jump.
