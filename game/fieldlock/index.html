About Fieldlock

The Vault beneath the city has broken its lock, and its cores have scattered into the deep chambers. No hand can touch them — only the field can move them. Slide your electromagnetic rig around the chamber's rim and switch between PULL and PUSH to guide each core to the exit. Every chamber is a handcrafted physics puzzle: glass that breaks, shields that bounce, gates that need a charge, currents that drift, gravity wells, rotating arms, heat that builds and a twin-core finale.

How to play

Touch / mouse: drag along the rim to move the rig; tap the dial to switch PULL / PUSH. Keyboard: arrow keys move the rig, Space switches PULL / PUSH, Enter confirms, Esc pauses. Guide the core to the exit — faster routes earn up to three marks.
