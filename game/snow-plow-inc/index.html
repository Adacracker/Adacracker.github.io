About Snow Plow Inc.

Snow Plow Inc. loads straight into this page as a free simulation game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the arrow keys, W A S D and touch.

Snow Plow Inc. is a relaxing snow plowing game. Drive your orange plow truck through deep snow and thick ice, fill your truck bed with snow blocks, then head back to the yard and sell your load for coins. LEVELS: 15 hand-built levels. The snow gets tougher as you go, from soft powder to packed snow and solid ice. Clear the field to earn up to 3 stars and unlock the next level. UPGRADES: Spend coins on plow count, plow teeth, plow power, engine, earnings and capacity. Progress saves automatically.

Why you might like it: Fans of Tidy Bot and Build a Keycap Pyramid may like this one too, since it shares their “relaxation” and “casual” tags.

How to play Snow Plow Inc.

Keyboard WASD or Arrow Keys to drive Mobile Touch and drag anywhere to steer Drive onto a pad in the yard to Sell Upgrade open the Shop or pick Levels.
