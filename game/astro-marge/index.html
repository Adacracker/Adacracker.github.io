About Astro Marge

Quick fun ahead. Astro Marge is a free arcade game that runs right in your browser. It works on phones, tablets and computers. You play it with the mouse and taps. On a phone, turn it sideways for the wide landscape screen.

Merge falling planets, create combos, and build the biggest stack! The pressure keeps rising, so think fast and beat your high score.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Chaindrop and TILT, give Astro Marge a try: they share the “ball” and “physics” tags.

How to play Astro Marge

Move your mouse or finger to aim. Click or tap to drop a planet. Merge matching planets to grow your stack and score higher!
