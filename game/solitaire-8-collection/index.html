About Solitaire 8 Collection

Solitaire 8 Collection loads straight into this page as a free board or card game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the mouse, taps and touch.

Solitaire 8 Collection brings together eight classic solitaire games in one place: Klondike, Spider, FreeCell, TriPeaks, Pyramid, Golf, Yukon and Scorpion. Each one keeps its own rules, its own options and its own statistics, and you can switch between them whenever you like. Deals come from a list checked to have a solution, so you are never stuck with an impossible hand. Hints are free and undo is unlimited, so you can take your time and think. There is a new daily challenge every day, the same deal for every player, with a streak to keep alive. Your games played, win rate, best times and fewest moves are recorded for every game.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Klondike Solitaire Treasures and Solitaire The Collection, give Solitaire 8 Collection a try: they share the “card” and “solitaire” tags.
