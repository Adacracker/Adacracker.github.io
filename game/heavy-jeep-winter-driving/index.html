About Heavy Jeep Winter Driving

Buckle up: Heavy Jeep Winter Driving loads straight into this page as a free driving game. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the arrow keys and W A S D.

You won't want to miss out on this game if you love driving through snow. Pass through a town, over the hill, and through mountain trees. Collect gifts, coins, trophies, and gasoline. Make it to the checkpoints and the Finish Line before time runs out. Make sure you don't get hit or bumped by other vehicles. You can enjoy all this and more when you play Heavy Jeep Winter Driving.

Why you might like it: Heavy Jeep Winter Driving has a lot in common with Monster Truck 3D Winter and Arctic Truck Driver - they share the “car” and “speed” tags.
