About Dino Dash

Adventure awaits. Dino Dash is a free adventure game that runs right in your browser. It works on phones, tablets and computers. On a phone, hold it upright: the game is made for portrait mode.

Embark on an adrenaline-fueled adventure as a baby dinosaur in Dino Dash, where intuitive gameplay meets high-speed chases. Navigate vibrant landscapes, dodge predators, and escape danger with simple controls. Swift reflexes are key to surviving this exhilarating, endless pursuit!

Why you might like it: Fans of Cadillacs: Dino Riot and Donutosaur 2 may like this one too, since it shares their “dinosaur” tag.

Dino Dash: FAQ

Does Dino Dash cost anything?

No, it costs nothing. Dino Dash is free to play in your browser on Arcade Cabin. It is supplied by GamePix, and the provider may show ads before or during the game.

Can I play Dino Dash on my phone?

Yes. GamePix lists Dino Dash as mobile-friendly, so it works with touch on phones and tablets as well as on computers. It is made for portrait mode, so hold your phone upright.

Do I need to download Dino Dash?

No. Dino Dash runs in your browser right on this page - press Play to start. There is nothing to install and no account to create.
