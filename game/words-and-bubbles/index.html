About Words and Bubbles

Fancy a brain-teaser? Words and Bubbles is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with taps.

Words and Bubbles is a relaxing association puzzle where you find words connected by a common meaning. Merge matching bubbles, reveal new categories, and use completed categories in further combinations.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Bubble Merge: Word Sort and Fruit Box Sort, give Words and Bubbles a try: they share the “sorting” and “bubble” tags.

How to play Words and Bubbles

Tap the bubbles containing words that belong to the same category. If the combination is correct, the selected bubbles will merge into one larger bubble showing the category name.

Some completed categories can be used in later combinations, so watch for new connections.

Complete every available combination before you run out of moves. Use boosters whenever you need a little help.
