About Magline Moto

Magline Moto is a free driving game that runs right in your browser. It works on phones, tablets and computers. Controls use the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar. On a phone, turn it sideways for the wide landscape screen.

Magline Moto is a futuristic stunt bike racer. Ride a hyperbike on glowing energy-ring wheels across 30 hand-built levels in 3 worlds: Neon District, Emberfall and Cryo Spire. Flip in the air for time bonuses, blast drones, aliens, mines, barrels and laser gates with your front laser, and burn nitro to clear huge gaps. Survive boss chases against the Forge Matriarch and the Cryo Sentinel, earn cash and unlock 12 bikes with unique perks.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Hilltop Hustle and Extreme Quad Bike Racing, give Magline Moto a try: they share the “driving” and “racing” tags.

How to play Magline Moto

Arrow keys or WASD to ride brake and lean flip in the air Space to fire the front laser Shift for nitro R to restart P to pause On mobile use the on-screen touch buttons Best played in landscape.
