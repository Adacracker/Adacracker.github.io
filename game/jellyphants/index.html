About Jellyphants

Got a minute? Jellyphants is an arcade game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. Controls use taps.

Baby elephants are hungry!

Feed them with delicious jelly and enjoy the flow!

Use conveyor belt to feed a lot of elephants at once, but be warned - they are picky and only eat jelly of their own color.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Polar Fall and Claw Machine : Animal Friends, give Jellyphants a try: they share the “arcade” and “animal” tags.

How to play Jellyphants

Tap to the elephant to send it to the conveyor. If elephant returned to the waiting spot. tap on it to send it to the conveyor again.
