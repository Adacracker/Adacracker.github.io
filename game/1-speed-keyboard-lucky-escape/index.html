About +1 Speed Keyboard Lucky Escape

You can play +1 Speed Keyboard Lucky Escape, an adventure game from our Adventure category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar. It can also be played by two people on one device.

+1 Speed Keyboard Lucky Escape is a fast-paced brainrot collecting and escape game where luck, upgrades, speed, and survival all come together on one giant keyboard! Launch Lucky Blocks as far as possible with your bazooka, discover increasingly valuable Brainrots, and then run for your life as Tung Tung Sahur chases you back toward the Safe Zone. Collect 93 different Brainrots, upgrade your launch range, increase the value of every +1 Speed pickup!

How to play +1 Speed Keyboard Lucky Escape

Player 1.

Move: W, A, S, D.

Jump: Space.

Speed Up: Shift.

Launch Lucky Brainrot: TAB or E.

Interact: E.

Upgrade Brainrot: Q.

Open Rebirth Menu: T.

Player 2.

Move: Arrow Keys.

Jump: L.

Speed Up: K.

Launch Lucky Brainrot: U.

Interact: O.

Upgrade Brainrot: U.

Open Rebirth Menu: Y.

Mobile Controls.

Mobile gameplay is available in 1 Player mode.
