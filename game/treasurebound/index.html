About TreasureBound

Treasure Bound is a turn-based treasure hunt on a hex grid. Every tile might hide gold or nothing. Spend your shovels wisely, read the board, and claim the biggest finds before they are gone. Play two ways: Adventure: clear solo levels with a set number of shovels. Beat the level by uncovering the treasures before you run out. Duel: face an opponent across three rounds. Take turns digging tiles, bank points, and pick powers that can steal the lead. Between matches, return to camp, climb the level path, and unlock more of the hunt. Easy to pick up, hard to master when the last shovel is on the line.
