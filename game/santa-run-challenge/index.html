About Santa Run Challenge

Got a minute? Santa Run Challenge is an arcade game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It works on phones, tablets and computers. Controls use the mouse and touch. On a phone, turn it sideways for the wide landscape screen.

In the game Santa Run Challenge will help the brave Santa to fulfill his mission. Your hero will run along the city streets with a bag in his hands. As soon as various obstacles and monsters get in his way, you will have to click on the screen and make him jump. Thus, he will jump over the monsters and continue his journey. You can also strike at monsters with a bag and knock it down.

Why you might like it: Fans of Santa Christmas Run and Christmas Panda Adventure may like this one too, since it shares their “christmas” and “winter” tags.

How to play Santa Run Challenge

Use mouse on desktop or touch buttons on your mobile device.
