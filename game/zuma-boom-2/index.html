About Zuma Boom 2

Puzzle time. Zuma Boom 2 is a puzzle game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the space bar, the mouse and taps.

Enjoy the sequel to a classic arcade Zuma Boom game. Shoot colored balls to create matches of three or more of the same color and clear the chain. Do not let the balls reach the finish hole. Create chain reactions to earn bonus points and achieve higher scores. Whats new in the sequel: - 15 new levels - Armored balls that you have to match twice - Bridges that let the line of balls cross itself - New bonus: rainbow ball.
