About Santa Claus Coloring

Santa Claus Coloring loads straight into this page as a free arcade game. It works on phones, tablets and computers. You play it with the mouse and taps. On a phone, turn it sideways for the wide landscape screen.

For Christmas holidays you can relax with Coloring games. This is Coloring Game with Santa Claus. You can choose one image of twelve and to start with coloring. All images is with funny Santa and his friends. Choose your favorite image and play. Use the colors fro the right side of the game. On the left side of the game you can change the brush side. Erase colors if you do some mistakes. You can save the colored image and to send to someone as Christmas card.

Why you might like it: Fans of Santa Claus Coloring Book and Fun Christmas Coloring may like this one too, since it shares their “christmas” and “coloring” tags.

How to play Santa Claus Coloring

Use mouse or tap on the image to color.

Santa Claus Coloring: FAQ

Does Santa Claus Coloring cost anything?

No, it costs nothing. Santa Claus Coloring is free to play in your browser on Arcade Cabin. It is supplied by GameDistribution, and the provider may show ads before or during the game.

Can I play Santa Claus Coloring on a tablet?

Yes. GameDistribution lists Santa Claus Coloring as mobile-friendly, so it works with touch on phones and tablets as well as on computers. It uses a wide landscape screen.

No. Santa Claus Coloring runs in your browser right on this page - press Play to start. There is nothing to install and no account to create.
