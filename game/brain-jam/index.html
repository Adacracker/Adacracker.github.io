About Brain Jam

Brain Jam is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. You can play it on a computer or a touch screen. Controls use the mouse, touch and dragging. On a phone, turn it sideways for the wide landscape screen.

Brain Jam is a sliding car puzzle with one twist: it counts the calories your brain burns while you think. The parking lot is jammed. Slide the cars out of the way and drive the red one to the exit. Every second you spend thinking feeds a live calorie counter, and the longer you concentrate without moving a car, the faster it climbs. Solve a level in par moves and your result gets a perfect bonus. 120 hand-checked levels plus a Daily Puzzle that is the same for every player. One-finger controls, works on phone and desktop. No timers, no lives, no pressure.

Why you might like it: Fans of Rope Chef and Slime Buffet may like this one too, since it shares their “logic” and “puzzle” tags.

How to play Brain Jam

Mouse or touch drag a car along its lane Cars move only forward and back Clear a path and slide the red car out through the exit on the right Stuck Press Hint to see the next good move.
