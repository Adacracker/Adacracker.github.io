About Save Santa Claus

Save Santa Claus is a free arcade game that runs right in your browser. You can play it on a computer or a touch screen. You play it with the mouse and touch. On a phone, turn it sideways for the wide landscape screen.

This is a casual game in which you as a player should fight with the snowman and avoid icicles. Santa Claus has to throw the gift boxes to the snowman by moving up and down and destroy them so you can get points. Gradually, the speed of the game is increased to make you confused.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Lumberjack Santa Claus and Santa Christmas Run, give Save Santa Claus a try: they share the “christmas” and “santaclause” tags.

How to play Save Santa Claus

Use the mouse click or touch to move Santa Claus up and down and destroy the snowman.
