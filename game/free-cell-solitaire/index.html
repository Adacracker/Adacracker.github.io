About Free Cell Solitaire

You can play Free Cell Solitaire, a board or card game from our Board & Cards category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the space bar.

The Goal: Build four HomeCells by suit in ascending order (Ace to King).

The Rules:

Move: Shift the bottom card of any column to a FreeCell to uncover the cards beneath.

Build: Move cards between columns in descending order with alternating colors (e.g., a Red 9 on a Black 10).

Empty Spaces: Any card can fill an empty tableau column—use these spots wisely to maneuver!

The Win: Keep moving cards one by one until all suits are stacked in their HomeCells.
