About Candlebound Coven

Got a minute? You can play Candlebound Coven, an arcade game from our Arcade & Casual category, free in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the arrow keys, W A S D and dragging.

Candlebound Coven is a top-down survivor-like about a witch's cat baking her way through five Halloween dreams. Rites fire on their own — a bell, a candle wisp, a frost halo, a purifying chime — and you only steer Mina and choose which one levels up.

Why you might like it: Candlebound Coven has a lot in common with Trick Paws and Witchy - they share the “arcade” and “cat” tags.

How to play Candlebound Coven

Desktop: WASD or the arrow keys move Mina. Your rites fire by themselves.

Mobile: drag anywhere on the field to steer.

When you level up, pick one of three cards. Survive each dream's full clock to open the next one; a fallen run can be revived once.
