About Swordstep

Swordstep is a free adventure game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

Throw the sword. Climb the tower. Relight the sun. Climb, cross and drop through 50 levels in five areas, from candlelit rooftops to a crystal cave and a frozen lake, up to the summit where the sun comes back. Find 150 stars and unlock 18 characters and 9 swords. Plays on desktop and on phones held either way.

Why you might like it: Swordstep has a lot in common with Drople and Trap Step - they share the “platformer” and “jumping” tags.

How to play Swordstep

Desktop: Arrow keys or WASD to move and aim. Space or Z to jump. X or left click to throw the sword (press again to call it back). C, Shift or right click for Phase Shift (a quick dash). Esc or P for the menu.
