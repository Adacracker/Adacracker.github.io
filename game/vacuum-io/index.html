About Vacuum.IO

.io fans, take note: Vacuum.IO loads straight into this page as a free .io game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the arrow keys, the mouse and touch.

In Arena Mode, collect trash and household items to grow bigger, absorb larger objects, and compete against other robots.

In Mission Mode, clean rooms in homes and schools. Complete each task to unlock the next room and progress through new areas.

Collect, grow, and become the ultimate robot vacuum!

Why you might like it: Fans of SnakeArea.io and Red and Green Christmas may like this one too, since it shares their “arcade” and “multiplayer” tags.

How to play Vacuum.IO

Desktop Controls.

Mouse / A-D / Arrow Keys: Move.

Hold Left Mouse Button / W / Up Arrow: Boost.

Mobile Controls.

Touch & Drag: Move.

Hold with a Second Finger: Boost.
