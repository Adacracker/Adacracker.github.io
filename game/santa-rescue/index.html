About Santa Rescue

Pack your bags: Santa Rescue is a free adventure game that runs right in your browser. It works on phones, tablets and computers. You play it with dragging. On a phone, hold it upright: the game is made for portrait mode.

This is a Christmas game.

Move the pins to rescue the Santa and collect the gifts.

This game is very fun to play.

There are 30 levels to complete.

Enjoy!
