About Candy Flow Factory

You can play Candy Flow Factory, a puzzle game from our Puzzle category, free in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the mouse, taps and touch.

Connect matching candy machines with colorful tubes and fill every playable tile in Candy Flow Factory! Plan each route carefully because tubes cannot cross or overlap. Enjoy 90 handcrafted puzzles, cheerful candy-factory visuals, smooth mouse and touch controls, helpful hints, and relaxing gameplay with no timer or move limit.

Why you might like it: Candy Flow Factory has a lot in common with Candy Sort and Jewel Christmas Mania - they share the “matching” and “color” tags.

How to play Candy Flow Factory

Press or tap a candy machine, then drag through adjacent tiles to connect it with the machine of the same color. Move only up, down, left, or right. Tubes cannot cross, overlap, enter cream blocked tiles, or connect to another color. Connect every pair and fill all dark playable tiles to complete the level. Drag backward to change a path.
