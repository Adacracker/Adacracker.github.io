About Race Through Traffic

Start your engine. You can play Race Through Traffic, a driving game from our Cars & Racing category, free in your browser. It runs on desktop and mobile alike. Controls use the arrow keys. On a phone, turn it sideways for the wide landscape screen.

A single-player arcade racing game where you drive along a busy road, overtake cars, and try to survive as long as possible. The farther you go and the riskier your maneuvers are, the higher your final score becomes. Collect coins, unlock new cars, and upgrade speed, handling, and other stats. Choose a mode, watch the road carefully, and set new records in each run.

Why you might like it: Fans of Police Chase Run and Need For Drift may like this one too, since it shares their “car” and “race” tags.

How to play Race Through Traffic

Control the car with on-screen buttons or the keyboard Press A or the left arrow to move left Press D or the right arrow to move right Use the up and down arrows to accelerate and brake when available in the current mode.
