About Tiny Tomb

Tiny Tomb loads straight into this page as a free adventure game. You can play it on a computer or a touch screen. On a phone, hold it upright: the game is made for portrait mode.

Unveil the secrets of Tiny Tomb, a captivating 3D voxel adventure where you navigate enigmatic chambers and unearth treasures. Strategically explore, overcome traps, and complete quests while evading lurking enemies. Harness unique abilities to uncover hidden mysteries, collect coins, and unlock the tomb’s puzzled passageways.

Tiny Tomb: FAQ

Is Tiny Tomb free?

Yes. Tiny Tomb is free to play in your browser on Arcade Cabin. It is supplied by GamePix, and the provider may show ads before or during the game.

Can I play Tiny Tomb on a tablet?

Yes. GamePix lists Tiny Tomb as mobile-friendly, so it works with touch on phones and tablets as well as on computers. It is made for portrait mode, so hold your phone upright.

Do I need to download Tiny Tomb?

No. Tiny Tomb runs in your browser right on this page - press Play to start. There is nothing to install and no account to create.
