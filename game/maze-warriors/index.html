About Maze Warriors

Fast hands help here. Maze Warriors is an action game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the arrow keys, the space bar and taps. It can also be played by two people on one device.

Maze Warriors is a tactical tank duel inside a randomly built battlefield maze. Every shot ricochets off concrete walls, so aim smart and watch out for your own bullets. One hit ends the round. Grab supply drops for lasers, shotguns, shields and rapid fire. Battle a clever CPU or a friend on the same keyboard, switch between 2D and 3D views, and unlock 10 legendary tanks in the Hangar.

Why you might like it: Maze Warriors has a lot in common with Anything Goes: Stickman Brawl and Hands Attack - they share the “2players” and “fighting” tags.

How to play Maze Warriors

Player 1: move with W A S D and fire with Space. Player 2: move with the arrow keys and fire with Enter. On mobile, drive with the left joystick and tap the fire button. Gamepads are supported. Bullets bounce off walls, so use angles to hit your enemy. Collect supply crates for special weapons. Press C to change the camera and Esc to pause. Win 5 battles to claim victory.
