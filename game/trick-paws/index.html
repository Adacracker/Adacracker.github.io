About Trick Paws

Trick Paws: Trap Run is a funny troll platformer where nothing is what it looks like! Guide a brave little cat through 100 tricky levels full of hidden spikes, crumbling floors, falling rocks, rolling traps and fake platforms that will surprise you when you least expect it. Run, jump and double jump across 4 worlds - Rookie, Explorer, Hero and Legend - and blast enemies with your cats blaster or stomp them from above.
