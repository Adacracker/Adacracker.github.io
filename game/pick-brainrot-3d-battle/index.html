About Pick Brainrot: 3D Battle

Ready for some action? You can play Pick Brainrot: 3D Battle, an action game from our Action category, free in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with W A S D, the space bar and the mouse.

Choose one of two Brainrots, transform into them, and engage in intense battles with other players. Defeat opponents and unlock new abilities: a Brainrot morph, unique weapons, and powerful abilities. Every battle is a chance to prove yourself! At level 5, you'll unlock a protective shield, and at level 10, you'll unlock slow-motion , which will give you a huge advantage in battle. Earn titles for your victories, from "Noob" to a true "Pro"!

Why you might like it: Fans of Hero Rush and Anything Goes: Stickman Brawl may like this one too, since it shares their “arena” tag.
