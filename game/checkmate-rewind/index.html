About Checkmate Rewind

Board and card game fans, this one is for you: Checkmate Rewind is a free board or card game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the mouse, taps and dragging.

Every case starts at the end: a chess game is over, and the final position is the only evidence. Take the moves back one by one - return captured pieces, undo castling, en passant and promotions - and restore the starting position in exactly the number of moves the case allows. Learn the backwards moves in 8 short lessons, then crack 55 handcrafted cases, from quick blunders to famous opening traps. A new case every day comes from real Lichess games.

Why you might like it: Checkmate Rewind has a lot in common with Ultimate TicTacToe and Chess Multiplayer Online - they share the “board” and “logic” tags.

How to play Checkmate Rewind

Tap or click a piece, then tap the square it came from - or drag it back. Unwind the game to the starting position in exactly the given number of moves. Take back undoes your last move, Start over resets the case, Hint shows which piece moved last.
