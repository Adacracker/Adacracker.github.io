About Wrecking Ball Tycoon

Simulation fans, take note: you can play Wrecking Ball Tycoon, a simulation game from our Build & Simulate category, free in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the space bar, the mouse and taps.

Swing a wrecking ball through six colorful districts and grow your demolition business. Hit the green zone for a super strike with extra damage, slow motion, zoom and comic impact bursts. Smash supports, send bricks and sparks flying, then spend the cash on bigger balls, faster swings and five cranes. Collect ball skins, complete daily contracts and renew your license for permanent bonuses.

Why you might like it: Wrecking Ball Tycoon has a lot in common with Crush Yard Tycoon and Smash Yard - they share the “idle” and “simulation” tags.

How to play Wrecking Ball Tycoon

Desktop hold SWING with the left mouse button or hold SPACE Release in the green zone for a SUPER HIT with 2 8x damage Use W S Up Down or LOW MID HIGH to aim Mobile hold and release SWING tap the height buttons or drag vertically over the building.
