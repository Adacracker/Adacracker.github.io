About Sunfield Vegetable Farm

You can play Sunfield Vegetable Farm, a simulation game from our Build & Simulate category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

Grow, harvest and sell fresh vegetables in Sunfield, a cozy 3D farming game! Plant carrots, tomatoes and cabbages, water them and watch them grow. Stock the market carts, serve customers at the checkout and collect your money. Buy new land to expand your farm with more beds, fields and carts. Unlock corn, pumpkin, eggplant, potato, onion, pepper and watermelon. Hire a cashier and farm workers to help you. Mode: single player, endless farm progress. Characters: farmer, customers, cashier, farm workers. Saves automatically. Plays on desktop and mobile.
