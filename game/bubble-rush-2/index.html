About Bubble Rush 2

Bubble Rush 2 is a circular bubble shooter where every shot starts from the center. Match three or more bubbles of the same color, break supporting connections, and clear each formation before new rows move inward from the walls. Complete challenging patterns, adapt to different movement modes, and use powerful bonus bubbles to survive increasingly difficult levels.

How to play

Aim with the mouse or touchscreen and release to shoot. Match three or more bubbles of the same color to remove them. Bubbles that lose their connection to an active wall will fall. Watch the arrows around the center to see which side will move next. Clear all required bubbles before they reach the central shooter. Use bonus bubbles to destroy colors, blast groups, or burn through bubbles along the shot trajectory.
