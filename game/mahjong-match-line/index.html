About Mahjong Match Line

Mahjong Match Line is a puzzle game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the space bar.

Mahjong Match Line is a relaxing tile-matching puzzle inspired by classic Mahjong. Move tiles horizontally and vertically across the board to bring identical tiles together and merge them. Plan each move carefully, clear the board, and enjoy increasingly challenging levels in a peaceful atmosphere.

Why you might like it: Mahjong Match Line has a lot in common with Bird Tile Trio and Puppy Paw Match - they share the “matching” and “tiles” tags.

How to play Mahjong Match Line

Select a tile and move it horizontally or vertically.

Bring two identical tiles together to merge and remove them.

Use the empty spaces to reposition tiles and create new matches.

Clear all the tiles to complete the level.
