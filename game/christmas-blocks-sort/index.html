About Christmas Blocks Sort

Fancy a brain-teaser? Christmas Blocks Sort is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with dragging.

Blocks Sort Christmas Edition brings a cozy holiday twist to the classic sorting puzzle. All the blocks have turned into festive Christmas colors like bright red, pine green, frosty blue, and warm gold. They start mixed together in different columns, and your job is to sort them so each column ends up with just one color. The rules are simple, but the challenge is still satisfying. Think ahead, plan your moves, and slowly bring order to the holiday chaos.

Why you might like it: Christmas Blocks Sort has a lot in common with Atomic Puzzle XMas and Christmas sorting - they share the “logic” and “christmas” tags.

How to play Christmas Blocks Sort

Drag a block into any empty spot on the grid. Match all blocks by color to complete the level successfully.
