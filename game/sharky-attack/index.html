About Sharky Attack

Fast hands help here. Sharky Attack is an action game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it.

Get ready for thrilling underwater action! Finish each round with a full belly and unlock up to 10 different sharks, each with unique abilities. Explore the ocean and feast on 30 different types of fish. Collect coins and trophies and most importantly, stay alive!

Why you might like it: Sharky Attack has a lot in common with One More and Dice Hero - they share the “attack” tag.

How to play Sharky Attack

Your mission is to eat as many fish as you can in just 60 seconds. At the end of each round, earn coins and trophies. Spend coins to unlock power-ups that boost your shark's speed, strength and invincibility. Use trophies to unlock new stages and face even bigger challenges!
