About Cop Simulator: My Prison 3D

Simulation fans, take note: you can play Cop Simulator: My Prison 3D, a simulation game from our Build & Simulate category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer. You play it with W A S D, the mouse and taps.

In this engaging simulator, you are the sheriff who can turn a prison into a profitable business. Build new blocks, hire staff, keep order, and increase income. Every decision affects security and budget – become the true king of prison management!

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Obby Plane Power Challenge: Fly and Bee Rise, give Cop Simulator: My Prison 3D a try: they share the “upgrade” and “prison” tags.

How to play Cop Simulator: My Prison 3D

1. Goal – build the largest prison, earn maximum money, and top the sheriff rankings.

2. Controls:

On PC: Use WASD to move around the prison, click with the mouse on buildings, inmates, or menus to build and upgrade.

On mobile: Move using the on-screen joystick in the bottom left corner, tap objects with your finger to interact.

Open upgrade menus, build new cells and workshops, allocate budget – all intuitive and accessible with a few taps.
