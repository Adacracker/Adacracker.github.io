About Noob vs Pro : Drift Tag

Noob vs Pro : Drift Tag is a free driving game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use W A S D, the space bar and touch. It can also be played by two people on one device.

Noob vs Pro: Drift Tag — use your speed, drifting skills, and reflexes to pass the bomb to your opponents! The car holding the bomb must pass it to another player before the timer runs out. If you are caught with the bomb, your car explodes and you are eliminated. Drift through tight spaces, use ramps and obstacles, outsmart your rivals, and become the last driver standing! Race with different cars, compete with your friends, and rise from Noob to Pro!

Why you might like it: Fans of Extreme Gear Shift Racing and Roadster Dash: Halloween Night may like this one too, since it shares their “car” and “2 player” tags.

How to play Noob vs Pro : Drift Tag

Use WASD keys to move Press E to make your car jump The game can be played on both mobile and PC with touch controls available on mobile devices.
