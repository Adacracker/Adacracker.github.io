About Pizza Now!

Ready to run the show? You can play Pizza Now!, a simulation game from our Build & Simulate category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer.

Enjoy the hustle and bustle of a busy pizza restaurant. Experience the daily rhythm, maintain cleanliness and create a satisfying experience for every customer who walks through your doors. How fast can you serve the next order? Let's find out!

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Falafel Master and Kalulu Tanhulu: ASMR Mukbang, give Pizza Now! a try: they share the “cook” and “manager” tags.

How to play Pizza Now!

Manage your pizzeria by preparing pizzas, serving customers, cleaning tables and hiring staff. Invest in upgrades and expand your restaurant to serve more guests efficiently. Progress unlocks new locations and custom looks for your character.
