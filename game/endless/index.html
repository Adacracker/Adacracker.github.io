About Endless

Adventure awaits. Endless is a free adventure game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with taps.

Run up the endless stairs!

Tap or hold the Climb button to climb.

Press the Turn button to change direction.

Don’t slow down, or the monster will catch you!

Turn the wrong way, and it’s game over.

Magic Flask: Climb with only the Climb button for a limited time.

Blue Lantern: Makes the monster disappear for a limited time.

Why you might like it: Endless has a lot in common with Dont Splash and Magikmon - they share the “monster” and “escape” tags.

How to play Endless

Tap repeatedly or hold the Climb button to climb the stairs.

Press the Turn button to change direction.
