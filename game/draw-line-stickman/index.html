About Draw Line Stickman

Fancy a brain-teaser? You can play Draw Line Stickman, a puzzle game from our Puzzle category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer.

Draw Line Stickman is a creative physics puzzle game where every line you draw can save the hero. Observe each dangerous situation, sketch a barrier or clever solution, and protect the stickman from traps, enemies, falling objects, and other hazards. Each level introduces a new challenge that tests your imagination, timing, and problem-solving skills. Draw freely, experiment with different strategies, and discover the smartest way to survive. Simple controls, playful graphics, and increasingly tricky puzzles make every level fun and rewarding. Can you protect the stickman and complete them all?
