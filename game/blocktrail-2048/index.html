About Blocktrail 2048

Puzzle time. Blocktrail 2048 loads straight into this page as a free puzzle game. It works on phones, tablets and computers. Controls use the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar. On a phone, turn it sideways for the wide landscape screen.
