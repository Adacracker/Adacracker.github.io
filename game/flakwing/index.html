About FlakWing

FlakWing loads straight into this page as a free shooting game. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

Flakwing is a vertical air-combat shooter where every upgrade counts. Battle through 18 missions over tropical islands, deserts, frozen mountains and enemy bases. Dodge incoming fire, rescue stranded pilots and defeat powerful bosses. Collect stars to upgrade six aircraft systems and unlock stronger planes. Clear Easy to unlock Medium, then Hard, or challenge yourself in Daily Sortie and Endless Survival. Progress saves automatically in your browser.

Why you might like it: FlakWing has a lot in common with Paint or Hunt and One Shot Duel Snipe Hide - they share the “shoot-em-up” tag.

How to play FlakWing

Move with WASD or arrow keys, or drag with your mouse or finger. Your cannon fires automatically. Use Space for bombs, Q for shields and E for lasers when available.

On mobile, tap the bottom weapon buttons. Press P/Escape or tap Pause to pause or resume.

Collect stars, stay inside rescue beacons to save pilots, and upgrade your aircraft between missions. Special weapons have limited charges.
