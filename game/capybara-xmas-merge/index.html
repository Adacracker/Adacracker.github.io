About Capybara Xmas Merge

You can play Capybara Xmas Merge, a puzzle game from our Puzzle category, free in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the space bar, taps and touch.

Capybara Xmas Merge is a cute and cozy holiday game where you combine adorable Christmas capybaras!

Drop festive capybaras into the box, watch them roll and bump into each other, and merge into bigger, funnier holiday characters.

Create giant capybaras, score points, and enjoy the warm winter atmosphere.

Why you might like it: Capybara Xmas Merge has a lot in common with Sorting Xmas Balls and Christmas Penguin Puzzle - they share the “christmas” and “ball” tags.

How to play Capybara Xmas Merge

Tap to drop a capybara into the jar.

When two identical capybaras touch, they merge into a larger one.

Plan your moves carefully — the jar fills up quickly!

Build the biggest capybara you can and earn the highest score before you run out of space.
