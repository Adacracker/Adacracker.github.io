About Merge and Harvest

Ready to run the show? You can play Merge and Harvest, a simulation game from our Build & Simulate category, free in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the space bar, the mouse and taps.

Build a thriving market, one merge at a time! Combine matching fruits, vegetables, and farm goods to discover new products. Complete customer orders, earn coins, and unlock more space on your board. Discover new production stations, plan your moves, and turn a handful of fresh produce into a bustling market!

Why you might like it: Fans of Eggspansion and My Perfect Farm may like this one too, since it shares their “farm” tag.

How to play Merge and Harvest

Use your mouse or touchscreen. Click or tap production buttons to create items. Drag two identical items together to merge them into a higher-level product. Complete customer orders to earn coins, then unlock more board spaces and production stations. Keep some spaces free to continue creating and merging!
