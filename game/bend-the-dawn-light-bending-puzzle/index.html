About Bend the Dawn: Light Bending Puzzle

Fancy a brain-teaser? Bend the Dawn: Light Bending Puzzle loads straight into this page as a free puzzle game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the arrow keys, the mouse and taps.

Bend light to wake a sleeping machine. Turn prisms to split colors, redirect beams with mirrors and focus them with lenses across 50 connected puzzle levels. Later rooms add living supports, changing tides and gravity wells. Watch the light react as you experiment, undo moves and use progressive hints. Return for 12 optional repair studies that challenge you to find what stopped working.

Why you might like it: Bend the Dawn: Light Bending Puzzle has a lot in common with Rewind House and Snack & Warp - they share the “logic” and “relaxing” tags.

How to play Bend the Dawn: Light Bending Puzzle

Click or tap a part to select it. Drag its round handle to turn it, or its body to move it when movable. Drag new parts from the tray onto the light. Wake each bloom with its own color. Undo reverses a move; Reset restores the room; Hint explains what is missing. Keyboard: [ and ] select parts, arrows move, Q/E rotate (Shift for fine turns), R changes a divider share, M switches the tide, Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes, P pauses.
