About Crown & Conquest

Strategy fans, take note: Crown & Conquest is a strategy game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

Crown & Conquest is a single-player strategy game where you lead a kingdom across a contested map. Choose a campaign, send troops between cities, and capture enemy territory. Click or tap cities and the on-screen controls to issue orders. Drag to explore the map; use the mouse wheel or pinch with two fingers to zoom.

Why you might like it: Crown & Conquest has a lot in common with Mars Invasion and Legiontide Army Runner - they share the “war” and “army” tags.

How to play Crown & Conquest

Desktop: Click cities and buttons to give orders. Drag to move the map and use the mouse wheel to zoom. WASD or arrow keys move the map; 1/2/3 select 50/80/100% of troops; R rallies troops; Space or Esc pauses.

Mobile: Tap cities and buttons. Drag with one finger to move the map and pinch with two fingers to zoom.

Capture rival cities while defending your own.
