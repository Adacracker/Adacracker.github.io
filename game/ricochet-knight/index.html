About Ricochet Knight

Ricochet Knight is an action game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. Controls use dragging.

Ricochet Knight is a bouncy fantasy adventure. Pull back, let go, and the little knight flies across the room, bouncing off walls, rocks and crystals and hitting every monster in his way. Plan each dash: a monster attacks when its counter reaches zero. Fight through 4 lands and 24 stages - green woods, crystal caves, frozen peaks and a dragon's castle - and beat 4 big bosses. Collect gold, buy permanent upgrades, earn stars to unlock new heroes, and climb the Endless Tower.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Rift Fighter, give Ricochet Knight a try: they share the “rpg” tag.

How to play Ricochet Knight

Drag back to aim. The dotted line shows where the knight will bounce.

Let go to dash. Every monster you hit takes damage; fast hits deal more.

The number on a monster shows how many of your dashes are left before it attacks.
