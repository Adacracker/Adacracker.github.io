About Definitely Not a Platformer

Adventure awaits. Definitely Not a Platformer is an adventure game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the arrow keys, the space bar and the mouse.

Definitely Not a Platformer is a puzzle-platforming game where appearances cannot be trusted. Cross 20 handcrafted stages filled with invisible paths, fake platforms, collapsing floors, shifting colors, surprise hazards, misleading signs, and a narrator who may or may not be helping you. Learn each level’s rules, spot the clues, and reach the exit while improving your completion times and death count.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Trap Step and Ambidieztro, give Definitely Not a Platformer a try: they share the “platformer” and “skill” tags.

How to play Definitely Not a Platformer

Move with the Left and Right Arrow keys or A/D. Q/D is also supported for AZERTY keyboards. Jump with Up Arrow, W, Z, Space, or a mouse click. On mobile, use the on-screen Left, Right, and Jump buttons; you can also tap the game area to jump. Press R to restart, M to mute, and Escape or P to return to the menu. Reach the portal at the end of each stage, but do not trust every platform or sign.
