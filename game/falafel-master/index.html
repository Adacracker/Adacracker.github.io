About Falafel Master

Simulation fans, take note: Falafel Master loads straight into this page as a free simulation game. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the mouse and touch.

Falafel Master is a restaurant time-management game where you prepare falafel, bread, tea, and other customer orders before patience runs out. Serve a changing queue, earn coins, unlock restaurant improvements, and keep each day’s kitchen running smoothly.

Why you might like it: Falafel Master has a lot in common with Pizza Now! and Monster Café: Idle Tycoon - they share the “restaurant” and “cook” tags.

How to play Falafel Master

Use the mouse or touch controls to select ingredients, operate kitchen tools, prepare each order, and place finished food on the correct serving area. Watch the customer bubbles and patience bars, then serve before customers leave. Use the pause menu to adjust sound and language.
