About Escape the Giant School 3D

Adventure awaits. Escape the Giant School 3D loads straight into this page as a free adventure game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

Shrink into a giant school and turn everyday classroom objects into an epic 3D parkour course! Jump across oversized books, pencils and desks in 20 themed rooms, collect coins, discover secret rewards and reach checkpoints on your way to freedom. Earn stars, unlock achievements and customize your tiny hero with outfits, wings and effects. Free checkpoints and saved progress let you keep improving at your own pace. Play with keyboard and mouse or use the on-screen mobile controls.

Why you might like it: Escape the Giant School 3D has a lot in common with Daddy Mouse and Obby: Death Run - they share the “platform” and “jumping” tags.

How to play Escape the Giant School 3D

Desktop WASD or arrows to move Space to jump drag mouse to rotate camera P or Esc to pause Mobile joystick to move jump button to leap swipe to look Collect coins and follow platforms through checkpoints to the exit.
