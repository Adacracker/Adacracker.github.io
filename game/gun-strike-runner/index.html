About Gun Strike Runner

Got a minute? Gun Strike Runner is a free arcade game that runs right in your browser. It runs on desktop and mobile alike. It is played with the arrow keys, W A S D and the mouse. On a phone, turn it sideways for the wide landscape screen.

Gun Strike Runner is an action-packed game that blends fast-paced shooting with weapon evolution. Dash through levels, eliminate obstacles, and take down enemies using a growing arsenal of guns. Merge gun pieces to enhance your firepower, allowing you to tackle increasingly difficult challenges. Test your aim and reflexes in this nonstop shooting adventure.

Why you might like it: Fans of Crazy Bus Escape Runner and Ragdoll Rush 3D: Sky Race may like this one too, since it shares their “obstacle” tag.

Use WASD mouse or arrow keys to move.
