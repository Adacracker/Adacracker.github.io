About Scribble Slash

Ready for some action? You can play Scribble Slash, an action game from our Action category, free in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the mouse, swipes and taps.

Your pen is your weapon! Draw on the notebook page to slash, lasso and zap waves of scribbled monsters. Every shape does something different: slash through monsters, draw a circle to lasso a whole group, scribble a zigzag for lightning, a spiral for a tornado, a triangle for a shield, or tap for an ink bomb. Pick power cards as you level up, beat giant bosses like the Paint Golem, the Ink Dragon and the Spider Queen, and travel through new worlds. How long can you survive the scribble storm?

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Voxhalla and Last Ring, give Scribble Slash a try: they share the “survival” tag.

How to play Scribble Slash

Hold and drag (mouse or finger) to draw. Swipe through monsters to slash them. Draw a circle to lasso, a zigzag to zap, a spiral for a tornado, a triangle for a shield, and tap for an ink bomb. Press P to pause.
