About Up Hero

Adventure awaits. Up Hero is a free adventure game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the arrow keys, W A S D and dragging.

Ready to challenge endless hordes of monsters? Up Hero is an action-packed RPG where you level up and power up your character right on the battlefield. Wipe out waves of enemies and prove that you are the ultimate hero!

What awaits you in Up Hero:

Horde Battles: Fight your way through crowds of monsters!

Vast Arsenal & Epic Skills: Mix and match swords, firearms, and magic!

Dangerous Worlds: Explore unique locations, each with its own monsters and bosses!

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Ligmar Online and Mask of six Moons, give Up Hero a try: they share the “rpg” and “idle” tags.

How to play Up Hero

Controls: On PC, move your hero using WASD or arrow keys. On mobile, simply drag your finger anywhere on the screen (virtual joystick).

Auto-Combat: No need to mash attack buttons! Your character automatically strikes monsters as soon as they get close. Just maneuver through the crowd and dodge threats.

Gather Resources & Cargo: Explore locations, chop wood, mine stone, deliver valuable cargo, and collect gold dropped by defeated enemies.
