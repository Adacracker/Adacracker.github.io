About Jelly Mob Rush

Jelly Mob Rush is an arcade game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the arrow keys and dragging.

Lead a wobbly mob of jelly people down a candy lane and pick the gates that grow your crowd. Squeeze past pizza cutters and sweepers, beat the red jelly army, then climb the macaron staircase for a coin multiplier. Four candy worlds, four jelly king bosses, upgrades, jelly skins and hats.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Slime Buffet and Dog Runaway, give Jelly Mob Rush a try: they share the “casual” and “cute” tags.

How to play Jelly Mob Rush

Drag left and right anywhere to steer Pick gates that add or multiply jellies dodge the kitchen tools and beat the red army at the end Controls - Drag A D arrows steer the jelly crowd - Drag anywhere start the run from the start screen.
