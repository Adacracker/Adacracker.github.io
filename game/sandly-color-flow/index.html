About Sandly: Color Flow

Fancy a brain-teaser? Sandly: Color Flow is a puzzle game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the space bar, the mouse and taps.

Turn colorful sand pictures into a satisfying sorting puzzle! Send matching buckets along the conveyor and collect falling sand, one color at a time. Plan your moves carefully: space on the belt is limited, and the next bucket can make all the difference. Clear every grain to finish the picture, discover new designs, and enjoy a relaxing mix of color, motion, and strategy.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Ant Moving Simulator and Candy Sort, give Sandly: Color Flow a try: they share the “colormatch” and “relaxing” tags.

How to play Sandly: Color Flow

Click or tap a front bucket to send it onto the conveyor. Buckets collect sand of their own color and leave when full. Clear all the sand to win. Plan your moves to avoid blocking every slot. Shuffle rearranges queues; Speed toggles 1x/2x. Optional rewarded ads add slots, skip a level, or let you continue.
