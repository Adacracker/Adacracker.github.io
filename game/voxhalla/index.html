About Voxhalla

Fast hands help here. Voxhalla is an action game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. Controls use W A S D, the space bar and the mouse.

Voxhalla is a 3D voxel survivors-like roguelite. Drop onto a blocky island, fight off ever-growing hordes of monsters, collect XP and level up to pick new weapons and upgrades. Your weapons attack automatically - you focus on moving, jumping and dashing to stay alive. Choose your survivor, each with a unique starting weapon and signature ability, earn silver after every run and unlock permanent upgrades and new characters. How long can you survive?

Why you might like it: Voxhalla has a lot in common with Last Ring and Office Knight 3D: Castle Defense - they share the “survival” and “monster” tags.

How to play Voxhalla

WASD - move, Mouse - look around, Space - jump, Shift - dash, Q - signature ability, C - slide, Esc - pause. Weapons attack automatically. Number keys or mouse to pick level-up upgrades.
