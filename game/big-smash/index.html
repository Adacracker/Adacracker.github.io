About Big Smash

Game on! You can play Big Smash, a sports game from our Sports category, free in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the mouse, taps and dragging.

Aim your tennis balls at a tower of cubes on the table and knock the whole lot down. Stone, wood, ice, jelly... every.

Cube falls differently!

Why you might like it: Big Smash has a lot in common with Mini Golf 3D - Island Course and Sky Golfer - they share the “physics” tag.

How to play Big Smash

Objective.

Bring down the tower.

CONTROLS.

Computer:

Click and hold to pull back the cannon, aim by dragging, and release to fire.

Phone or tablet:

Tap and drag to pull back and aim, then lift your finger to fire.
