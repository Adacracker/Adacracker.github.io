About Brick Out Candy

Quick fun ahead. Brick Out Candy is a free arcade game that runs right in your browser. It runs on desktop and mobile alike.

Enter a world of vibrant fun in Brick Out Candy! Bounce your ball to shatter candy bricks in this bright arcade challenge. Master precise shots, gather power-ups, and aim high as you keep the ball alive, scoring against increasingly tricky levels. An irresistible sugar-coated adventure awaits!
