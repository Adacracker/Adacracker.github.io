About Christmas sorting

Fancy a brain-teaser? Christmas sorting is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer.

Christmas Sorting is a relaxing game for all ages. Place objects next to each other to collect 3 identical objects in a row.

Collect all the unique decorations for a Christmas atmosphere.

Create Christmas coziness, decorate the Christmas tree the way you want.

Why you might like it: Christmas sorting has a lot in common with Atomic Puzzle XMas and Christmas Blocks Sort - they share the “logic” and “sorting” tags.

How to play Christmas sorting

Place objects along the portals, connect three identical objects in a row to make them disappear.

To complete the level, you must clear all portals of objects.

As you earn points, you can unlock more different avatars, portals, and backgrounds.

Use a magic wand to remove paired items.

Use a magnifying glass to highlight identical objects.

As you complete levels, get Christmas tree decorations, decorate the Christmas tree and watch how other players do it.
