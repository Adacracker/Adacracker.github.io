About Pocket Golf

Warm up first: Pocket Golf loads straight into this page as a free sports game. It works on phones, tablets and computers. It is played with dragging. On a phone, turn it sideways for the wide landscape screen.

Pocket Golf is a hand-drawn minigolf game sketched on notebook paper. Aim, choose the right power and sink your ball in as few strokes as possible across dozens of doodled courses full of pencils, erasers and surprises.

Why you might like it: Pocket Golf has a lot in common with PocketGolf and Golf Orbit - they share the “golf” and “hypercasual” tags.

How to play Pocket Golf

Drag to aim release to shoot the ball.
