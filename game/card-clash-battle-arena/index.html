About Card Clash : Battle Arena

Card Clash : Battle Arena is a free action game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it.

Card Clash: Battle Arena is an exciting card-merge battle game where strategy and timing decide victory!

Merge cards of the same color to create powerful characters.

After merging 5 identical cards, a unique hero is summoned to the battlefield. These heroes automatically fight enemies, pushing forward step by step.

Defeat all enemies to advance, then lead your characters to destroy the enemy base and complete the level!

Why you might like it: Fans of One More and Pick Brainrot: 3D Battle may like this one too, since it shares their “arena” and “arcade” tags.

How to play Card Clash : Battle Arena

Merge 5 cards of the same color.

Spawn powerful characters.

Watch heroes battle enemies automatically.

Clear all enemies to move forward.

Destroy the enemy home to finish the level.
