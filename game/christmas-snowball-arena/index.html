About Christmas Snowball Arena

Jump into the arena. Christmas Snowball Arena is a free .io game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with swipes and dragging.

Christmas Snowball Arena is a 3D stickman snowball IO game. You have to compete with nine other players in collecting snow blocks and making your snowball bigger. The goal is to be the last player standing by defeating and collecting other snowballs. You can also collect blank snowballs that are free for anyone. You can use two power-ups in the game to boost your snowball: one that makes it twice as big and another that makes it twice as fast. If you are looking for some snowball action, try it!

Why you might like it: Fans of Santa Christmas Delivery and Hit The Christmas Elves may like this one too, since it shares their “christmas” and “snowball” tags.

How to play Christmas Snowball Arena

Drag and swipe to control your snowball.
