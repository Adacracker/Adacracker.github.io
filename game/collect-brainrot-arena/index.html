About Collect Brainrot Arena

Got a minute? Collect Brainrot Arena loads straight into this page as a free arcade game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the arrow keys, the space bar and the mouse. It can also be played by two people on one device.

Collect Brainrot Arena is a chaotic multiplayer collecting and stealing game where luck, strategy, and timing are equally important. Enter the arena, gather valuable Lucky Blocks, unlock rare Brainrots, upgrade your collection, and protect your base from rival thieves. Every Lucky Block can contain a surprise, and every opponent is looking for an opportunity to steal your most valuable Brainrots.
