About CatDoku

Puzzle time. CatDoku is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the mouse and taps.

Cat Doku is a cozy cat-themed logic puzzle game. Use the clues on the board to place cats in the correct positions: one cat per color, one cat per row and column, and no adjacent cats. Solve handcrafted levels, unlock Daily Challenge and Survival Mode, and use hints or revives when you get stuck. It’s easy to learn, relaxing to play, and perfect for short puzzle sessions.

Why you might like it: Fans of Cat arrows: Cozy Puzzle and Catculus may like this one too, since it shares their “cat” and “logic” tags.

How to play CatDoku

How to Play.

Find and match the hidden cat pairs on the board.

Tap a tile to mark it, and use the clues at the top to decide where cats can or cannot be placed. Each row, column, and color area has rules that help you find the correct pairs.

Clear the level by finding all required cats without running out of chances.

Controls.

Tap / Click: Select or mark a tile.

Hint Button: Reveal help when you are stuck.

Clear Button: Remove your current marks.

Restart: Start the level again.
