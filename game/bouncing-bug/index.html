About Bouncing Bug

Quick fun ahead. Bouncing Bug is a free arcade game that runs right in your browser. It runs on desktop and mobile alike.

Help a quirky bug navigate its way through a bouncy box filled with lurking monsters. Skillfully change its direction to dodge threats while snatching shiny treasures and tasty treats to survive. Test your reflexes in this dynamic, gravity-defying adventure and unlock a variety of bug companions.

Why you might like it: Fans of Going Right and Dog Runaway may like this one too, since it shares their “animal” tag.

Bouncing Bug: FAQ

Does Bouncing Bug cost anything?

No, it costs nothing. Bouncing Bug is free to play in your browser on Arcade Cabin. It is supplied by GamePix, and the provider may show ads before or during the game.

Can I play Bouncing Bug on my phone?

Yes. GamePix lists Bouncing Bug as mobile-friendly, so it works with touch on phones and tablets as well as on computers. It uses a wide landscape screen.

Do I need to download Bouncing Bug?

No. Bouncing Bug runs in your browser right on this page - press Play to start. There is nothing to install and no account to create.
