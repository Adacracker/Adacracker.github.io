About Pirate Dodge

Quick fun ahead. Pirate Dodge loads straight into this page as a free arcade game. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the arrow keys, the space bar and swipes.

The river is narrow, your boat is tiny, and every pirate on the water wants it gone. Slip between lanes to dodge rocks and kraken tentacles, and keep an eye on the enemy ships: some shoot, some cut across your path. Fire back when you can. Sunk pirates drop coins and pieces of an old map, and coins buy you a faster, meaner ship.

Why you might like it: Fans of I Get You and DarkHood may like this one too, since it shares their “arcade” tag.

How to play Pirate Dodge

PC Left Right arrows or A D to change lanes Space to fire P to pause Mobile tap the left or right side of the screen or swipe to change lanes tap Fire to shoot.
