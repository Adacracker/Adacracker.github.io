About Vector Strike

Vector Strike loads straight into this page as a free shooting game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

Pilot your fighter through 10 neon wireframe sectors and take on 10 giant bosses. Dodge enemy fire, collect upgrades and build your arsenal with rear shots, missiles, shields and split fire. Break each boss’s weak points to expose its core, then finish it off. VECTOR STRIKE keeps the action fast, with short stages and autofire you can switch off for manual control.
