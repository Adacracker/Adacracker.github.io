About Shoot the Bubbles

Fancy a brain-teaser? Shoot the Bubbles is a puzzle game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the mouse, taps and dragging.

Aim, shoot, and match 3+ bubbles of the same color! Take on hundreds of levels in Adventure mode or see how long you can last in endless Challenge Mode. Special bubbles and explosive combos await!

Why you might like it: Fans of Jewel Christmas Mania and Xmas Match 3 Dare may like this one too, since it shares their “matching” and “bubble” tags.

How to play Shoot the Bubbles

Desktop: Move the mouse to aim, then left-click to shoot. Use the walls to bounce shots and reach difficult groups.

Mobile: Drag your finger to aim, then release or tap to shoot. Use the walls for angled bounce shots.
