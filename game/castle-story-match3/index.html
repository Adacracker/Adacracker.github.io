About Castle Story Match3!

Fancy a brain-teaser? Castle Story Match3! is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer.

Dream Castle is an exciting match-3 puzzle game where you help a young princess bring her ruined castle back to its former glory. Game features: More than 100 exciting levels with gradually increasing difficulty. A magical transformation of the castle: walls, floors, and furniture are restored right before your eyes. An engaging plot with dialogues and new characters. Cleaning and interior design mechanics: choose a style, arrange the furniture, create coziness. Daily bonuses.

Why you might like it: Fans of Color Coin Merge and PushPlush may like this one too, since it shares their “match 3” and “match-3” tags.
