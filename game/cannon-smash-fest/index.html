About Cannon Smash Fest

You can play Cannon Smash Fest, an action game from our Action category, free in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the mouse and dragging.

Welcome to Cannon Smash Fest — a fun physics-based destruction game where every cannon shot creates a satisfying chain reaction!

Load your cannon, adjust the perfect angle and power, and blast through challenging levels. Destroy fragile jars, break heavy stone blocks, and knock down massive structures with realistic physics. Plan each shot carefully to clear every obstacle using as few cannonballs as possible.

Easy to learn, fun to master, and packed with satisfying destruction!
