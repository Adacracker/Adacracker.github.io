About Five Banners Online

Five Banners Online loads straight into this page as a free action game. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

Five Banners Online is a fantasy action game with survival, crafting and multiplayer adventures. Explore a floating island, gather resources and fight monsters, then reach the safe zone to turn your haul into supplies. Other players might help you in a fight — or knock loot out of your backpack. When the island begins falling into the abyss, every detour becomes a gamble.

The island changes each cycle. Your base and gear stay with you.

Why you might like it: Fans of Super Chop: Forest Hero and Office Knight 3D: Castle Defense may like this one too, since it shares their “monster” and “survival” tags.

How to play Five Banners Online

Head through the gates, gather wood, stone and ore, and fight monsters. Return to the safe zone to move your backpack's contents into storage automatically. Build workshops and craft equipment to venture farther on your next run.

On a computer:

WASD or arrow keys — move; mouse — aim; left click — attack and gather.

Space — dash; Q, E, R — weapon, armor and amulet skills.

// — select a weapon; F — interact.

G or — first potion; / — other potions; Tab — cycle statistics.
