About Drifty Road Rush

Drifty Road Rush is a free driving game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the arrow keys, the mouse and touch.

Speed along twisting roads where every corner demands a perfect drift. Guide your car through traffic, collect rewards, avoid crashes, and keep your run alive as the challenge grows. Learn the rhythm of each turn, unlock new vehicles, and chase huge scores in this fast, addictive arcade driving adventure today.
