About Void Tapper Clicker

VoidTapper is a dark fantasy incremental clicker where every tap strikes a creature from the void. Target moving weak points, build powerful combos, unleash Overdrive, and upgrade your Power, Drones, and Profit to overcome increasingly dangerous enemies. Fight through five unique worlds, choose skills that shape four different combat builds, defeat timed bosses, unlock achievements, and Prestige for permanent Star Dust upgrades and higher difficulty modes.

How to play

Tap or click the enemy in the center of the screen to attack. Strike moving weak points for bonus damage, maintain your combo, and charge the Overdrive meter for a temporary power boost.

Use earned coins to improve your tap Power, unlock automatic Drones, and increase your Profit. Choose new skills as you progress and combine them into Critical, Combo, Drone, or Overdrive builds.
