About Wobble It

Fancy a brain-teaser? Wobble It is a puzzle game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the space bar, taps and touch.

WOBBLE IT is a 3D rolling-ball puzzle platformer. Guide a beach ball across floating block worlds where the ball sticks to every side of a block, so walls become floors as you roll over an edge. Collect every key to open the exit before the hourglass runs out, grab coins and gems, and avoid spikes, crumbling tiles and moving blocks. 150 handcrafted levels across 10 themed worlds, plus 30 bonus and 10 hidden levels, with a star rating for every stage. Spend coins on TIME UP upgrades and unlock 10 elemental ball skins (fire, water, ice, cosmos and more) with glowing trails.

Why you might like it: Wobble It has a lot in common with Rope Chef and Draw the RailBall Physics - they share the “brain” and “puzzle” tags.

How to play Wobble It

Keyboard W Up roll A D or Left Right turn Space hop E jump forward Esc pause R restart Touch hold the lower screen to roll tap upper-left upper-right to turn on-screen buttons to hop and jump.
