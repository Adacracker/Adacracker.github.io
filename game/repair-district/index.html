About Repair District

Simulation fans, take note: Repair District loads straight into this page as a free simulation game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the mouse, taps and dragging.

Bring a neighbourhood back to life, one repair at a time! Diagnose faulty appliances, use hands-on tools and manage your own repair workshop. Help local businesses reopen, hire staff and upgrade your workspace as you work towards a neighbourhood street festival.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Ink Shop: Dress & Tattoo and Night Lake, give Repair District a try: they share the “relaxing” and “relax” tags.

How to play Repair District

Click or tap to select jobs, tools and parts. Follow the repair hints, then click, drag or turn components to fix each device. Test your repairs and return completed jobs to earn money. Buy supplies, upgrade your workshop and help local shops reopen. Your progress saves automatically in your browser.
