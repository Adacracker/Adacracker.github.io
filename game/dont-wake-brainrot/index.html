About Don't Wake Brainrot

Don't Wake Brainrot loads straight into this page as a free adventure game. You can play it on a computer or a touch screen.

Tiptoe through a thrilling stealth adventure, snatching elusive brainrots as they slumber. But beware—one false step and they'll give chase! Can you keep your cool and escape with your prize? Every encounter is a pulse-pounding escape from cunning brainrot creatures.
