About Road Fury - Racing

Road Fury - Racing is a free driving game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use W A S D, swipes and taps.

Dive into dynamic 3D racing where your main goal is to reach the finish line first. The track is full of rivals, dangerous obstacles, traps, ramps, and sharp turns that will test your reaction speed and driving skills.

Every collision damages your car: the body breaks, wheels can fly off, and handling becomes harder. Use nitro, avoid hazards, upgrade your vehicle, and buy new cars to become the race leader.

Why you might like it: Fans of Hustle & Drift in ZIL and Crazy Driver may like this one too, since it shares their “car” and “speed” tags.

How to play Road Fury - Racing

Goal:

Reach the finish line first while overtaking rivals, avoiding obstacles, and escaping traps. Avoid collisions, use turbo, and upgrade your car to win the race.

Controls:

Mobile devices: use touch and swipes to control the car.

PC: WASD — control the car, double-tap W — turbo.
