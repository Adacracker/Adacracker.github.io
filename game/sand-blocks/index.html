About Sand Blocks

Sand Blocks is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. It runs on desktop and mobile alike. You play it with the space bar, the mouse and dragging. On a phone, turn it sideways for the wide landscape screen.

Drop colorful block shapes and watch them break into flowing sand. Match the same sand colors to complete full lines and clear space on the grid. Every block behaves differently as the sand falls, stacks, and settles naturally. How to Play You are given three sand block shapes at a time. Place each block one by one anywhere on the grid, in any order. When a block is placed, it breaks into sand and flows naturally based on its shape. Complete a full horizontal line of the same sand color to clear it. The game ends when sand rises past the limit line, so keep the grid under control and plan ahead.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Ants Clear All : Color Block Puzzle and TideRoll: Block Puzzle, give Sand Blocks a try: they share the “logic” and “brain” tags.
