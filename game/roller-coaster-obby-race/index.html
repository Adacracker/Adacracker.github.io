About Roller Coaster: Obby Race

Adventure awaits. Roller Coaster: Obby Race is an adventure game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with W A S D, the space bar and swipes.

Race along narrow rails where every turn can be your last. Speed, reaction, and precise minecart control matter here: accelerate, brake, and pass dangerous sections without falling.

Tracks become harder, obstacles get trickier, and each run becomes more intense. Unlock new minecarts, try different routes, and prove you can complete every challenge. Dangerous tracks with sharp turns await you.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Steal Animal Eggs Online and +1 Speed Keyboard Lucky Escape, give Roller Coaster: Obby Race a try: they share the “roblox” and “control” tags.

How to play Roller Coaster: Obby Race

The goal of the game is to reach the finish, stay on the rails, and complete all dangerous sections. Control your speed, jump in time, interact with objects, and keep your balance on turns.

Controls: on desktop, use WASD to move, Space to jump, E to interact, and LMB to control the camera. On mobile, use touch and swipe.
