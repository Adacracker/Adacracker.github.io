About Left Dead: Last Convoy

Left Dead: Last Convoy is a free shooting game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the arrow keys, the space bar and the mouse.

Help medic Tess keep Omar and the convoy alive across 23 stages in five campaign routes. Fight through city streets, carnival grounds, bayou paths, a storm and the final bridge. Aim your rifle, reload between threats, shove infected away and use medical supplies wisely. Rescue survivors and secure fuel while checkpoints preserve the journey.
