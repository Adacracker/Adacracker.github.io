About Paint or Hunt

Paint or Hunt delivers a colorful, Meccha-inspired twist on hide-and-seek. Play as one of the chameleons, sample nearby colors, paint your character, and choose from 17 poses to disappear into the scenery. As a Hunter, follow whistle clues and inspect suspicious areas before time runs out. Customize the rules, explore seven unique maps, and play online or with a friend in local 2 Player Mode.

How to play

Move: W, A, S, D or Arrow Keys.

Aim: Mouse - (Hunters)

Shoot: Left-Click - (Hunters)

Run: Left Shift.

Jump: Space.

Choose Pose: R - (Hiders)

Open Paint Panel: V.

Eyedropper / Select Color: B + Left-Click.

Adjust Brush Size: Alt + Mouse Scroll.

Taunt: T - (Hiders)

Fly Mode: F.

Rotation Lock: X - (Hiders)

Free Cam: C - (Hiders)

Pause: P.
