About Paint or Hunt

Lock and load: Paint or Hunt is a shooting game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar. It can also be played by two people on one device.

Paint or Hunt delivers a colorful, Meccha-inspired twist on hide-and-seek. Play as one of the chameleons, sample nearby colors, paint your character, and choose from 17 poses to disappear into the scenery. As a Hunter, follow whistle clues and inspect suspicious areas before time runs out. Customize the rules, explore seven unique maps, and play online or with a friend in local 2 Player Mode.

Why you might like it: Fans of Tower Smash: Can Blaster and Red and Green Christmas may like this one too, since it shares their “2players” and “multiplayer” tags.
