About Bean Party Obby

Pack your bags: Bean Party Obby is a free adventure game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

Bean Party Obby is a colorful 3D party game where you race, jump and survive against 14 computer-controlled beans. Pick one of 6 bean characters and a hat, then choose one of 5 modes. Three obby courses: Rainbow Road (easy), High Peaks (ramps, bridge, trampoline, spinning beams) and Turbo Galaxy (fast roads, big jumps, sliding platforms). Hexagon Survival: four layers of hexagons vanish under your feet, be the last bean standing. Color Block: run to the announced color before the other tiles fall, for 12 rounds. Double jump, speed boosts and checkpoints help you. Plays on desktop and mobile.

Why you might like it: Fans of Obby: Three Challenges and Obby: Death Run may like this one too, since it shares their “platform” and “jumping” tags.

How to play Bean Party Obby

Desktop WASD or arrow keys to move Space to jump press again in the air to double jump mouse to look around R to return to the last checkpoint P to pause M to toggle music Mobile left joystick to move JUMP button to jump tap again in the air t.
