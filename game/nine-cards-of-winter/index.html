About Nine Cards Of Winter

Puzzle time. Nine Cards Of Winter is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the mouse, taps and touch.

Get into the holiday spirit with Nine Cards of Winter, a fun and relaxing tile-matching game!

Pick up to 9 Christmas tiles and match 3 of the same to clear them. Watch out — if your stack fills up, the game is over!

Use smart moves to uncover hidden tiles, solve tricky puzzles, and enjoy cute holiday designs.It’s easy to play, relaxing to enjoy, and full of winter charm!

Why you might like it: Nine Cards Of Winter has a lot in common with Jewel Christmas Mania and Christmas Grab Match 3 - they share the “winter” and “matching” tags.

How to play Nine Cards Of Winter

Use your mouse or touchpad to tap tiles and match three of the same to clear them.
