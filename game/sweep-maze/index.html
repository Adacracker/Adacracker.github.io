About Sweep Maze

Puzzle time. You can play Sweep Maze, a puzzle game from our Puzzle category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

Sweep Maze is a relaxing puzzle game where you guide the ball across the board and paint every open tile. Plan each move carefully, avoid blocked spaces, and complete the entire maze to finish the level. With procedurally generated boards and multiple difficulty levels, every puzzle offers a different challenge.

Why you might like it: Sweep Maze has a lot in common with Rewind House and Bend the Dawn: Light Bending Puzzle - they share the “logic” and “relaxing” tags.

How to play Sweep Maze

Desktop: Use the Arrow Keys or WASD to move the ball.

Mobile: Swipe in the direction you want the ball to move.

Cover every available tile to complete the level. If you get stuck, use the hint feature to help find the next move.
