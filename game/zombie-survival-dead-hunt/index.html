About Zombie Survival: Dead Hunt

Ready for some action? Zombie Survival: Dead Hunt is a free action game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. You play it with W A S D, the space bar and the mouse.

Survive the zombie apocalypse in Zombie Survival: Dead Hunt! Fight waves of deadly undead enemies, aim carefully, shoot fast, and stay alive as long as possible. Become the ultimate zombie killer and test your shooting, reaction, and survival skills. Keep moving, avoid getting surrounded, eliminate zombies, and beat your highest score. If you love zombie games, zombie shooters, zombie killing games, survival games, and action games, the undead are waiting for you!

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Playdough School and Super Hero Survivor Io, give Zombie Survival: Dead Hunt a try: they share the “zombie” and “shoot-em-up” tags.

How to play Zombie Survival: Dead Hunt

Move through the danger zone and fight approaching zombies. Use WASD to move, mouse to aim, and Left Mouse Button to shoot. Press R to reload and Space to jump. Keep moving so zombies cannot surround you. Aim for approaching enemies, eliminate as many undead as possible, and survive for as long as you can. Your goal is simple: kill zombies, stay alive, and achieve the highest survival score!
