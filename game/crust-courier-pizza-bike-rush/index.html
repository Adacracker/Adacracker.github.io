About Crust Courier: Pizza Bike Rush

Start your engine. Crust Courier: Pizza Bike Rush loads straight into this page as a free driving game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the arrow keys and the space bar.

Hop on your delivery bike and race the clock in Crust Courier: Pizza Bike Rush! Pick up hot orders, follow the route on your mini-map and get every pizza to the customers door before time runs out. Dodge busy traffic and ride carefully, because every crash damages the food and a ruined order means a failed mission. Finish fast with the food in good shape to earn 3 stars, bigger tips and more coins. Can you complete all 20 levels and become the citys fastest courier?

Why you might like it: Crust Courier: Pizza Bike Rush has a lot in common with Highway Traffic Racer 3D and Magline Moto - they share the “bike” tag.
