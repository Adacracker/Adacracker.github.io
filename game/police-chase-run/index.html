About Police Chase Run

Buckle up: Police Chase Run loads straight into this page as a free driving game. It works on phones, tablets and computers. On a phone, hold it upright: the game is made for portrait mode.

Take the wheel and try to survive as long as possible in a tense police chase. The car keeps moving forward, while you steer at the right moment, avoid police cars, and collect coins. The longer the run, the higher the score. Unlock new cars, improve your result, and compete for a place on the leaderboard. The game is made for one player and works well for quick arcade sessions.

Why you might like it: Fans of Race Through Traffic and Need For Drift may like this one too, since it shares their “car” and “racing” tags.

How to play Police Chase Run
