About Hexa Honey Sort

Hexa Honey Sort is a puzzle game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the space bar and dragging.

Enter a cheerful honey garden in Hexa Honey Sort! Place colorful tile stacks on the honeycomb, connect matching top tiles, and collect groups of 10 or more. Plan each move carefully, trigger satisfying chain reactions, complete collection goals, and keep the hive from running out of space across 90 relaxing puzzle levels.

Why you might like it: Fans of Sandly: Color Flow and Ant Moving Simulator may like this one too, since it shares their “colormatch” and “sorting” tags.

How to play Hexa Honey Sort

Drag one of the three available stacks onto an empty honeycomb cell. Matching top tiles on neighboring stacks automatically move together. Collect 10 or more identical tiles to clear them and fill the level goal. Place all three stacks to receive a new batch. Complete the target before every board cell becomes occupied. Use boosters when needed.
