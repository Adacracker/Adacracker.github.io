About Snack Stack

Snack Stack is a relaxing tile-matching puzzle set in a colorful Asian food market. Pick tasty food tiles from the board, collect matching snacks, and complete each level’s goal. Plan your moves carefully as the board gradually opens up, and enjoy satisfying matches with fish, steak, corn, eggs, crab, and more.

How to play

Tap an available food tile to move it into the tray at the bottom. Collect 3 identical tiles to match and remove them from the tray. Keep matching tiles to reach the level goal and clear the required items. Be careful — the tray has limited space, and if it fills up before you can make a match, you lose. Use Undo or Shuffle when you get stuck.
