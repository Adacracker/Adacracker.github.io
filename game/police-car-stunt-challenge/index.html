About Police Car Stunt Challenge

Start your engine. Police Car Stunt Challenge is a free driving game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the arrow keys, the space bar and touch.

Drive powerful police cars across extreme sky tracks filled with ramps, loops, rotating barriers, narrow platforms, and huge jumps. Choose your patrol vehicle, complete challenging stunt levels, collect coins, and master every obstacle. Use precise steering, braking, nitro, and camera changes to become the ultimate police stunt champion today online.

Why you might like it: Police Car Stunt Challenge has a lot in common with Extreme Gear Shift Racing and Extreme Quad Bike Racing - they share the “driving” and “racing” tags.

How to play Police Car Stunt Challenge

Use W or Up Arrow to accelerate Use S or Down Arrow to brake and reverse Steer with A D or Left Right Arrow Press Space for the handbrake either Shift key for nitro C to change the camera and Escape to pause Touch controls remain available on mobil.
