About Obby Yard Sale

Adventure awaits. Obby Yard Sale is an adventure game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use W A S D.

Open your own trade right in the yard of your house! Order boxes with mysterious goods, unpack them and put them up for sale to passers-by. Each item can be common or rare and legendary, which makes every purchase special.

Keep an eye on your yard: buyers not only buy goods, but also try to steal them. Your task is to stop the thief in time and collect a collection of all types of items, turning simple yard trading into an exciting adventure.

Why you might like it: Obby Yard Sale has a lot in common with Obby Escape from Tsunami Brainrot and Roller Coaster: Obby Race - they share the “roblox” tag.

How to play Obby Yard Sale

The goal of the game is to promote your business and collect a complete collection of all types of goods.

You start with regular boxes, gradually open rare and legendary items, protect them from thieves and earn more and more to develop the establishment.

Controls: WASD to walk, E to interact.
