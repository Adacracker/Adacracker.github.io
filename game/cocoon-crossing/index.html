About Cocoon Crossing

Cocoon Crossing 3D is a relaxing 3D routing puzzle. Drag the head or tail of each caterpillar and it crawls, tile by tile, toward the cocoon of its own color — reach it and it turns into a butterfly. Worms can't cross on the same level, but at a crossing they can climb over a bridge or slip under a tunnel. Solve each board within the move budget, chase the par star, and untangle your way through dozens of handcrafted levels.

How to play

Drag the head or tail of a caterpillar to make it crawl one tile at a time toward its matching cocoon. Two worms can't overlap on the same level — at a crossing tile, send one over a bridge and the other under a tunnel. Get every worm home (they turn into butterflies) to win. Finish within the move budget; match par for a star. Use Undo to take back a move, or Hint when you're stuck.
