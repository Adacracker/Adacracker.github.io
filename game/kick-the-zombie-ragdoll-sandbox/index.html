About Kick the Zombie: Ragdoll Sandbox

Kick the Zombie is a goofy ragdoll physics toy set in a moonlit graveyard. Meet Gus, a polite cartoon zombie who is happy to be dragged, flung, kicked and bonked. Grab him and throw him into bouncy headstones and walls, boot him with a giant sneaker, smack him with a frying pan, freeze him, zap him, blast him from a pumpkin cannon or tie balloons to him and watch him float away. His head pops off and hops right back, so it is all slapstick fun.

How to play

Mouse or touch drag Gus to grab and fling him Tap or click to use the selected tool kick smack zap freeze Tap the toolbar to switch tools and tap the door to change rooms No keyboard needed.
