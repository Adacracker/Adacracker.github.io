About Hook A Brainrot

Hook A Brainrot is a collection and survival game where every throw of your hook can bring you a new Brainrot, but catching one is only the beginning. The real challenge starts when a giant tsunami appears behind your catch!

Throw your hook into the field, pull a random Brainrot out of the ground, and quickly drag it toward the Safe Zone before the wave reaches it. Bring your catch back to your Base, add it to your collection, and let your Brainrots continuously generate money for you.

How to play

Player 1 Controls.

W, A, S, D: Move.

TAB or E: Throw Hook.

S: Pull Hook.

F: Train Muscle.

Shift: Speed Up.

E: Interact.

Player 2 Controls.

Arrow Keys: Move.

U: Throw Hook.

Down Arrow: Pull Hook.

Right Shift: Train Muscle.

K: Speed Up.

O: Interact.
