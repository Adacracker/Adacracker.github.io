About Golden Fish

Adventure awaits. Golden Fish is an adventure game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the mouse and touch.

Dive into an addictive underwater adventure in Golden Fish! Control your fishs jump, choose the right power, and land safely in the next aquarium. Every jump is a test of timing, precision, and skill — go too far and youll miss, but dont jump far enough and you wont make it! Explore colorful underwater worlds, discover new aquariums, and see how far you can go. Can you master the perfect jump?
