About Break a Lucky Egg Brainrots

Break a Lucky Egg Brainrots is a fast and rewarding collection game where action, risk, and strategy come together. Your mission is to break brainrot eggs, collect rare Brainrot characters, and safely deliver them to your base before time runs out. Each run is a mix of decision making and reflex. That income allows you to unlock stronger pickaxes and hammers, break higher tier eggs, and expand your base up to 3 floors. With full upgrades, you can store up to many Brainrots and build a strong passive economy. The game supports both 1 player and 2 player modes.

How to play

Player 1 Move W A S D Run Left Shift Hit F Pick Up or Sell E Jump Space Player 2 Move Arrow Keys Run K Hit J Pick Up or Sell U Jump L.
