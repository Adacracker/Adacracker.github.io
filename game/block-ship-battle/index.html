About Block Ship Battle

Block Ship Battle is a fast pirate brawler with real broadside combat. Your cannons fire to the side, so every fight is a dance of turning, timing and reloading until your guns bear on the enemy. Sink rival ships, scoop up floating treasure and bank your gold in a harbour before someone sinks you. Choose from six ships and captains with abilities and ultimates, unlock visible upgrades every level and brave storms, fog and lightning in five game modes.

How to play

W/S raise and lower sail, A/D steer, mouse to look. Hold right mouse to aim your broadside, left mouse to fire. X switches broadside, Shift/E use abilities, Q the ultimate (from level 5), Tab scoreboard, Esc pause. Touch: left stick to sail and steer, drag on the right to look, hold AIM and tap FIRE. Gamepad: left stick sail, right stick look, L2 aim, R2 fire. Turn until an enemy is beside you, then fire. Bank gold in a harbour to win.
