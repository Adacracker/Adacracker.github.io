About Hexa Stack Christmas

Hexa Stack Christmas is a puzzle game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. You play it with taps and dragging.

Get into the holiday spirit with Hexa Stack Christmas: a festive twist on the classic hexagon-stacking puzzle game. Place the Christmas-themed hexagon tiles stacks onto the board and sort the same color tiles to collect them. The game combines a light puzzle that requires attention and logic — perfect for relaxing while stimulating your brain. 2 game modes, infinite levels, a user-friendly interface, and save support let you play anytime without losing progress.

Why you might like it: Hexa Stack Christmas has a lot in common with Christmas Puzzle and Jewel Christmas Mania - they share the “christmas” tag.

How to play Hexa Stack Christmas

Tap to select a stack of hexagonal tiles.

Drag to move it onto the board. Release to place it.

Same color tiles will be auto-sorted (stacked).

Tiles are collected once at least 10 tiles having the same color are sorted.
