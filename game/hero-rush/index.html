About Hero Rush

You can play Hero Rush, an action game from our Action category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the arrow keys, W A S D and dragging.

Lead a squad of brave heroes through 10 worlds full of skeletons, slimes and giant bosses! Move to dodge, stop to shoot — your hero fires automatically. Pick a new skill card every level, evolve your abilities, unlock four heroes with their own supers, open chests, earn skins and beat the bosses of the forest, ice and lava worlds.

Why you might like it: Hero Rush has a lot in common with Anything Goes: Stickman Brawl - they share the “arena” and “boss” tags.

How to play Hero Rush

Move with WASD / arrow keys or drag on the screen. Stand still to attack automatically. Press the super button when it is charged. Choose a skill card after each level-up.
