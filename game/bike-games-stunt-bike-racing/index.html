How to play Bike Games: Stunt Bike Racing

Race / Accelerate: Press and hold the Race button to accelerate your bike.

Front Flip: Tap the Front Flip button while airborne to perform a forward stunt.

Back Flip: Tap the Back Flip button while in the air to perform a backward stunt.

Boost (Nitro): Activate Nitro Boost to gain a temporary burst of speed.

Missile Attack: Tap the Missile button to launch a missile at your opponents.

Objective: Reach the finish line before your opponents using stunts, boosts, and missiles.
