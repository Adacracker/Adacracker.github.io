About Goods Sort Match Puzzle

Fancy a brain-teaser? Goods Sort Match Puzzle is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the space bar, the mouse and touch.

Goods Sort Match Puzzle is a colorful shelf-sorting game. Drag drinks, snacks, toys, and other goods between shelves, then line up three identical items to clear them. Each match opens space and reveals more products. Plan your moves, manage empty slots, and clear the board before the timer runs out. Enjoy varied shelf layouts and helpful boosters with mouse or touch controls. Progress is saved in the current browser.

Why you might like it: Goods Sort Match Puzzle has a lot in common with Color Coin Merge and Slime Buffet - they share the “puzzle” and “brain” tags.

How to play Goods Sort Match Puzzle

Desktop Click and hold an item drag it to an empty slot on another shelf and release Mobile Touch and drag items to empty slots Match three identical goods on the same shelf to clear them Use on-screen buttons for boosters and pause.
