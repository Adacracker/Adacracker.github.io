About Merge Cars: Defense

Simulation fans, take note: Merge Cars: Defense is a simulation game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. It is played with dragging.

Merge identical cars to create more powerful vehicles and defend your base from advancing enemies. Each new vehicle deals more damage and helps you overcome increasingly difficult waves. Expand your garage, purchase upgrades, complete missions, and unlock new opportunities. Earn resources while you are away and use them to strengthen your defenses. Plan your vehicle placement, upgrade your cars at the right time, and prepare to face powerful opponents. Simple controls make it easy to begin, while steady progression provides new goals and rewards.

Why you might like it: Fans of Bubble Clicker and Build a Keycap Pyramid may like this one too, since it shares their “idle” and “clicker” tags.

How to play Merge Cars: Defense

Open boxes and place cars in available slots Drag a car onto another car of the same level to merge them into a stronger vehicle Position your cars to defend the base and defeat incoming enemy waves.
