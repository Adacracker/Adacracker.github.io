About Noob Huggy

Noob Huggy is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. You can play it on a computer or a touch screen.

Embark on a whimsical quest with the endearingly clumsy Huggy in "Noob Huggy." Navigate vibrant, blocky mazes, dodge mischievous slimes, and solve puzzles to uncover hidden keys. With seamless controls and the thrilling double-jump, every level becomes a test of timing and exploration in this captivating pixelated world.
