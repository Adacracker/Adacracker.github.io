About WHAT'S WRONG?

WHAT'S WRONG? is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the mouse and taps.

WHAT'S WRONG? is a colorful observation puzzle game with 60 unique scenes. Look carefully, find strange and impossible details, earn stars and coins, and use hints when you get stuck. Each level brings a new picture and new surprises. Can you spot everything that is wrong?

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Hidden Shop Hunt and Secret Hall: Hidden Objects, give WHAT'S WRONG? a try: they share the “find” and “hidden” tags.

How to play WHAT'S WRONG?

Look carefully at each picture and find the strange or impossible objects.

Desktop: Use the left mouse button to select objects and interact with the interface.

Mobile: Tap objects on the screen.

Find all required anomalies to complete the level. Use a hint if you get stuck.
