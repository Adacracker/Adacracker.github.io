About Timber Squirrel

Fancy a brain-teaser? Timber Squirrel loads straight into this page as a free puzzle game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the arrow keys, W A S D and swipes.

Timber Squirrel is a cozy 3D physics-puzzle game starring a hungry squirrel who dreams of one big pinecone across the pond. Push logs, rafts and fallen trees into the water to build floating bridges, then walk across to reach the prize. Features: - 50 handcrafted logic levels with rising difficulty - 3-star rating system: finish each level in fewer moves for a better score - Simple push-and-float mechanics: every object drifts after one push, so plan carefully - Undo (Z), restart (R), level menu (L) and a hint system with arrow guidance - Cute low-poly forest world with a lake, stumps and pine trees - 4 languages: English, Russian, Chinese,.

Why you might like it: Fans of Chinchilla Trails and Rooster Ranch may like this one too, since it shares their “brain” and “logic” tags.
