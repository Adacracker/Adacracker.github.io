About Santa Christmas Delivery

Adventure awaits. Santa Christmas Delivery is a free adventure game that runs right in your browser. You can play it on a computer or a touch screen. Controls use the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar. On a phone, turn it sideways for the wide landscape screen.

With Christmas close to the corner, we have the perfect game to place you in the occasion state of mind and it's called Santa Christmas Delivery. In this new Christmas game for youngsters, you'll assist Santa Claus with conveying presents to the great children on Christmas night while staying away from the Grinch who needs to get Santa's hand taking his presents.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Santa Adventure and Santa Parkour, give Santa Christmas Delivery a try: they share the “christmas” and “winter” tags.

How to play Santa Christmas Delivery

WASD / Arrow Keys to Drive. SPACE for Boost.
