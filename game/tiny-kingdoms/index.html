About Tiny Kingdoms

Tiny Kingdoms is a free strategy game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the mouse and taps.

Build a tiny kingdom across three ages. Gather resources, upgrade buildings, recruit an army and conquer 30 territories through the Tribal, Medieval and Industrial eras. Choose tactical perks, use battle skills and carry permanent legacies into the next age. Every soldier matters: casualties are permanent, so plan your upgrades and formations carefully.

Why you might like it: Fans of Citadel: Fortress Defense may like this one too, since it shares their “building” tag.

How to play Tiny Kingdoms

Click or tap buildings to upgrade them. Open Conquer to choose a territory, recruit troops, adjust your formation and deploy. During battle, use Rally and aim Barrage at enemies. Switch between 1x and 2x battle speed. Press Escape to close a panel or pause battle. Best played in landscape orientation.

GameDistribution does not list Tiny Kingdoms as mobile-friendly, so it is best played on a computer using the mouse.
