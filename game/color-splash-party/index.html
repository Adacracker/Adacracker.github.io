About Color Splash Party

Color Splash Party is a free arcade game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the space bar, the mouse and taps.

A cheerful, family-friendly coloring game with 328 original pictures for kids and grown-ups. Tap any space to fill it with color, or use the brush to draw freely. Color dinosaurs, pets, animals, cars, space, letters and more, or detailed pages like fantasy, cities and nature. Every page has Easy, Medium and Hard versions. Finish pictures to unlock new colors, level up and collect stickers of your artwork. Progress saves automatically, and you can save or share your masterpieces.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Unspool Art and Pixel Number - DIY Coloring, give Color Splash Party a try: they share the “coloring” and “drawing” tags.

How to play Color Splash Party

Mouse or touch. Tap/click a space to fill it with the selected color. Choose colors from the palette at the bottom. Brush = freehand drawing, Pick = copy a color, Undo/Redo, hold Clear to start over, pinch or mouse wheel to zoom, Fit to reset the view. Press Done when finished.
