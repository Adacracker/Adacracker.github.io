About Dig and Descend: Obby Mine

Dig and Descend: Obby Mine is a free simulation game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. You play it with W A S D, the space bar and the mouse.

Descend deeper into the mine, break blocks, unlock new dungeon levels, and discover more valuable resources with greater rewards.

Go through the whole mine, collect cups, and open new areas!

Mine ore, sell it, earn coins, and upgrade your equipment. Buy stronger pickaxes, unlock effects, and boost your digging power.

Get pets that increase mining and income. Collect rare, epic, and legendary helpers, level them up, and meet other miners in the village.

Why you might like it: Dig and Descend: Obby Mine has a lot in common with Drillfall and Diggosaur - they share the “mining” and “dig” tags.

How to play Dig and Descend: Obby Mine

PC:

WASD — movement.

Right mouse button — view.

Left mouse button — dig.

Space bar — jump.

Smartphone:

Joystick on the left — movement.

Buttons on the right — dig and jump.
