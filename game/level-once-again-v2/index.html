About Level Once Again(v2)

Pack your bags: Level Once Again(v2) loads straight into this page as a free adventure game. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the arrow keys, the space bar and taps.

Kaal has kidnapped Tara, and Veer has to get through 100 rooms to save her. Every room looks the same: one hero, one button, one door. But each one hides a new trick. Floors fall, spikes pop out of nowhere, and doors run away. Think ahead, expect the unexpected, and beat 7 bosses across 4 worlds in this troll puzzle platformer.

Why you might like it: Level Once Again(v2) has a lot in common with Drople and Definitely Not a Platformer - they share the “platform” and “escape” tags.

How to play Level Once Again(v2)

PC: Move with Arrow keys or A/D. Jump with Space, W or Up. R = restart, H = hint, M = mute, Esc/P = pause.

Mobile: Use the on-screen Left, Right and Jump buttons (landscape mode).

Stuck? Tap the bulb to see the full solution for the room.
