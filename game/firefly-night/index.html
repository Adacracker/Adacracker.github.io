About Firefly Night

Got a minute? You can play Firefly Night, an arcade game from our Arcade & Casual category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the arrow keys, W A S D and the mouse.

Firefly Night is a calm, glowing adventure through a dark forest. Lead your swarm of fireflies from tree to tree, gather wild fireflies on the way and light every lantern. Thorns, hungry frogs, spider webs, gusts of wind and bats all want a piece of your light. Bring enough fireflies to the moon gate to open it. 12 levels, 3 stars on each, and upgrades that make your swarm bigger and brighter.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Overkill: Top Down Arcade Shooter and Crystal Circuit, give Firefly Night a try: they share the “arcade” tag.

How to play Firefly Night

With a mouse, point where you want the swarm to fly. On a touch screen, drag anywhere, like on a trackpad. You can also fly with WASD or the arrow keys. Fly close to wild fireflies to add them to your swarm. Hover near a lantern until it lights up. Keep away from thorns, frogs, webs and bats. Bring the number of fireflies shown on the gate to the moon gate to finish the level.
