About Carletto Halloween Dreams

Carletto Halloween Dreams is an adventure game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the arrow keys, the space bar and touch.

Carletto enters a Halloween dream of floating islands, haunted forests and glowing castles. Use his telescope to reveal mist-hidden paths and guide starlight through mirrors.
