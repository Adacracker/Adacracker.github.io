About Ultimate TicTacToe

A table classic, no table needed: you can play Ultimate TicTacToe, a board or card game from our Board & Cards category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer.

Ultimate TicTacToe is a strategic twist on the classic tic tac toe game. Play across nine mini boards, send your opponent to specific boards with every move, and win three mini boards in a row to claim victory. Simple to learn, tactical to master, and perfect for quick puzzle battles.

Why you might like it: Fans of Checkmate Rewind and Ludo Quick: Dice Board Games may like this one too, since it shares their “board” and “logic” tags.

How to play Ultimate TicTacToe

Choose a game mode and place your symbol on an open cell. The cell you choose sends the next player to the matching mini board. Win mini boards by making three in a row, then win the match by claiming three mini boards in a row. If the target board is already full or won, you may play on any open board.
