About Bubble Pillar

Puzzle time. Bubble Pillar is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the space bar, the mouse and swipes.

Immerse yourself in a world of classic arcade games, reimagined in a new D format!

This isn't just another "bubbles" game — it's a puzzle where the playing field is located on the surface of a tower, and the space is enclosed in a circle. Here, every trajectory matters, and every shot can be part of a brilliant combo.

Enjoy beautiful graphics, pleasant sounds, and the unique atmosphere of three different locations. Perfect for relaxing after a hard day or for practicing your aim and logic.

Why you might like it: Bubble Pillar has a lot in common with Bubble classic and Words and Bubbles - they share the “timekiller” tag.

How to play Bubble Pillar

Rotate the tower, aim, and shoot!

Your goal — clear the tower of all bubbles.

PC Controls:

Hold the right mouse button and move the cursor to rotate the tower.

Click the left mouse button to shoot.

Mobile Controls:

Swipe along the bottom of the screen to rotate the tower.

Hold your finger on the playing field to aim.

Release your finger — and the bubble flies!
