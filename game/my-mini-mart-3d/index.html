About My Mini Mart 3D

Ready to run the show? You can play My Mini Mart 3D, a simulation game from our Build & Simulate category, free in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the mouse.

Welcome to My Mini Mart 3D, the ultimate supermarket management and shopping simulation game! Start with a small mini market and transform it into a thriving supermarket empire. Grow fresh crops, stock shelves, serve customers, earn profits, and unlock exciting new sections as your business expands. Take control of every part of your store—from farming and harvesting to managing inventory and satisfying customers. Upgrade your market, hire helpers, and become the best supermarket tycoon in town!
