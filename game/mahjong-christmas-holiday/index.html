About Mahjong Christmas Holiday

Mahjong Christmas Holiday is a free board or card game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the mouse and touch.

Step into a Mahjong adventure with plenty of tile puzzles to solve. Mahjong Christmas Holiday is the perfect way to get into the Christmas spirit and get ready for Winter. Solve the engaging mahjong puzzles while enjoying snowy landscapes. Perhaps you’ll even travel back into the past and spot the famous titanic in its heyday….

Why you might like it: Fans of KrisMas Mahjong 2 and Mahjong Tiles Christmas may like this one too, since it shares their “christmas” and “matching” tags.

How to play Mahjong Christmas Holiday

How to play mahjong? Solve the puzzles by eliminating pairs of identical mahjong tiles from play. You can only select the tiles if the left and right sides are free, and if there are no other tiles on top. Wipe the entire board to finish every level.

Use the mouse or touchscreen to play.
