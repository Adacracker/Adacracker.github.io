About Color Sort Party

Color Sort Party is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it.

Have fun with this super-addictive game. Get to work: pour liquids between tubes, sort the colors and beat increasingly challenging levels. Use special boosters, think strategically and keep moving forward.

Why you might like it: Fans of Color Coin Merge and Meow Sudoku may like this one too, since it shares their “logic” and “color” tags.

How to play Color Sort Party

Select a tube and pour the liquid into another matching the colors Be careful Dont get stuck Use boosters like Level Skip One Step Back or Extra Tube to stay ahead Play on 4 tropical backgrounds and customize your tubes with up to 18 different style.
