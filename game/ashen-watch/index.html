About Ashen Watch

Time to plan ahead. Ashen Watch loads straight into this page as a free strategy game. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

Princess Elira is sheltering inside the kingdom’s last keep, surrounded by an endless siege. Control the guardian, defend the castle against waves of enemies, and survive for as long as you can. Fight eight enemy types and powerful bosses, then choose a roguelike perk after each wave: unleash arrow volleys, chain lightning, a flaming blade, or a dodge roll. Earn coins in battle to unlock permanent upgrades and new armor skins. The story unfolds across 20 waves, with a boss and a new cinematic chapter every five waves.
