About City Delivery Rider Simulator

City Delivery Rider Simulator is a free simulation game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the arrow keys, the space bar and touch.

Ride through a lively city as a delivery courier, transport parcels, follow checkpoints, and reach customers before time runs out. Choose motorcycles, navigate busy streets, avoid traffic, manage fuel, and complete increasingly challenging routes.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed City Rescue Forklift Simulator and Car Wash Idle, give City Delivery Rider Simulator a try: they share the “simulation” and “city” tags.

How to play City Delivery Rider Simulator

Use W or Up Arrow to accelerate and S or Down Arrow to reverse Steer with A D or Left Right Arrow Press Space to brake either Shift key to shift your balance C to change the camera and R to reset the motorcycle Touch controls remain available on mob.
