About Bird Sort

Help colorful birds find their flock by sorting them between tree branches. Move birds strategically until every branch contains a complete matching flock. Relax with charming animations, cozy visuals, and satisfying puzzle gameplay that becomes more challenging as you progress.

How to play

Use mouse or touch:

Click or tap a branch to select birds.

Click or tap a highlighted branch to move them.

Complete every branch by grouping matching birds together.
