About Factory Builder

Ready to run the show? Factory Builder loads straight into this page as a free simulation game. You can play it on a computer or a touch screen.

Become an industrial tycoon in Factory Builder, mastering production processes from lamp bulbs to flashlight manufacturing. Strategically upgrade technology, reduce costs, and diversify products to expand your empire. Experience idle gameplay where progress continues while you plan the perfect balance of growth, efficiency, and profitability.

Why you might like it: Factory Builder has a lot in common with Build a Keycap Pyramid and Wrecking Ball Tycoon - they share the “building” tag.

Factory Builder: FAQ
