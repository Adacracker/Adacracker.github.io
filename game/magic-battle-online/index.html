About Magic Battle Online

Ready for some action? Magic Battle Online loads straight into this page as a free action game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with W A S D and the space bar. It can also be played by two people on one device.

Magic Battle Online is an epic online battle with different elements and abilities! Choose an element: fire, water, wind, earth, ice, lightning, light, darkness, crystals, void, and time. Each has its own unique spells and combat abilities! Up to 10 real players can fight in the arena simultaneously. The map changes every five minutes! Level up your elemental mastery from Apprentice to Alpha, increase your spell ranks, forge runes, and fuse them into more powerful ones! Challenge your friends to a duel and participate in events! Can you become the most powerful mage in this cubic world?

Why you might like it: Magic Battle Online has a lot in common with Egg Wars Battle Arena 3D and Verity Block Survival - they share the “minecraft” and “fighting” tags.

How to play Magic Battle Online

Computer controls WASD - move space - jump Shift - crouch Ctrl - run Key 1 - blade 2 3 4 - spells Phone controls joystick on the left look by swiping the screen jump crouch and sprint buttons on the right Spells are on the bottom panel.
