About Deadfloor Factory

You can play Deadfloor Factory, a simulation game from our Build & Simulate category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use W A S D, the space bar and the mouse.

Build the factory. Feed the guns. Hold the core. Deadfloor Factory combines factory automation and tower defense. Route materials through conveyors, storage rooms, and production machines to keep your defenses supplied. Research upgrades, expand your production lines, and hold off growing zombie hordes. Pause to plan your next move, then put your factory to the test. Your progress saves in this browser.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Halloween Hand Care and Halloween Dentist For Kids 2, give Deadfloor Factory a try: they share the “zombie” and “defence” tags.

How to play Deadfloor Factory

Desktop: Left-click to build or select; drag to lay belts and walls. Right-click removes pieces. WASD pans, mouse wheel zooms, R rotates, Space pauses for planning, and Esc opens the menu. Use 1–3 for build menus and Ctrl+Z to undo. Touch: Choose a tool, then tap or drag to build. Use REMOVE to delete pieces. Drag with no tool to pan; pinch to zoom.
