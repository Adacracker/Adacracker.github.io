About Arrow Rescue

Arrow Rescue loads straight into this page as a free adventure game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the arrow keys and taps.

Cute animals are trapped inside dangerous mazes, and only your strategy can save them!

Pull arrows in the correct order to remove obstacles, unlock hidden paths, and guide every animal to safety.

Each puzzle introduces new challenges that test your logic and planning.

With relaxing gameplay, colorful visuals, and hundreds of handcrafted levels, every rescue is both rewarding and satisfying.

Think carefully, choose wisely, and become the hero every animal is waiting for!

Why you might like it: Arrow Rescue has a lot in common with Steal Brainrot Original 3D and Italian Animal Alchemy - Brainrot - they share the “animal” and “escape” tags.

How to play Arrow Rescue

Tap and pull arrows to change the maze.

Remove obstacles in the correct order.

Open a safe route for the trapped animals.

Avoid blocking the only escape path.

Plan every move before taking action.

Rescue every animal to unlock more challenging puzzles and explore new levels.

Easy to learn but challenging to master—every level rewards smart thinking and careful planning.
