About Pixel Path 2

Got a minute? Pixel Path 2 is an arcade game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the arrow keys, the space bar and taps.

You'll play as Pixel Tadpole. You'll have to navigate twenty challenging levels. The difficulty increases with each level. You'll need to be attentive and agile to complete them all. Spikes, pits, and falling coconuts are just a few of the challenging challenges that await you. Your nervous system will need to be strong to successfully complete the levels. Only the most experienced players will be able to complete this game.

Why you might like it: Fans of Red and Green Christmas and SantaDays Christmas may like this one too, since it shares their “arcade” and “jumping” tags.

How to play Pixel Path 2

PC controls:

A, D, Left Arrow, Right Arrow - character controls.

Space - Jump.

Mobile device controls:

Tap the on-screen touch game buttons.

You need to reach the flag to complete the level.
