About Punch Through!

Ready to run the show? Punch Through! is a free simulation game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

Framed for stealing the Mayors Golden Donut, prisoner #4027 punches his way out! Smash walls block by block in first person, vacuum the rubble, cash it in and upgrade from fists to boxing gloves, a hammer, a pickaxe, a drill, dynamite and a laser blaster. Explore 7 zones and 38 rooms from the prison to outer space, beat 7 bosses, follow Moe the Moles clues at trap forks, find 55 hidden treasures, hire mole helpers that dig while youre away and Escape for permanent perks.

Why you might like it: Fans of Needle Rush and Build a Keycap Pyramid may like this one too, since it shares their “idle” and “clicker” tags.
