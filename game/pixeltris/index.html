About PixelTris

PixelTris loads straight into this page as a free puzzle game. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the arrow keys, the space bar and taps.

A falling-block puzzle where every piece shatters into flowing sand. Bridge one colour wall to wall to clear it, then chain combos.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Dessert Merge and Frosty Connection Quest, give PixelTris a try: they share the “matching” and “sliding-puzzle” tags.

How to play PixelTris

HOW TO PLAY.

Blocks fall, then shatter into loose sand when they land.

Connect one colour of sand from the left wall to the right wall to clear it.

Clear again within 3 seconds to build a combo multiplier.

Sand piles up as you go — the run ends when there's no room for a new block.

CONTROLS.

Move — Left/Right arrows, or A / D.

Rotate — Up arrow, W, or X.

Soft drop — Down arrow, or S.

Hard drop — Space.

Touch — drag to move, tap to rotate, flick down to drop.
