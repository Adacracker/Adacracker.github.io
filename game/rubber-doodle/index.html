About Rubber Doodle

Puzzle time. You can play Rubber Doodle, a puzzle game from our Puzzle category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the arrow keys, the mouse and taps.

Rubber Doodle is a physics puzzle where every line you draw turns into a rubber band. Draw a band, stretch it and let it snap: catch the ball, fling it like a slingshot, wrap bands around pegs and bounce off walls. Guide the ball into the cup through 75 hand-drawn stages and 3 tutorials, with saws, lasers, TNT, portals, keys, boost arrows, upside-down gravity and long-shot baskets. Earn stars, unlock new worlds and discover your own solutions.

Why you might like it: Fans of Happy Orange Glass and Color Coin Merge may like this one too, since it shares their “brain” and “logic” tags.

How to play Rubber Doodle

Mouse touch draw rubber bands between pins or black ink Tap the band holding the ball to release it Stretch a band and let go to launch pull too far and it snaps Z undo R restart Light bulb hint Guide the ball into the cup.
