About Tumble Dungeon: Gravity Puzzle

Fancy a brain-teaser? You can play Tumble Dungeon: Gravity Puzzle, a puzzle game from our Puzzle category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the arrow keys, the mouse and touch.

Tumble Dungeon is a turn-based gravity puzzle. Your hero only walks left and right, but you can spin the whole dungeon: turn gravity left, right or upside down, and the hero tumbles until they hit a wall. Find lost gears, repair every broken mechanism and pull the portal switch to escape. Spikes kill on a fall, holes drop you into the void. Pull levers, open coloured locks, fire cannons and time your moves around ghosts, slimes and zombies. 30 levels, keyboard or touch, portrait or landscape.

Why you might like it: Fans of Slime Buffet and Atomic Puzzle XMas may like this one too, since it shares their “logic” and “pixel” tags.

How to play Tumble Dungeon: Gravity Puzzle

A / D or Left / Right arrows: walk. Q / E: turn gravity left / right. W: flip gravity 180°. Any key after a death or a win: retry / next level. Mouse / touch: on-screen buttons, walk on the left, gravity on the right.
