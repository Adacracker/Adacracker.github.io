About Diggosaur

Ready to run the show? Diggosaur is a simulation game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

Diggosaur is a cozy digging game with little dinosaurs. Grandpa Bruno went down to save the drying spring and never came back - dig after him! Tunnel through soil, ice, magma and crystal caves, collect ores, fossils and pages of his lost journal, and grow your village with a garden, furnace, dojo and museum. Unlock 12 dinos with their own abilities, befriend pets, solve puzzles and fly a balloon to the Fern Island. No fighting, no stress. Progress is saved automatically.

Why you might like it: Diggosaur has a lot in common with Winter Wolf and Block Runner: Subway Escape - they share the “minecraft” and “mining” tags.

How to play Diggosaur

Dig down, collect treasures and return to the surface before your energy runs out. Spend your loot in the village, then dig deeper.

PC: arrows or WASD walk and dig (hold to keep digging), Q ability, 1-9 items, B back to the surface, E/Enter/Space talk and enter buildings, I backpack, P photo, mouse for menus.

Mobile: swipe to walk and dig, hold your finger down to keep digging, tap the buttons.
