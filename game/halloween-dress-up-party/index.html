About Halloween Dress Up Party

Halloween Dress Up Party is a dress-up game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the mouse and taps.

Halloween Dress Up Party is a spooky-cute fashion game in six themed levels: witch, vampire, zombie, ghost, pumpkin and mummy. Style the model from head to toe with skin tones, faces, hairstyles, dresses, capes, shoes, hats, jewelry, bags, pets and backgrounds. Tap Done and three judges score your look for up to 3 stars. Every finished look is saved to your gallery and unlocks the next party. No reading needed: play on phone, tablet or desktop.

Why you might like it: Halloween Dress Up Party has a lot in common with Besties Halloween Fashionista and Spooky Halloween Makeover - they share the “fashion” and “halloween” tags.

How to play Halloween Dress Up Party

Tap or click to play. Pick a party on the map, choose a tab (skin, face, hair, dress, shoes, hat, pet, background...) and tap a card to put that item on. Use the dice for a random look or reset to start over. Tap Done to let the judges score your look, then Next to save it to your gallery and unlock the next party.
