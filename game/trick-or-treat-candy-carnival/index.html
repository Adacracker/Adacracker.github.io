About Trick Or Treat Candy Carnival

Trick Or Treat Candy Carnival loads straight into this page as a free puzzle game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

Choose from seven games: pop ghosts, build 24 jigsaw pictures, find matching pairs, explore a talking alphabet, catch falling candy, recognize shadows and clear groups of monster faces. Pick gentle play or a challenge, collect candy, unlock four baskets and fill your collection of 24 cartoon friends. Puzzles offer 4, 9, 16 or 25 interlocking pieces.

Why you might like it: Fans of Spooky Vending Machine 2 and Candy Blast - Candy Bomb Puzzle Game may like this one too, since it shares their “monster” and “halloween” tags.
