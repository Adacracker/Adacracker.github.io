About Cozy Puzzle: Cats

Fancy a brain-teaser? Cozy Puzzle: Cats is a puzzle game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the mouse and dragging.

Fit shapes onto the board, discover new cats, complete levels, and earn daily rewards.

A cozy puzzle game where every cat-shaped piece needs to find its perfect place on the board. Drag pieces, rotate them, and build complete compositions. Progress through a large campaign, meet new cats, and grow your collection.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Blankets and CatDoku, give Cozy Puzzle: Cats a try: they share the “cat” and “cute” tags.

How to play Cozy Puzzle: Cats

Drag cat-shaped pieces from the bottom of the screen onto the board. Place them so they fill the empty cells without overlapping or going outside the borders. Click a piece to rotate it, then keep dragging it into position.

Complete a level by fitting every piece into the correct place. The cleaner your solution and the fewer extra moves you make, the more stars you earn. Unlock new campaign levels, try random puzzles, and complete daily and weekly challenges.
