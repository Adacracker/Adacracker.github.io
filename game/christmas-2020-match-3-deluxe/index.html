About Christmas 2020 Match 3 Deluxe

Fancy a brain-teaser? Christmas 2020 Match 3 Deluxe is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. You can play it on a computer or a touch screen. You play it with the mouse. On a phone, hold it upright: the game is made for portrait mode.
