About Snake Duel

Got a minute? Snake Duel loads straight into this page as a free arcade game. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the arrow keys, the mouse and touch. It can also be played by two people on one device.

Snake Duel is a fast-paced snake game where you collect food, grow bigger, and outplay your opponents.

Play in Single Player or challenge a friend in 2 Player mode. Defeat other snakes, survive as long as possible, and become the last snake standing.

Unlock 40 unique skins by completing challenges and customize your snake.

Enter the arena, grow bigger, and claim victory!

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Red and Green Christmas and Geometry Vibes 2 Online, give Snake Duel a try: they share the “2players” and “snake” tags.

How to play Snake Duel

1 Player Mode Controls.

Desktop.

Mouse: Move.

Hold Left Mouse Button: Boost.

Mobile Controls.

Touch the screen and drag: Move.

Keep touching and make repeated drag movements: Boost.

2 Player Mode Controls.

Player 1.

A / D: Move.

W: Boost.

Player 2.

Left / Right Arrow: Move.

Up Arrow: Boost.

Mobile Controls for Both Players.

Touch the screen and drag: Move.

Keep touching and make repeated drag movements: Boost.
