About Santa Claus Rush

Got a minute? You can play Santa Claus Rush, an arcade game from our Arcade & Casual category, free in your browser. It works on phones, tablets and computers. You play it with the mouse and taps. On a phone, turn it sideways for the wide landscape screen.

Santa Claus Rush is fun and addicting arcade game suitable for all ages. You need to stay alive as long time as possible and collect as many gifts as posssible. Jump and avoid incoming rockets. Many children are waiting for the christmas gift so collect as many as possible. We are counting on you, dear Santa! Have fun playing.
