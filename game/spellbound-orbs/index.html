About Spellbound Orbs

Fancy a brain-teaser? Spellbound Orbs is a puzzle game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the mouse, taps and touch.

Spellbound Orbs is a magical bubble shooter set in a wizards castle. Aim and shoot colorful orbs, match three or more of the same color to pop them, and watch the orbs below fall. Shots are limited, so aim carefully and bounce off the walls to reach tricky spots. Play 1000 levels, some with special goals: free the fairies, free the dragon or collect dragon eggs. Stuck? Use magic boosters: Color Pop clears one color, Hook drops an orb and everything below it, Blast destroys the orbs around your tap. Complete daily quests, spin the lucky wheel and open the hourly reward chest.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Galaxy Pop and Candy Match Pop, give Spellbound Orbs a try: they share the “puzzle” and “match 3” tags.

How to play Spellbound Orbs

Touch and drag or hold the mouse button to aim release to shoot.
