About Cut the Nut

Fancy a brain-teaser? You can play Cut the Nut, a puzzle game from our Puzzle category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the mouse and taps.

Cut the Nut! Cut ropes, bounce objects, and use clever tricks to guide the nut safely into the squirrel's mouth. Each level brings new challenges that test your timing, creativity, and problem-solving skills.

With adorable animations, satisfying interactions, and tons of brain-teasing fun, Cut the Nut is a charming adventure for players of all ages. Can you help the squirrel grab every nut?
