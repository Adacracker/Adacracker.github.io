About Fat Duck Frenzy

Fat Duck Frenzy is a free driving game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the space bar, swipes and touch.

An endless candy road that folds through a dream. Weave between lanes of candy traffic, leap striped barricades and void gaps, and hold tight when reality flips into loop-the-loops and reality folds. Three hearts per run — grab shields to soak a hit, snatch glowing shards for combo bonus points, and squeeze past traffic for near-miss bonuses. The road only gets faster. How far can the fat duck dream?

Why you might like it: Fat Duck Frenzy has a lot in common with Nutty Rush Kart and Extreme Quad Bike Racing - they share the “racing” and “driving” tags.

How to play Fat Duck Frenzy

Keyboard mdash or A D steer between lanes SPACE W jump press twice for a double jump P or the on-screen pause button to pause any time even mid-stunt Touch mdash on-screen steer and jump buttons drag to steer swipe up to jump Auto-paus.
