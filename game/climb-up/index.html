About Climb Up

Pack your bags: you can play Climb Up, an adventure game from our Adventure category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the arrow keys, the mouse and touch.

Climb Up is a one-finger precision platformer about getting a small cube to the top of a mountain that keeps knocking it back down. Drag to aim, release to jump: pull further to fly further, with a dotted line showing exactly where you are headed. Every level is generated fresh, so nothing is memorised - saw blades, spikes, pistons and moving platforms sit somewhere new each run. Checkpoints hold your progress, and collected coins drop you back at the last one. How high can you get?

Why you might like it: Fans of Trap Step and VEX 3 Xmas may like this one too, since it shares their “platformer” and “jumping” tags.

How to play Climb Up

Click and hold anywhere, drag to aim, then release to jump. On touch devices, press and hold, drag to aim, and lift your finger to jump. Pull further for a longer jump. The dotted line and the arrow show the direction and the distance you will travel. Reach the flag at the top of each level to move on.
