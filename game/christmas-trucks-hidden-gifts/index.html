About Christmas Trucks Hidden Gifts

Buckle up: you can play Christmas Trucks Hidden Gifts, a driving game from our Cars & Racing category, free in your browser. You can play it on a computer or a touch screen. You play it with taps. On a phone, turn it sideways for the wide landscape screen.

Christmas Trucks Hidden Gifts is a game where you have to find the ten hidden gifts in the picture with Christmas trucks. You have a limited time of 60 seconds to find them all. If you run out of time, repeat the level and try again. Have fun with 6 different pictures where gifts are hidden.

Why you might like it: Christmas Trucks Hidden Gifts has a lot in common with Santa Gift Delivery Truck and Monster Truck 3D Winter - they share the “christmas” and “gifts” tags.
