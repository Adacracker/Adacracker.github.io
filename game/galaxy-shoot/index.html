About Galaxy Shoot

Galaxy Shoot loads straight into this page as a free arcade game. It is best played on a computer. It is played with taps.

Galaxy Shoot is a hyper-casual arcade game designed to test your absolute focus and timing. You will oversee a central turret that continuously auto-rotates, facing targets that are also constantly moving in orbit.

Why you might like it: Players who like Astro Marge and Red and Green Christmas often enjoy it too - they are all Arcade & Casual games.

How to play Galaxy Shoot

Gameplay requires only a single tap. Tap anywhere on the screen to fire exactly when the gun's barrel points directly at the passing target.
