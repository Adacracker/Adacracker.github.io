About Mask of six Moons

Mask of six Moons is a free adventure game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the arrow keys, the space bar and the mouse.

Mask of Six Moons is a pixel-art action platformer. Dive through the moon gates, battle monsters and loot weapons from Common all the way to the ultra-rare Phantom tier. Choose from 12 weapon types, including greatswords, scythes, hammers, bows, staffs and chain whips, each with its own moves. Every weapon rolls random bonuses, and rare ones carry unique effects. Level up, spend points on five stats and unlock dash, double jump and wall jump. Two gates are open now: the Sunken Crypt, guarded by the Drowned Carillon, and the Fungal Caverns, ruled by the Mother Bloom. More gates are coming in future updates.

Why you might like it: Mask of six Moons has a lot in common with Gorbo the Orc: Crown Quest and Villadash Hero Rescue - they share the “adventure” and “action” tags.
