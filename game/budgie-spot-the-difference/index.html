About Budgie Spot the Difference

Puzzle time. Budgie Spot the Difference is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. It runs on desktop and mobile alike. It is played with the mouse. On a phone, turn it sideways for the wide landscape screen.

Get ready for a fun bird puzzle adventure with Budgie Spot the Difference! Look carefully at two colorful budgie pictures and try to find everything that has changed. With cute budgies, colorful scenes, and relaxing gameplay, this is a fun game for kids and bird lovers of all ages.
