About Planet Blast: Rockets

Ready for some action? You can play Planet Blast: Rockets, an action game from our Action category, free in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the mouse and taps.

Take control of a rocket arsenal and turn entire worlds into blazing craters. Choose a target, adjust the design, and watch a precise strike reshape the planet.

Every launch is a search for the perfect balance of fuel, mass, and blast power. Upgrade modules, unlock new planets, and reach the farthest corners of the galaxy.

Why you might like it: Fans of Boom Boss may like this one too, since it shares their “destroy” and “explosion” tags.

How to play Planet Blast: Rockets

On desktop, press and hold the left mouse button to adjust rocket parameters, choose a target, and launch. On mobile, perform the same actions with taps and finger gestures. After each launch, review the result, improve weaker parameters, and prepare the next rocket.
