About Ciallo World

You can play Ciallo World, an adventure game from our Adventure category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the mouse and touch.

Ciallo World is a single-player poker roguelike. Each round you draw 8 cards, play 1 to 5 of them as a poker hand and try to reach the target score with a limited number of plays and discards. Clear the round and recruit one of three programming-language girls; their skills trigger on every hand and stack into wild multipliers. Every 4th round is a boss with its own rule, and every cleared round adds a bug card you must refactor away.

Why you might like it: It sits in our Adventure games alongside VEX 3 Xmas and Christmas Candy Escape 3D, so if you like those, try this one.

How to play Ciallo World

Mouse or touch. Click cards to select them, then Play or Discard. One click for everything.
