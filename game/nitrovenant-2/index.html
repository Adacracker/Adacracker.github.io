About Nitrovenant 2

Buckle up: Nitrovenant 2 is a driving game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the arrow keys, the space bar and the mouse.

Drive through endless hordes of zombie workers in an armored car with a rooftop missile launcher. Your car fires on its own: you pick the lane, jump the potholes and hit the nitro to plow through the crowd. 120 stages across six places, from sunny fields and rainy forests to the desert, a frozen pass, a dead city and the Inferno. Every stage ends with a giant boss with its own moves. Wait for it to get dizzy and hit back. Pull off flips and barrel rolls on ramps, keep your combo going to unlock Fury mode, grab power-ups, rescue survivors and upgrade your weapon, armor and tires. Collect 12 cars, paint jobs and parts, finish daily challenges and missions, and see how far you can go in Endless mode.
