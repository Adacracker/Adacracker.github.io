About Prison Break

Manage your prison! Improve the prison block.

Build fortifications.

Fight with prisoners, catch them and return them to their cages.

There are several prisons in the game.

You need to stay in each prison for about 10 days.

Every day you need to build fortifications, hire personnel and upgrade.

At the end of each day, prisoners escape, and your task is to stop it. Defeat all the prisoners and move on to the next day, where new challenges, new prisoners and even Bosses await you!

How to play

Control:

For PC.

Character control - "WASD", "arrows" or hold the left mouse button and drag the mouse in the desired direction.

Shooting - the hero shoots automatically.

For Mobile devices.

Character control - press your finger on the screen and drag your finger in the desired direction.

Shooting - the hero shoots automatically.

To interact with any activities, you just need to stand on them as a character.
