About Merge a Mansion

You can play Merge a Mansion, a puzzle game from our Puzzle category, free in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the space bar, the mouse and dragging.

Merge a Mansion is a relaxing isometric merge game where you combine together basic materials to build grand structures. Keep combining to climb from a tiny cabin all the way to a sprawling Grand Estate, and if youve got the patience, unlock the secret Ultimate Estate beyond it. The higher your buildings level up, the more your property earns. Once you have a basic shack, your buildings generate passive income even while you plan your next merge, so your cash keeps stacking as you play. Spend it to create better materials, unlock more space, and give yourself the tools for bigger and better creations.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Rope Chef, give Merge a Mansion a try: they share the “arcade” tag.

How to play Merge a Mansion

Drag the left mouse button to merge move the objects Use the scroll wheel to zoom in out Optional Spacebar to create new objects.
