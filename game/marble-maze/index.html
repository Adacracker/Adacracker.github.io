About Marble Maze

Got a minute? Marble Maze loads straight into this page as a free arcade game. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

Roll. Dodge red. Reach green.

Guide a glossy marble through wild worlds where danger comes disguised as decoy holes, spinning blades, moving walls, and toxic blobs. Grab coins, chain power-ups, and ride a hot streak into GOLD RUSH. Unlock skins, chase stars, and see how far "one more level" takes you.

Why you might like it: Fans of SantaDays Christmas and Pharaoh Runner: Ancient Escape may like this one too, since it shares their “arcade” and “runner” tags.

How to play Marble Maze

Desktop: Use WASD or the Arrow keys to roll the marble. Hold Shift to boost. Press R to restart, Enter or Space to confirm, and Esc or P to pause.

Mobile: Tilt your device to steer, or drag on the screen to move. Hold BOOST to speed up.
