About BeesPuzzle

BeesPuzzle is a puzzle game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. It is played with taps and dragging.

Welcome to the cozy world of little bees! BeesPuzzle is a casual puzzle, where every level is a new mini-puzzle with vibrant, colorful artwork.

Drag the hives across the field and help the bees collect tiles. The further you go, the trickier it gets.

The perfect game to unwind in the evening, take a break, or pass the time on the go. No stress, no timers — just warm buzzing and sweet honey.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Blobby Jars and Christmas sorting, give BeesPuzzle a try: they share the “sorting” and “relaxing” tags.

How to play BeesPuzzle

Goal — fill all the slots on the board and collect all the tiles.

Controls:

Tap a hive of the desired color — the bees will fly out to collect the tiles.

Objects connected by a rope move together. Drag any one — the rest will follow.

Unlock hives that are currently unavailable.

The speed-up button at the bottom of the screen makes the bees fly faster when you don't feel like waiting.

Enjoy the game!
