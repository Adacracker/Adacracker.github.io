About Paddle Rush

You can play Paddle Rush, a driving game from our Cars & Racing category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the space bar.

An exciting boat adventure awaits! Avoid obstacles, jump off ramps, and collect coins to purchase upgrades. You can purchase peppers to speed up, anchors to stop, and propellers to reverse.

Why you might like it: It sits in our Cars & Racing games alongside Hyper Cars Stunt Racing and Speed Wilds, so if you like those, try this one.
