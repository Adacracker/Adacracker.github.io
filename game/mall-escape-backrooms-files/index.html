About Mall Escape: Backrooms Files

Mall Escape: Backrooms Files loads straight into this page as a free adventure game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with W A S D, the mouse and dragging.

Mall Escape: Backrooms Files is a first-person stealth horror game. You wake up alone in an abandoned shopping mall with a flashlight and no way out. The Custodian patrols the halls and hunts by sound, so walk quietly, crouch, switch off your light and hide in lockers when you hear whistling. Find keys, restore the power, read notes and solve keypad codes to escape, then follow the signal to Westbrook House in Chapter 2.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Shadows of the Forest: Reign of Triduja and Dont Splash, give Mall Escape: Backrooms Files a try: they share the “horror” and “escape” tags.

How to play Mall Escape: Backrooms Files

Use WASD to move and the mouse to look around. Press E or left click to interact and to hide in lockers. Hold Shift to run, but running makes noise. Press C or Ctrl to crouch, F or right click to toggle the flashlight, and R to cycle the signal tuner. Press Esc or P to pause. On phones and tablets, drag the left side to move, drag the right side to look, and use the on-screen buttons.
