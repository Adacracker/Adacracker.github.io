About Ice Girl Rescue

Adventure awaits. Ice Girl Rescue is a free adventure game that runs right in your browser. You can play it on a computer or a touch screen.

Embark on a thrilling quest in Ice Girl Rescue, where cunning strategy and quick reflexes are your allies. Use Fire Boy to vanquish fireball threats and navigate tricky obstacles like button doors and lethal lasers. With only three lives, every calculated move counts in your mission to save Ice Girl.

Why you might like it: Fans of Snowball Destroyer and SnowBall Adventure may like this one too, since it shares their “adventure” tag.

Ice Girl Rescue: FAQ

Does Ice Girl Rescue cost anything?

No, it costs nothing. Ice Girl Rescue is free to play in your browser on Arcade Cabin. It is supplied by GamePix, and the provider may show ads before or during the game.

Can I play Ice Girl Rescue on my phone?

Yes. GamePix lists Ice Girl Rescue as mobile-friendly, so it works with touch on phones and tablets as well as on computers. It uses a wide landscape screen.

Do I need to download Ice Girl Rescue?

No. Ice Girl Rescue runs in your browser right on this page - press Play to start. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

Who made Ice Girl Rescue?

Ice Girl Rescue is distributed by GamePix, one of the three game providers whose catalogs Arcade Cabin uses. The game belongs to its developer, who is not named in GamePix's catalog feed.

What should I play after Ice Girl Rescue?

Try Snowball Destroyer, SnowBall Adventure and Chicken Jockey: Penguin Rescue. Like Ice Girl Rescue, they are in our Adventure category.
