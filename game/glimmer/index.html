About Glimmer

Glimmer loads straight into this page as a free adventure game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the arrow keys, the space bar and touch.

Guide a tiny spark through a world swallowed by darkness. Master precise jumps, dashes and checkpoints as you relight lanterns and conquer challenging handcrafted stages. Travel across four themed chapters, learn new movement challenges and discover the secret behind the final Sun Lantern. Fast restarts and checkpoints keep the adventure moving.

Why you might like it: Glimmer has a lot in common with Flamit and Daddy Mouse - they share the “adventure” tag.

How to play Glimmer

Move A D or Left Right arrows Jump Space W or Up hold for extra height Dash X or Shift Pause Esc or P Touchscreen buttons are supported on mobile.
