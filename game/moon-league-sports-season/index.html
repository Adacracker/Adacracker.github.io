About Moon League Sports Season

Moon League Sports Season is a free sports game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the mouse and touch.

Under the light of the full moon, three monster-girlfriends are preparing for the brightest sports competitions of the year - the Lunar League Championship. Help the girls choose stylish sports outfits, trendy hairstyles and bright makeup for each competition. Create unique sports looks: from elegant dresses for the opening ceremony to uniforms for training. Mix colors, accessories and shoes to make each monstro-athlete shine in her own way. The Lunar League is waiting for its champions!

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Snowball Kickup and Soccer Orbit, give Moon League Sports Season a try: they share the “sport” tag.

How to play Moon League Sports Season

Go to the monstro-championship Moon League to create a collection of the best sports bows of the season and take a keepsake photo at the end of the game. Depending on the gaming device, either a click of a computer mouse or a simple touch on touchscreens is used to control it.
