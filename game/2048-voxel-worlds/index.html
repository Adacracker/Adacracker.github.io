About 2048 Voxel Worlds

2048 Voxel Worlds loads straight into this page as a free puzzle game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use swipes.

2048 Voxel Worlds is the classic merge puzzle rebuilt in full 3D. Swipe to slide a whole board of lit voxel blocks, merge matching numbers and chase the target cube past 2048. Spend Swap, Blast and Rotate powers, earn coins on every merge, and unlock eleven worlds.

Why you might like it: Fans of Blocktrail 2048 and Cube Snake 2048 may like this one too, since it shares their “2048” tag.

How to play 2048 Voxel Worlds

GOAL: merge matching numbered blocks until you build the target tile. On a.

4x4 board that is 2048, on 5x5 it is 4096, and on 6x6 it is 8192. You can keep.

Playing past it.
