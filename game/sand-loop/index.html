About Sand Loop

Fancy a brain-teaser? Sand Loop is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. You play it with taps.

Discover Sand Loop, a uniquely relaxing puzzle experience where you collect the sand from the beautiful sand art which is very satisfying to watch and play.

Guide moving buckets along a conveyor belt, collect sand by color, and watch as each level comes to life in a smooth cascade of motion. Every grain falls with realistic physics, creating a mesmerizing and satisfying visual journey.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Sandly: Color Flow and Sort Right, give Sand Loop a try: they share the “sorting” and “sand” tags.

How to play Sand Loop

How to Play:

Tap to release and guide buckets along the conveyor.

Collect the right color sand in the right place.

Fill each section to gradually reveal the hidden artwork.

Adapt to new obstacles and level layouts as you progress.

Complete the picture and enjoy the satisfying finish.
