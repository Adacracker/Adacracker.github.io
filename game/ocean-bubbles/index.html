About Ocean Bubbles

Puzzle time. Ocean Bubbles loads straight into this page as a free puzzle game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with taps.

Beneath the waves lies a colorful bubble world where skill and focus are everything. Tackle infinite levels filled with unique obstacles and special balls, unlock powerful boosters and earn bonuses on your path to perfection.

Why you might like it: Ocean Bubbles has a lot in common with Words and Bubbles and Shoot the Bubbles - they share the “bubble” and “aim” tags.

How to play Ocean Bubbles

Aim carefully and tap to shoot bubbles, matching three or more of the same color to clear the board. Use boosters like bombs, rainbows, rockets and duo-color balls to tackle tricky obstacles. Finish levels with precision to earn stars and unlock rewards!
