About Stellar Bastion

Lock and load: you can play Stellar Bastion, a shooting game from our Shooting category, free in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the arrow keys, W A S D and the mouse.

Stellar Bastion is an endless wave-survival shooter. You are the last gunner on a star-fortress flight deck, and the bugs only want one thing: you. Hold to move while your rifle auto-fires at the nearest bug — aiming is handled, so every decision is positioning. Waves escalate every 13 seconds, and every 5th wave a HIVE KING speeds up the whole swarm. Kills drop MGs, shotguns and rocket launchers with limited ammo; your MK-1 sidearm never runs dry. Level-ups offer 3-pick-1 perk cards from 22 upgrades without ever pausing the fight. How many waves will you bank?

Why you might like it: Stellar Bastion has a lot in common with Cold Iron: Last Extraction and Grave Blast - they share the “shooter” and “survival” tags.
