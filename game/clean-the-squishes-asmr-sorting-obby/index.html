About Clean the Squishes! ASMR Sorting Obby

CLEAN UP THE SQUISHY STORE!

The best squishes in one game! Dumplings, Butter, NeeDoh and other popular ASMR toys are scattered all over a huge D store. Collect them all and place them on the correct shelves!

The more toys you organize, the more rewards you'll earn! Make money, buy upgrades, boost your aura, unlock new abilities, and become the fastest player to clean the entire store!

In the game you'll find:

Hundreds of squishies and popular toys.

Relaxing ASMR sorting and store cleaning.

How to play

Desktop.

WASD — Move.

Left Mouse Button — Hide cursor (activate camera)

Mouse — Look around.

F — Show cursor.

Space — Jump.

Left Mouse Button — Pick up, squeeze or place a squish.

Q — Throw a squish.

E — Activate ability.

— Inventory navigation.

G — Switch mode.

Y — Settings.

C — Magic.

B — Inventory.

Z — Upgrades.

V — Shop.

R — Rewards.

Mobile Controls.

Use the joystick to move.

Swipe on the right side of the screen to control the camera.

To pick up a squish, aim the camera at it and tap the item.
