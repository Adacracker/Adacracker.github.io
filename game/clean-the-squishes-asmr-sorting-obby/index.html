About Clean the Squishes! ASMR Sorting Obby

Clean the Squishes! ASMR Sorting Obby loads straight into this page as a free simulation game. It is best played on a computer. You play it with W A S D, the space bar and the mouse.

CLEAN UP THE SQUISHY STORE!

The best squishes in one game! Dumplings, Butter, NeeDoh and other popular ASMR toys are scattered all over a huge D store. Collect them all and place them on the correct shelves!

The more toys you organize, the more rewards you'll earn! Make money, buy upgrades, boost your aura, unlock new abilities, and become the fastest player to clean the entire store!

How to play Clean the Squishes! ASMR Sorting Obby

Desktop.

Q — Throw a squish.

E — Activate ability.
