About Smashling

Fancy a brain-teaser? Smashling is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use dragging.

Smashling is a physics-based knockdown puzzler. Pull back, aim, and launch to topple stacked structures and rescue the trapped creature inside — real physics throughout, no scripted collapses. Three materials behave differently: wood breaks clean, stone bounces and absorbs weak hits, glass shatters in a chain reaction. Clear each level in fewer shots for up to 3 stars. Simple to pick up, hard to master — the perfect solution is always one clever shot away.

Why you might like it: Smashling has a lot in common with Santa Puzzles and Mine Hunt Classic - they share the “arcade” and “physics” tags.

How to play Smashling

Drag the projectile back, aim using the trajectory line, and release to launch. Knock down the structure to destroy the target inside. Clear each level in as few shots as possible for a higher star rating.
