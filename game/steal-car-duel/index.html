About Steal Car Duel

Steal Car Duel is a driving game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the arrow keys, the space bar and the mouse. It can also be played by two people on one device.

Step into a high-energy world of engines, rivalry, and fast reflexes.
