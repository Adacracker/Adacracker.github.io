About Threads

Puzzle time. Threads is a puzzle game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it.

In this game, you untangle pieces of fabric and wind them onto matching colored spools. Fabric falls from above, and your task is to correctly place spools so each thread is collected by its matching color.

Each level becomes more challenging: more colors appear, the speed increases, and combinations become more complex. You need to make quick decisions and carefully plan your moves to avoid mistakes.

Why you might like it: Threads has a lot in common with Tile Sort: Color Columns and Blobby Jars - they share the “sorting” and “sliding-puzzle” tags.

How to play Threads

In this game, your goal is to untangle pieces of fabric and correctly distribute them onto matching spools. On each level, fabric pieces of different colors fall from the top, while spools are placed at the bottom.

Your task is to match each fabric color with the correct spool. If the match is correct, the fabric is automatically wound onto the spool. If you make a mistake, the level becomes more challenging and requires more careful planning.
