About Arms Race

Got a minute? Arms Race is a free arcade game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the mouse.

Arms Race is a fast-paced game where you merge duplicate weapons to forge an increasingly devastating arsenal. Combine identical firearms to unlock higher-tier guns, boost your firepower, and continuously escalate your combat potential in an addictive, endless pursuit of total battlefield dominance.

Why you might like it: Arms Race has a lot in common with Mathematics Frenzy and Apex Prime - they share the “arcade” and “adventure” tags.
