About Winter Wolf

Ready to run the show? Winter Wolf is a simulation game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. You play it with W A S D.

Winter has come, and the snowfall has begun. Since it's Christmas time, the wolf must find three stars during the winter. The wolf needs to carefully hunt and catch three sheep, take their stars, and gather all the bones on the path to avoid starving. It must also be cautious of the hunters in the forest. At the end of the level, it must reach the dark portal to teleport to its desired location.

Why you might like it: Winter Wolf has a lot in common with Getting Over Snow and Evil Santa - they share the “christmas” and “minecraft” tags.

How to play Winter Wolf

Use the "WASD" keys to move.

Double Jump is active.

Collect all 3 stars and bones to activate the dark portal.
