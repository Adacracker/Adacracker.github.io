About Planet Escape

Adventure awaits. Planet Escape is a free adventure game that runs right in your browser. It works on phones, tablets and computers. On a phone, hold it upright: the game is made for portrait mode.

Pilot your rocket through vibrant alien worlds in Planet Escape, where quick reflexes and strategic mineral collection are key. Does Planet Escape cost anything?

No, it costs nothing. Planet Escape is free to play in your browser on Arcade Cabin. It is supplied by GamePix, and the provider may show ads before or during the game.

Yes. GamePix lists Planet Escape as mobile-friendly, so it works with touch on phones and tablets as well as on computers. It is made for portrait mode, so hold your phone upright.

No. Planet Escape runs in your browser right on this page - press Play to start. There is nothing to install and no account to create.
