About Steal Memerot: Zoo Tycoon

Create a bright zoo with rare brainrot animals: buy new creatures, place them on special spots, and earn more money as your collection grows.

Unlock unusual pets, expand your territory, improve income, and turn a small base into a rich zoo where every new creature makes your progress feel bigger.

How to play

Goal: collect a large brainrot animal collection, grow your zoo, unlock rare pets, and increase your income.

Controls: on computer, use WASD to walk, Space to jump, LMB to act, and RMB to rotate the camera. On mobile, use touch and swipe.
