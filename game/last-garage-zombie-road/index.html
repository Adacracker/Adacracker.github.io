About Last Garage: Zombie Road

Start your engine. Last Garage: Zombie Road loads straight into this page as a free driving game. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the arrow keys and the space bar.

Hit the road in Last Garage, a fast-paced 3D zombie survival driving game. Race through shifting districts—from city streets and suburbs to a forest highway—while dodging wrecks, fallen trees, barriers, and evacuation traffic. Fight walkers, armored brutes, and the towering Colossus with machine guns and rockets. Collect scrap, fuel, and ammo during each run, then use your haul to repair and upgrade your vehicle between runs. Choose from four vehicles and improve your armor, engine, weapons, and nitro. Complete challenges and achievements as you push deeper into the outbreak. One of three lanes is always open, but four crashes or an empty tan.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Nitrovenant 2 and Zombie Drift: Steel Rage, give Last Garage: Zombie Road a try: they share the “car” and “action” tags.

How to play Last Garage: Zombie Road

Desktop Use A D or the Left Right Arrow keys to steer Press SPACE for nitro and Q for shockwave Weapons fire automatically when available Mobile Use the on-screen buttons to steer activate nitro and trigger the shockwave.
