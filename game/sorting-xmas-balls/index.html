About Sorting Xmas Balls

Sorting Xmas Balls loads straight into this page as a free puzzle game. It is best played on a computer. You play it with taps.

Sorting Xmas Balls is a cozy winter puzzle game where you sort colorful Christmas ornaments into their correct tubes. Move the balls, group matching colors together, and enjoy calm, festive gameplay.

As you progress, the puzzles become more challenging with more tubes and more colors to organize. Bright graphics and a cheerful holiday theme make every level relaxing and satisfying to complete.

Why you might like it: Fans of Capybara Xmas Merge and Winter Memory may like this one too, since it shares their “christmas” and “sorting” tags.

How to play Sorting Xmas Balls

Tap a tube to pick up the top ball, then tap another tube to place it there. You can move a ball only onto the same color or into an empty tube.

Keep sorting until every tube contains balls of a single color — that’s when the level is complete. Think ahead, plan your moves, and enjoy the warm Christmas atmosphere!
