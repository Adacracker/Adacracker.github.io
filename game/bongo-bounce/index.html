About Bongo Bounce

Got a minute? Bongo Bounce is an arcade game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the arrow keys, the mouse and dragging.

Bongo Bounce is a musical arcade game where every bounce plays the beat. Keep a bouncy slime in the air with your drum, hit the bumpers in time, and the soundtrack builds itself as your groove climbs. Land perfect hits to trigger Fever, collect notes, unlock new slimes and drum kits, and chase a higher score every run.

Why you might like it: Fans of Chaindrop and Stack Bounce 3D may like this one too, since it shares their “arcade” and “music” tags.

How to play Bongo Bounce

Slide the drum left and right to keep the slime from falling.

Mouse or finger: drag anywhere on the screen to move the drum.

Keyboard: arrow keys or A / D.

Hit the slime with the middle of the drum for a Perfect and your groove goes up. The higher the groove, the fuller the music and the bigger the score. Miss and you lose a heart. Level up mid run to pick a boost.
