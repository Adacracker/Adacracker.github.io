About Zombie Drift: Steel Rage

Zombie Drift: Steel Rage is a post-apocalyptic 3D action game that mixes arcade drifting with arena survival. Drive an armored battle car into a ruined city arena full of zombie hordes and mutant giants. Speed and controlled slides are your weapons: the sharper the drift, the faster your nitro rage builds and the harder your spiked bumper hits - up to 2.5x ramming damage. Mow down walkers and runners, blow up barrels and collect scrap gears. Campaign of 100 levels in 10 chapters with 20 titan bosses, plus endless survival mode. Unlock the Iron Fang, the Dune Reaper buggy and the heavy War Titan, upgrade armor and turbo, and pick weapon modules between waves: miniguns, buzzsaws, flamethrowers and rocket mortars.
