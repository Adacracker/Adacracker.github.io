About Munch Mob

Ready to run the show? Munch Mob is a simulation game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the space bar, the mouse and taps.

Steer a swarm of hungry clay gremlins and devour a whole miniature world, one bite at a time! Munch gnomes, cars, carnival rides, ships and entire skyscrapers across 7 handcrafted claymation dioramas: cozy suburbs, downtown blocks, a busy harbor, a night carnival, a mega mall, a city plaza and the grand skyline. Grow your mob from tiny crumb snackers to towering city eaters across 5 tiers, chain juicy combos, unleash feeding frenzies, grab power pickups and catch the Golden Rush events while staying ahead of the cleanup crew. Unlock 8 gremlin types with unique powers, buy upgrades in the Goo Shop, fill the devourable codex, earn 21 clay badge.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Monster Café: Idle Tycoon and Boo Brew, give Munch Mob a try: they share the “monster” and “casual” tags.

How to play Munch Mob

Move your mouse or drag on the touchscreen to steer the mob Tap the frenzy meter or press Space to unleash a Feeding Frenzy when charged P or ESC pauses M toggles sound.
