About Koron Domino

Koron Domino loads straight into this page as a free board or card game. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the mouse and taps.

Rotate the tiles, connect the domino path to every bell, then tap "Push!" and watch the dominoes topple in one satisfying chain - clack, clack, ding! Koron Domino is a relaxing brain-teaser with 150 hand-made levels and a new Daily Puzzle every day. No time limits: think at your own pace. Branching paths that ring several bells at once will test your logic, and stars unlock cute domino skins (sakura, matcha, ramune and more). Available in English, Japanese, Chinese and Korean.

Why you might like it: Fans of Checkmate Rewind and Ultimate TicTacToe may like this one too, since it shares their “logic” and “domino” tags.

How to play Koron Domino

Tap (or click) a tile to rotate it 90 degrees. Connect the domino path from the start to every bell. When the path is complete, tap "Push!" to topple the dominoes and clear the level.
