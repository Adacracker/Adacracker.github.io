About Arrow Sand Puzzle

Puzzle time. Arrow Sand Puzzle is a puzzle game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the arrow keys, the mouse and taps.

Arrow Sand Puzzle combines arrow puzzles with colorful sand sorting. Tap an arrow with a clear path to send it out of the board and watch it turn into flowing sand. Fill the matching buckets below while keeping the conveyor belt from overflowing.

Think ahead, choose your moves carefully, and work through 100 levels. Use Hammer, Shuffle, and Clear boosters when you need a little help. Simple tap controls make it easy to start, while layered arrow layouts give you plenty to puzzle over.

Why you might like it: Fans of Orbit Pals and Atomic Puzzle XMas may like this one too, since it shares their “logic” and “colorful” tags.

How to play Arrow Sand Puzzle

Desktop: Left-click an arrow to move it in the direction it points. Click a booster to use it.

Mobile: Tap an arrow or a booster. An arrow can leave only when its path is clear.
