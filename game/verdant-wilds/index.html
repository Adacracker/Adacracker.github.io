About Verdant Wilds

Ready to run the show? You can play Verdant Wilds, a simulation game from our Build & Simulate category, free in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

Explore an isometric survival voxel world. Gather wood, stone and ores, craft tools, build campfires and shelters, mine deep caves, and discover mysterious dimensions in Verdant Wilds!

Why you might like it: Verdant Wilds has a lot in common with EmberCraft and Blockland Oneblock Skyblock - they share the “minecraft” and “crafting” tags.

How to play Verdant Wilds

WASD or Arrow keys to move Touch joystick on mobile Left Click or E to gather and attack C for Crafting I for Inventory M for Map Q to Eat Space to jump or swim up.
