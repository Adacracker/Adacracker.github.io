About Cage Ballers Football

You can play Cage Ballers Football, a sports game from our Sports category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

Step into the cage and take on the CPU in a fast, 3D small-sided football match. Choose a pitch, team kit, match length, camera and difficulty, then pass, sprint, tackle and shoot your way to victory. Play two halves and, if the score is level, go to golden goal. Switch players, watch goal replays and check match stats after the whistle. Supports keyboard and gamepad controls.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Rocket Car Football Championship and Curve Kick, give Cage Ballers Football a try: they share the “soccer” and “multiplayer” tags.

How to play Cage Ballers Football

Keyboard WASD or arrow keys to move Shift to sprint hold and release Space to shoot E to pass R for a through ball Space or F to tackle while defending Q or Tab to switch players C to change camera Esc or P to pause Gamepad left stick to move.
