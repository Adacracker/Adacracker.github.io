About Sniper for Brainrot

Steady your aim. Sniper for Brainrot loads straight into this page as a free shooting game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar. It can also be played by two people on one device.

Use your sniper rifle to hit the balloon above a Brainrot, instantly transform into it, and race to the Safe Zone before a massive tsunami catches you. The farther you shoot, the rarer the Brainrot you can collect but the faster the wave becomes. Build your collection, expand your Base, unlock powerful sniper rifles, customize your character, and compete with friends in this addictive Brainrot adventure featuring 87 unique collectibles!

Why you might like it: Sniper for Brainrot has a lot in common with Tung Tung Sahur The Sniper Hitman and Cold Iron: Last Extraction - they share the “sniper” and “shooting” tags.
