About Urban Jump

Quick fun ahead. Urban Jump loads straight into this page as a free arcade game. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the space bar.

URBAN JUMP is a fast-paced, gritty, and unforgiving arcade platformer where only one thing matters - climbing higher than everyone else. Jump across rooftops, balconies, and crumbling platforms, collect coins, avoid falling, and try to beat your high score. Every meter you climb brings greater risk, faster action, and less room for mistakes. Unlock new characters, earn coins, and keep pushing higher through the concrete urban jungle. One bad jump and you’re going all the way down.
