About GOBLOX: Grow Your Empire

Welcome to Sunberry Valley! Restore a forgotten garden and grow it into the greatest farming empire in the land. Plant seeds, harvest crops and sell them for coins. Then reinvest in rarer seeds, bigger plots and new regions. Weather changes everything: rain, thunderstorms, rainbow rain, meteor showers and aurora nights can mutate your crops into Gilded, Frostbound, Starborn and other valuable variants.
