About Drag Shift: Street Race

Buckle up: Drag Shift: Street Race is a free driving game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the space bar, taps and touch.

Master the launch, nail every shift and burn NOS in Drag Shift: Street Race, a precision neon drag-racing game. Climb 3 districts and 60 events, beat 18 rival crew drivers, tune 25 cars with 5 upgrade paths, and chase your own ghost for the perfect run. Easy to learn, hard to master — how low can your ET go?

Why you might like it: Drag Shift: Street Race has a lot in common with Monsters Wheels 2 and GapSnap - they share the “car” tag.

How to play Drag Shift: Street Race

LAUNCH: Hold GAS (or Up/W) and keep the needle in the green zone before the lights turn green.

SHIFT: Tap SHIFT UP (or Space) when it glows green — perfect shifts are faster and boost torque.

NOS: Tap NOS (or N) from 2nd gear up. Higher gears benefit more.

Touch buttons on mobile, keyboard on desktop. Beat the rival, earn cash, upgrade your car!
