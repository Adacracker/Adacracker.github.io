About Hexshot ARENA

HEXSHOT ARENA is a first-person voxel hero shooter where building and destruction are part of the fight. Raise cover, mine through walls and pick one of ten classes, each with its own weapon, two abilities and an ultimate. Battle across nine arenas in team deathmatch, free-for-all, round-based elimination, control points and payload escort. Play solo against bots or online with up to ten players.

How to play

Move with WASD or the arrow keys and press SPACE to jump. Aim with the mouse, hold the left mouse button to fire and use the right mouse button for your alternate fire. SHIFT and E trigger your two class abilities; Q fires your ultimate once it is charged and unlocked at level 6. Press C to place blocks, F to mine blocks or strike in melee, R to reload and B to toggle the building preview. Hold TAB for the scoreboard and press P to pause. Gamepads are supported.
