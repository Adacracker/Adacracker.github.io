About Arrow Slide

Fancy a brain-teaser? Arrow Slide loads straight into this page as a free puzzle game. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the arrow keys and taps.

Arrow Slide is a satisfying grid-based puzzle where you tap colorful arrow tiles to slide them off the board. Each tile is shaped like a snake with an arrowhead pointing in one direction — tap it and the whole thing slithers along its path until it disappears off the edge. Clear every tile from the board to complete the level, but be careful — if a tile bumps into another and cannot exit, you lose a life.

Why you might like it: Arrow Slide has a lot in common with Santa Quest and Cozy Tiles – Relaxing Picture Puzzle - they share the “sliding-puzzle” and “relaxing” tags.

How to play Arrow Slide

Tap any arrow tile to slide it in the direction its arrowhead points.

Bent arrows slither snake-style — the body follows the head along its path.

A tile can only exit if its path is clear all the way off the board.

Tapping a blocked tile costs one life and bumps it back.

Clear every tile from the grid to complete the level.

Use Hint to reveal a safe move, or Eraser to remove a blocking tile.

You start with three lives — lose them all and it's game over.
