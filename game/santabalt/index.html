About Santabalt

Santabalt is a free arcade game that runs right in your browser. You can play it on a computer or a touch screen. It is played with the mouse and taps.

Dive into the festive spirit with Santabalt, an exhilarating endless runner with Santa at the helm. Leap between rooftops, dodging obstacles at thrilling speed with just a tap or click. Hone your timing and reflexes, extending Santa's journey in this addictive holiday adventure loaded with jolly excitement.

Why you might like it: It sits in our Christmas Games alongside Bff Christmas Cookie Challenge and Mr Santa Run 2, so if you like those, try this one.

Santabalt: FAQ

Does Santabalt cost anything?

No, it costs nothing. Santabalt is free to play in your browser on Arcade Cabin. It is supplied by GamePix, and the provider may show ads before or during the game.

Can I play Santabalt on a tablet?

Yes. GamePix lists Santabalt as mobile-friendly, so it works with touch on phones and tablets as well as on computers. It uses a wide landscape screen.

Do I need to download Santabalt?

No. Santabalt runs in your browser right on this page - press Play to start. There is nothing to install and no account to create.
