About Biblox Online

Adventure awaits. Biblox Online is a free adventure game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with W A S D and the space bar.

Explore unique worlds through a series of exciting mini-games.:

Mini Obby: Overcome dizzying obstacle courses, testing your agility and parkour skills!

Speed Simulator: Run, collect speed particles and accelerate to an incredible pace! Your character will become faster with every step!

Color Tycoon: Buy upgrades for your base, get items and build a huge building!

Why you might like it: Fans of Santa Parkour and Ninja Parkour Multiplayer may like this one too, since it shares their “parkour” and “runner” tags.

How to play Biblox Online

Moving to a PC:

W, A, S, D — the movement of the character forward, left, backward and right.

The space bar is a jump.

Movement on the phone is done through the buttons on the phone.

Biblox Online: FAQ

Does Biblox Online cost anything?

No, it costs nothing. Biblox Online is free to play in your browser on Arcade Cabin. It is supplied by GameDistribution, and the provider may show ads before or during the game.

Can I play Biblox Online on my phone?

GameDistribution does not list Biblox Online as mobile-friendly, so it is best played on a computer using W A S D and the space bar.

No. Biblox Online runs in your browser right on this page - press Play to start. There is nothing to install and no account to create.
