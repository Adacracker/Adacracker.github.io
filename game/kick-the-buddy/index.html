About Kick The Buddy

Kick The Buddy is a free action game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the arrow keys, the mouse and dragging.

Relieve your stress and unleash your creativity in this ultimate interactive action game! Kick the Buddy is the perfect browser game for anyone looking to unwind after a long day. If you have ever wanted to smash, punch, or explode something without any real-world consequences, this is the free game for you. Dive into a satisfying playground where you interact with a resilient ragdoll buddy using an arsenal of creative tools.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Kick the Zombie: Ragdoll Sandbox and Rocketman, give Kick The Buddy a try: they share the “ragdoll” tag.

How to play Kick The Buddy

Or the mouse controls, you simply click on Buddy to hit him. You can also click and drag to stretch him, throw him around or shake him. The scroll wheel or arrow keys sometimes let you rotate the view depending on the version.
