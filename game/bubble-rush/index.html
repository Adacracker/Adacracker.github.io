About Bubble Rush

Fancy a brain-teaser? Bubble Rush loads straight into this page as a free puzzle game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the mouse and touch.

Aim from the center and pop colorful bubbles in Bubble Rush. Match bubbles of the same color, plan each shot, and clear the board. Enjoy quick puzzle rounds with simple mouse and touch controls. Use bonus options to refill or continue after an ordinary ad.
