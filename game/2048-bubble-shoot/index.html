About 2048 Bubble Shoot

Fancy a brain-teaser? 2048 Bubble Shoot is a puzzle game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it.

2048 Bubble Shoot combines bouncing ball action with the classic 2048 merging mechanic. Fire balls into the field, hit numbered blocks and watch them bounce around the board. Collect the balls through the pipe and merge matching numbers to create higher values. Aim carefully, use ricochets to your advantage and keep increasing your score!

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Cube Snake 2048 and Xmas Downhill, give 2048 Bubble Shoot a try: they share the “2048” and “ball” tags.

How to play 2048 Bubble Shoot

Aim and shoot balls from the cannon into the playing field. Balls bounce off walls and objects, damaging or interacting with numbered blocks along the way. When balls enter the pipe, matching values can merge into a higher number. Plan your shots, create useful ricochets and keep merging numbers to reach higher values and earn the best score.
