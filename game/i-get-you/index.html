About I Get You

I Get You is a troll platformer where the level itself is your opponent. It has eyes, it talks, and every trap is something its body does: the floor yawns under your feet, the ground gets goosebumps, it sneezes you off a ledge, and doors turn into teeth. Every death teaches you its next trick. Beat a level and it gets deleted forever. 40 levels across two corridors, and the deleted ones come back. They’re mad. Short levels, instant respawn, and a death counter that never forgets.
