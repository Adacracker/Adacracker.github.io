About 2048 Ball Arena

Puzzle time. 2048 Ball Arena is a puzzle game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

Roll through a colorful 3D arena, collect numbered balls and merge matching values into larger ones. Dodge division traps and hungry holes, use speed, magnet and x2 power-ups, and outgrow 18 active AI rivals. Build the strongest chain, climb the live leaderboard and set a new high score in every run.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed 2048 Bubble Shoot and Cube Snake 2048, give 2048 Ball Arena a try: they share the “2048” and “ball” tags.

How to play 2048 Ball Arena

Use WASD or the Arrow keys to steer on desktop, or move the mouse around the center of the screen. On touch devices, drag anywhere outside the buttons to steer. Hold Space, the left mouse button or the Boost button to accelerate. Collect balls whose value does not exceed your leading ball, merge equal numbers, and avoid larger rivals and traps.
