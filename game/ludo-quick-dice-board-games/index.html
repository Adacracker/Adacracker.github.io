About Ludo Quick: Dice Board Games

Ludo Quick: Dice Board Games loads straight into this page as a free board or card game. It is best played on a computer. You play it with taps. It can also be played by two people on one device.

Ludo Quick: Dice Board Games brings classic Ludo to your phone with fast, smooth, and exciting gameplay. Roll the dice, move your tokens, and race to get them home before your opponents. Enjoy offline play, smart opponents, simple controls, colorful boards, and a perfect mix of luck and strategy. Play quick matches anytime and become the ultimate Ludo champion!

Why you might like it: Ludo Quick: Dice Board Games has a lot in common with Scopa and Domino Peak - they share the “board” and “multiplayer” tags.

How to play Ludo Quick: Dice Board Games

Tap the Roll Dice button to roll the dice. When a valid move is available, tap the token you want to move. Plan your moves carefully, protect your tokens, and capture opponent tokens by landing on their position. Controls are simple and easy to learn, making Ludo Quick fun for players of all ages.
