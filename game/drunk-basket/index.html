About Drunk Basket

Warm up first: Drunk Basket is a sports game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the arrow keys. It can also be played by two people on one device.

Drunk Basket is a hilarious 1-2 player active-ragdoll basketball game. Take control of wobbly, physics-driven players and time your shots to sink baskets before your rival play solo against the AI or go head-to-head with a friend on the same device. Every round throws in a random twist, from low gravity to a giant ball, keeping every match unpredictable and fun.

Why you might like it: Drunk Basket has a lot in common with Volley Clash and Toy Soccer Cup - they share the “sports” and “2 player games” tags.

How to play Drunk Basket

Player 1 Hold W to charge your shot release to throw Player 2 Hold the Up Arrow key to charge your shot release to throw On mobile use the on-screen buttons instead of the keyboard Get close to the ball to grab it then release inside the green zo.
