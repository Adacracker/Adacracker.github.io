About Extreme Quad Bike Racing

Extreme Quad Bike Racing is a fast 3D ATV racing game with 12 challenging levels and an endless mode. Choose from three quad bikes and race through four different environments. Steer through traffic and obstacles, collect coins, use nitro for extra speed, and complete each mission before you crash.

How to play

A D or Left Right Arrow keys steer W Up Arrow or Right Ctrl nitro S Down Arrow or Space brake Mobile tilt to steer and use the on-screen nitro and brake buttons Mouse or touch navigate menus.
