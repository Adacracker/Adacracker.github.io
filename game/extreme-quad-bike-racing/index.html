About Extreme Quad Bike Racing

Start your engine. You can play Extreme Quad Bike Racing, a driving game from our Cars & Racing category, free in your browser. It runs on desktop and mobile alike. It is played with the arrow keys, the space bar and the mouse. On a phone, turn it sideways for the wide landscape screen.
