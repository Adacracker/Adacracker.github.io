About Mystic Link

Fancy a brain-teaser? Mystic Link is a puzzle game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with taps and dragging.

Connect 3 or more matching jewels to clear them!

Each clear uses 1 Move, and the game ends when your Moves reach 0.

Make long chains to create bombs.

Tap bombs or connect them with jewels to activate them.

Create even longer chains to make bombs that restore Moves.

Clear jewels to fill the Skill and Fever gauges.

When a gauge is full, a support skill activates!

Why you might like it: Mystic Link has a lot in common with Jewel Christmas Mania and Magic & Wizards Match 2 - they share the “matching” and “jewels” tags.

How to play Mystic Link

Drag to connect matching jewels.
