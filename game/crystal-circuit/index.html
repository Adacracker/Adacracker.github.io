About Crystal Circuit

Crystal Circuit is an arcade game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the arrow keys, the space bar and the mouse.

Crystal Circuit is a fast-paced arcade game of pure timing. Crystals circle endlessly around a glowing circuit, and a queue of crystals waits at the entrance — launch every one of them onto the track without causing a crash. Time each launch into a gap between the crystals already looping the circuit; hit one and your crystal shatters, restarting the level. Clear your whole queue to complete the level and keep climbing through faster, busier circuits with new track layouts as you go.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Overkill: Top Down Arcade Shooter and Firefly Night, give Crystal Circuit a try: they share the “arcade” and “reflex” tags.

How to play Crystal Circuit

Tap, click, or press Space / W / Up Arrow to launch the next crystal onto the circuit. Wait for a gap in the moving crystals, then launch — that's the whole game, and it's all timing. Fail a level 3 times and helper buttons appear at the top-left: skip the level, or slow the circuit down. Top-right holds the mute button and, once you unlock your first crystal, the collection drawer where you can equip crystal skins.
