About Claw Machine : Animal Friends

Grab cute plush animals in a colorful 3D claw machine! Move the claw, drop it at the right moment and carry your catch to the prize chute. Collect 180 plush toys across 20 themed groups, from pets and farm animals to the jungle, the Arctic and the deep sea. Level up to unlock new machines, complete daily missions, and use Bomb, Hurricane and Shake boosters when the pile gets tricky.

How to play

Mouse or touch. Drag the pink button to move the claw, then press the yellow button to drop it and grab a toy. Use the side buttons for boosters, your bag, missions and Auto mode.
