About Streets of Rage: Neon District

Ready for some action? Streets of Rage: Neon District is an action game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

Fight through eight neon-lit district chapters as Rhea or Vale in this 2D street brawler. Mix punches, kicks and airborne attacks, dodge enemy windups, grab and throw opponents and pick up weapons.
