About Pedro: Double Cross

Lock and load: Pedro: Double Cross loads straight into this page as a free shooting game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the space bar, the mouse and touch.

Run the rooftops and expose a hidden manifest in Pedro: Double Cross, a 2D action shooter starring dual-pistol agent Noor. Explore forty connected scenes, clear each room to open its exit, and improvise with reflective rails, laser relays, breakable glass and cables. Split your aim, dodge attacks, reload behind cover and use Focus to slow the action during a risky crossing. Every shot needs a fresh press, rewarding timing and accuracy over blind spray. Detailed character animation, responsive combat effects, industrial ambience and an original electronic groove accompany the chase. Checkpoints save locally, with keyboard and mouse or touch controls available.
