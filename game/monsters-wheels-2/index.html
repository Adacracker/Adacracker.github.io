About Monsters Wheels 2

Monsters Wheels 2 is a driving game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the arrow keys and the space bar.

Monsters Wheels 2 is a monster truck game where every event asks something different. Race a packed grid to the finish line, flatten a row of parked cars, land the biggest backflip you can or beat the clock — 24 levels in total, spread across jungles, a beach, an amusement park and the Arizona desert. Three monster trucks are available, each with upgrades for engine, suspension, tyres and nitro. Prize money from every event goes straight into the garage.

Why you might like it: Fans of Monster Truck 3D Winter and Monster Truck Offroad 2 may like this one too, since it shares their “car” and “truck” tags.

How to play Monsters Wheels 2

PC:

Arrow Up / W — accelerate.

Arrow Down / S — brake and reverse.

Arrow Left / Right — lean back and forward in the air.

Space / X — nitro.

Mobile:

Acceleration - automatic.

Nitro button - Nitro.

Tilt buttons - rotate left/right.
