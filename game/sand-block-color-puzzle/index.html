About Sand Block Color Puzzle

Sand Block Color Puzzle loads straight into this page as a free puzzle game. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the mouse, touch and dragging.

Slide colorful trays beneath matching sand, fill them, and clear the board before time runs out. Solve 600 puzzles with new shapes and obstacles, and use magnet, hammer, or time-freeze boosters when you need help. Play with a mouse on desktop or drag with your finger on mobile. How to play Sand Block Color Puzzle

Mouse: Click and drag trays. Touch: Hold and drag trays. Move trays beneath matching sand to fill and clear them. Clear all trays before time runs out. Use on-screen buttons for boosters and menus.
