About Prism Blast 3D

Fancy a brain-teaser? Prism Blast 3D is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. It is played with taps and dragging.

Prism Blast 3D is a fresh take on block puzzle — played on a round 3D board instead of the usual square grid. Drag blocks onto the rings, line them up to blast whole rings and spokes, and keep the creeping grey junk from closing in. Chain clears for combos, spend power-ups when you get stuck, and work through score, junk-clear and survival goals. Easy to learn, satisfying to master.

Why you might like it: Prism Blast 3D has a lot in common with PrismRoll 3D and Arrows Disappear - they share the “logic” and “block” tags.

How to play Prism Blast 3D

Drag a block from the tray onto the round board. Fill a whole ring or spoke to clear it. Press a cell to aim, release to blast (tap outside to cancel). Grey junk creeps in from the outside — only a blast clears it. Use the power-ups below the board: ↩ Undo, Refresh tray, Swap, ⏳ +5 Turns, Hammer (tap a cell to smash). Don't run out of room to place the next block.
