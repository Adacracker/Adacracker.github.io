About Scraps!

Puzzle time. Scraps! is a puzzle game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the space bar.

Place hex tiles and connect matching colours to score. Level a colour to earn a merge powerup. Nothing clears — the run ends when nothing fits.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Mine Hunt Classic and Stealth Silent Squid, give Scraps! a try: they share the “logic” and “halloween” tags.

How to play Scraps!

Place Hex tiles on the grid.

Match same colors to gain points.

Level up each color score to get merge powerup.

Merge and clear space!
