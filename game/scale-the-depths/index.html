About Scale The Depths

Pack your bags: Scale The Depths loads straight into this page as a free adventure game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it.

Scale the Depths brings you into a peaceful underwater adventure where fishing becomes a relaxing and rewarding experience. This charming fishing game lets you explore quiet oceans, cast your hook, discover different sea creatures, and enjoy a simple catch-and-feed journey. If you love fishing simulator games, relaxing ocean exploration, and calming collection gameplay, this underwater world is waiting for you!

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Cozy Gnomes, give Scale The Depths a try: they share the “relax” tag.

How to play Scale The Depths

Choose a location and send your hook into the water. Move carefully through the depths and try to catch different types of fish and sea creatures. Bring your catches back and gather different resources from the ocean. Go to the kitchen area, clean the fish by removing scales, and turn them into meat.
