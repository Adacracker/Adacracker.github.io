About Arena Strike Battle

You will have a shootout in the arena with enemies. Choose any weapon, destroy your opponents.

Different types of weapons, characters and arena maps are already waiting, it's going to be hot! No distracting events, just endless gunfights!

Destroy as many opponents as possible to collect glory points and take the first place in the leaderboard.

How to play

PC Controls:

Use the arrow keys or WASD keys to move.

Move the mouse to aim.

Left-click to fire.

Mobile Controls:

Use the left blue stick to move.

Use the right red stick to aim.

Release the right red stick to fire.

Destroy as many enemies as possible.
