About Descent

Quick fun ahead. Descent is a free arcade game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the arrow keys, the space bar and dragging.

DESCENT is a downhill mountain-bike time trial. One mountain, . kilometres of hand-routed trail and metres of vertical drop: berms, rock gardens, root sections, jump lines and a final sprint. The bike is simulated for real — progressive fork and shock, per-wheel grip, load transfer, pumping and air control. Read the trail, carry speed through the berms, keep the wheels on dirt, and take down your personal best split by split. Chase the clock in Timed Runs or explore the hillside in Free Ride.

Why you might like it: Descent has a lot in common with Claw Machine : Animal Friends and Sprinkle Roll - they share the “arcade” tag.

How to play Descent

Keyboard: W/S or arrows — pedal and brake, A/D — lean, Space — pump/jump, R — restart, Esc — pause. Gamepad is supported. On the descent, braking before a corner and leaning through it is faster than dragging the brakes. Pump the backsides of rollers to gain speed without pedalling. Crashes cost seconds, not the run — get back on and finish. The default track is the ranked one: only it feeds the leaderboard.
