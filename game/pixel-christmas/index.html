About Pixel Christmas

Puzzle time. Pixel Christmas is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the space bar.

Pixel Christmas is the game that brings joy to all!

A pile of gifts falls from the sky and paints your screen with festive cheer!

But be careful!

Each package is waiting to be lined up to free up space for new gifts!

Load up with presents and increase your score!

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Atomic Puzzle XMas and Christmas Memory Match, give Pixel Christmas a try: they share the “christmas” tag.

How to play Pixel Christmas

Change the shape of the falling packages and fit them into place.

Each complete line you make will disappear to make room for other blocks.

The more tiles you line up, the higher your score and chances of staying in the game.
