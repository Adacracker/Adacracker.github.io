About Winter Wonderland Mahjong

Winter Wonderland Mahjong loads straight into this page as a free board or card game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it.

Escape into a cozy Winter Wonderland filled with snowflakes, penguins, and festive cheer! Relax with calming music as you match beautifully crafted tiles in this soothing Mahjong puzzle game. Unlock charming new backgrounds and unique tile designs as you progress, each adding warmth and wonder to your journey. Perfect for unwinding, celebrating the holidays, and discovering the magic of winter—one match at a time.

Why you might like it: Winter Wonderland Mahjong has a lot in common with Xmas Mahjong Trio Solitaire and Xmas Mahjong Tiles 2023 - they share the “christmas” and “winter” tags.

How to play Winter Wonderland Mahjong

Find and match identical winter-themed tiles, making sure each one is free on at least one side to clear it from the snowy board. Remove all tiles to complete each frosty layout and bring harmony to the Winter Wonderland! Use Shuffle, Hint, or Undo if you get stuck, and earn stars as you journey through endless cozy puzzles. Relax and enjoy the serene beauty of the snow-covered world in this heartwarming Mahjong adventure!
