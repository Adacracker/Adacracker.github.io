About Dangerous Turn

Test your reflexes in a fast arcade challenge with simple controls and steadily increasing difficulty. Your hero travels along a winding path where every new section may hide a trap, obstacle, or useful bonus. Collect coins, avoid fire, ice, and spikes, use power-ups, and try to travel as far as possible. Spend collected coins to unlock new characters. Improve your high score, choose your favorite hero, and return for a daily reward. Quick sessions make the game suitable for both a short break and a longer high-score run.

How to play

Tap the screen or click the left mouse button to change direction Your hero moves automatically along a diagonal path React early to avoid falling or hitting obstacles Collect coins and useful items along the way.
