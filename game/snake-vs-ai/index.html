About Snake VS AI

Got a minute? Snake VS AI is an arcade game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the arrow keys, W A S D and the mouse.

Slither out of your land on a bright 3D island, draw a loop with your trail and close it back home to claim everything inside. AI snakes, each with its own personality, race you for the same ground. Touch an AIs trail to knock it out, but if any of them touches yours, youre out! Grab the most land before the timer ends, or take over half the island to win instantly. Win matches to climb from Beginner to Pro and Elite, where the maps get bigger and you face up to 10 AI snakes at once. Reach Elite to unlock Unlimited mode: no timer, just you against the AI until someone gets you. Collect coins, unlock colourful snake skins, and use speed boosts, a bigger starting base or a trail shield to turn the tables. Psst... type the right secret name and you might unlock a T-Rex or Dragon that stomps across the map! Steer with mouse, WASD or arrow keys, or drag on mobile.

Why you might like it: Snake VS AI has a lot in common with Snake Block Rush and Chubby Duck Jump - they share the “arcade” and “snake” tags.
