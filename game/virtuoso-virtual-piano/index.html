How to play Virtuoso: Virtual Piano

How to Play:

Desktop: Use your mouse to click the falling notes or press the corresponding keys on your keyboard (D, F, G, H, J, K).

Mobile/Tablet: Tap the screen directly where the notes fall.

Goal:

Hit the notes exactly when they reach the bottom line to score points.

Timing matters! Aim for "Perfect" hits to build your Combo and get the highest score.

Avoid missing notes to keep the music playing beautifully.
