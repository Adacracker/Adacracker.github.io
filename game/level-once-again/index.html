About Level Once Again

Pack your bags: Level Once Again is an adventure game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the arrow keys and the space bar.

Level Once Again is a story-driven puzzle platformer with 100 rooms, 4 worlds and 7 bosses. Kaal has kidnapped Tara — guide Veer through 100 trapped rooms to rescue her. Same room, same door, but a brand new trick every time: floors drop, spikes pop up, doors run away. Only the first 3 rooms are easy — after that, every room is a trap. Features: - 100 handcrafted rooms across 4 worlds (Dungeon, Workshop, Tower, The Rift) - 7 boss battles with unique patterns - Story scenes (skippable) — English and Hindi - Hint system: stuck? Watch a short video ad to see the full room solution - Progress auto-saves, with export/import backup - Keyboard, gam.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Rope Chef and 100 Doors: Mystery Room Escape, give Level Once Again a try: they share the “brain” and “logic” tags.
