About Moto Extreme: Halloween Land

Start your engine. Moto Extreme: Halloween Land is a driving game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the arrow keys.

In this Halloween-themed 3D side-scrolling motorcycle game, an exciting riding experience awaits you on dark and mysterious tracks. Overcome challenging obstacles, keep your motorcycle balanced, and complete a variety of dangerous tracks. Carefully navigate through a world designed with a spooky atmosphere, use your riding skills, and try to reach the end of each track. Control your motorcycle, overcome the obstacles, and join the adventure in the eerie atmosphere of Halloween.

Why you might like it: Moto Extreme: Halloween Land has a lot in common with Magline Moto and Dirt Bike Trail Rider - they share the “moto” and “motorcycle” tags.

How to play Moto Extreme: Halloween Land

Use the W and Arrow Up keys to move forward Use the S and Arrow Down keys to move backward Use the A and D keys or the Arrow Left and Arrow Right keys to balance the motorcycle.
