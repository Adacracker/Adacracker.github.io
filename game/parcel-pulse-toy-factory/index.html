About Parcel Pulse Toy Factory

Got a minute? Parcel Pulse Toy Factory loads straight into this page as a free arcade game. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the arrow keys, the mouse and taps.

Parcel Pulse is a colorful conveyor-belt puzzle set in a busy toy factory. Boxes roll along branching belts, and you control the switches that decide where each one goes. Flip the arrows at the right moment and every box lands in the dock with the same color. The factory grows with you across 200 handcrafted levels. Use the Magnet, Speed and Freeze power-ups when the belts get busy, earn stars and coins, complete daily missions and unlock new avatars and frames.
