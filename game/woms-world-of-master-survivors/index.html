About WOMS World of Master Survivors

WOMS World of Master Survivors is a free action game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the arrow keys, W A S D and the mouse.

The city fell. You are the last line of defense holding the street corner. Endless zombie waves never stop. Surviving pays for your upgrades between waves: heavier ammo, cover drones, turrets, and gunship calls. City Siege: Urban choke point survival. Fortified Compound: Double the horde, four corner turrets. Daily Quests: 3 fresh daily challenges rewarding smart play.
