About Cube Snake 2048

Cube Snake 2048 is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. It runs on desktop and mobile alike. On a phone, turn it sideways for the wide landscape screen.

Get a bigger number by collecting free cubes and eating other players with a smaller number than you. Your cubes with the same value that bump into one another will merge.

Why you might like it: Fans of CuboMunch 2048 and 2048 Decks may like this one too, since it shares their “2048” and “snake” tags.

How to play Cube Snake 2048

Merge blocks to grow.

Slide around the arena picking up blocks to get bigger. Picking up two of the same number will merge them into a single block as the total sum of both.
