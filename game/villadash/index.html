About Villadash

Fast hands help here. Villadash is a free action game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

Morvane is carrying off the villagers of the kingdom, and each one he takes makes him stronger. Pick one of three heroes, race through the village against the clock, beat the guards and cut the captives free before Morvane reaches them. Every villager you save gives you more strength for the duel at the end of the round. 7 villages, each with a new twist, heroes that level up, and a final battle where you switch between all three.

Why you might like it: Fans of Office Knight 3D: Castle Defense and Ricochet Knight may like this one too, since it shares their “knight” and “hero” tags.

How to play Villadash

Move: WASD or arrow keys. Attack: left mouse button or J. Ability: right mouse button, R or K. Dodge: Space or Shift. Free villagers: hold F. Rotate camera: Q / E. Zoom: mouse wheel. Pause: P. Final battle: 1, 2, 3 to switch heroes. Gamepad supported. Mobile: left joystick to move and on-screen buttons to attack, use your ability, dodge and free villagers.
