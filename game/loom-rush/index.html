About Loom Rush

Got a minute? Loom Rush loads straight into this page as a free arcade game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the mouse, touch and dragging.

Loom Rush is an arcade game where you draw loops around swarms of blobs to pop them. The more blobs you catch in one loop, the bigger the multiplier. Blobs chase you, and every touch costs a life. Red cutters bounce around and snap your trail. After each wave, pick one of three upgrades. Between runs, spend sparks on permanent boosts.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Sketchtone and Color Splash Party, give Loom Rush a try: they share the “drawing” tag.

How to play Loom Rush

Hold the left mouse button and move to draw a trail. On mobile, touch and drag. Close the trail into a loop around blobs to pop them all at once. More blobs in one loop means a bigger multiplier. Blobs chase you and cost a life on touch. Red cutters snap your trail, so keep them away from it.
