About Spooky Vending Machine 2

Fancy a brain-teaser? Spooky Vending Machine 2 is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the mouse, taps and touch.

Pick an item, enter its code, and collect your Halloween treat! Spooky Vending Machine 2 is packed with cute monster toys, colorful candy, and delightfully creepy snacks. Browse the shelves, enter the code for your favorite item, confirm your choice, and pay the displayed price to collect it. How to play Spooky Vending Machine 2

Use your mouse or touchscreen Click or tap the keypad to enter an items code press the ENTER button then click or tap the coins to pay the displayed price Click or tap the pickup hatch to collect your item.
