About Winter CosmoFest

Winter CosmoFest is a free dress-up game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the mouse and touch.

Immerse yourself in a magical winter adventure in the Honkai universe. Show your creativity by creating festive cosplay outfits for the heroes of the game. Combine gorgeous costumes, accessories and hairstyles to capture the spirit of your favorite characters among snowflakes and winter landscapes. Show off your fashion skills by choosing the perfect ensemble for the winter cosplay festival. Win the hearts of Honkai fans and share your ideas with your friends!

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Winter Fairy and New Year Makeup Trends, give Winter CosmoFest a try: they share the “winter” and “makeup” tags.

How to play Winter CosmoFest

The girl and her boyfriend are fond of cosplay. This time they decided to create vivid images based on the popular anime game. To do this, you need to apply makeup, buy appropriate costumes and, of course, add accessories. And in order to better get used to their image, they came up with an unusual game... The result can be saved as a PNG image. Depending on the gaming device, a computer mouse click or a simple touch on touch screens is used to control it.
