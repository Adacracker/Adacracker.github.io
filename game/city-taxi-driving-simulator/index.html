About City Taxi Driving Simulator

Start your engine. City Taxi Driving Simulator is a free driving game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the arrow keys, the space bar and touch.

Drive a yellow taxi through busy city streets, follow checkpoints, pick up passengers, and deliver them safely before time expires. Choose vehicles, complete challenging routes, avoid traffic, collect rewards, and unlock new driving missions. Master smooth steering, careful braking, nitro boosts, and camera changes in this colorful urban taxi adventure.

Why you might like it: Fans of Warehouse Wobble 3D and Extreme Gear Shift Racing may like this one too, since it shares their “driving” and “car” tags.

How to play City Taxi Driving Simulator

Use W or Up Arrow to accelerate Use S or Down Arrow to brake and reverse Steer with A D or Left Right Arrow Press Space for the handbrake either Shift key for nitro C to change the camera and Escape to pause Touch controls remain available on mobil.
