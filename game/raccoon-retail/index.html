About Raccoon Retail

Simulation fans, take note: you can play Raccoon Retail, a simulation game from our Build & Simulate category, free in your browser. It runs on desktop and mobile alike.

Raccoon Retail transforms the chaotic charm of retail into a fun challenge! Step into a raccoon's paws, cleaning a bustling store with mischievous customers. Navigate spills, collect trash, and upgrade your cleaning gear as the aisles expand. Can you out-clean the messiest critters around? It's a wild, whimsical ride!
