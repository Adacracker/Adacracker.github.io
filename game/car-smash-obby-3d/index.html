About Car Smash Obby 3D

Car Smash Obby 3D loads straight into this page as a free driving game. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the arrow keys and the space bar.

Smash barriers, dodge giant traps and reach the finish in a car that can fall apart! Conquer 36 obstacle courses across six colorful worlds. Time your run past swinging hammers and crushers, jump gaps and escape giant rollers. Earn coins, collect 10 vehicles and upgrade impact power, speed, armor, grip and suspension.
