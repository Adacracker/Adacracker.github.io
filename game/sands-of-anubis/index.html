About Sands of Anubis

Puzzle time. Sands of Anubis is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the mouse and taps.

Sands of Anubis is an Egyptian tile matching puzzle. Tap a tile to move it into your tray. Collect three matching tiles to clear them. Only tiles that are not covered by another tile can be picked, so plan your moves. If the tray fills up with 7 tiles, the level starts over. Play almost 5000 levels among the pyramids. Stuck? Use boosters: Magnet pulls matching tiles to complete a triple, Undo sends the last tile back to the board and Shuffle mixes the tiles. Complete daily quests, spin the lucky wheel and open the hourly reward chest to unlock the pharaohs treasure.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Slime Buffet and Color Coin Merge, give Sands of Anubis a try: they share the “logic” and “puzzle” tags.

How to play Sands of Anubis

Click or tap a tile to move it into the tray.
