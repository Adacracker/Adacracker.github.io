About Hyper Cars Stunt Racing

Buckle up: you can play Hyper Cars Stunt Racing, a driving game from our Cars & Racing category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar. It can also be played by two people on one device.

Start your engines and explore a massive open world in Hyper Cars Stunt Racing! This fantastic 3D driving simulator gives you the complete freedom to tear through a sprawling city, complete high-stakes missions, and build the hyper-car of your dreams. Whether you are drifting through downtown traffic, catching massive air at the airport, or going head-to-head against a friend in local multiplayer!

Why you might like it: Hyper Cars Stunt Racing has a lot in common with Monster Truck 3D Winter and Extreme Gear Shift Racing - they share the “car” and “drift” tags.

How to play Hyper Cars Stunt Racing

Player 1.

W, A, S, D: Drive / Steer.

Shift: Nitro.

Space: Handbrake.

Player 2.

Arrow Keys: Drive / Steer.

K: Nitro.

J: Handbrake.

Mobile Controls: The game also supports touch controls on mobile devices.
