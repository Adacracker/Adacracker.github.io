About Sidefire

SIDEFIRE is a tactical 2D side-view team shooter. Jump into fast 5v5 Team Deathmatch against smart bots on Plant 7, a multi-level factory map with catwalks, containers and scaffolding. First team to 40 kills - or the leader after 5 minutes - wins. Choose from 9 realistic weapons, including the AK-74, M4A1, MP5, SVD, M870 and PKM, and tune them with optics, muzzles, underbarrel gear and magazines. Throw grenades, land headshots, chain killstreaks and level up to unlock new guns and attachments. Open cases for weapon skins and operator gear, claim daily rewards and pick easy, normal or hard bots.
