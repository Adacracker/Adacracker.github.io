About Candy Sort

Candy Sort loads straight into this page as a free puzzle game. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the mouse and touch.

Candy Sort is a colorful sorting puzzle where you move candies between tubes and group them by color. Place candies only in empty tubes or on the same-colored candy. Race against the clock, and when you need help, watch a short reward ad to add an extra tube or stop the timer for unlimited time.

Why you might like it: Fans of Slime Buffet and Color Coin Merge may like this one too, since it shares their “puzzle” and “brain” tags.

How to play Candy Sort

Use a mouse or touchpad to move candies between tubes Place a candy in an empty tube or on top of a candy of the same color.
