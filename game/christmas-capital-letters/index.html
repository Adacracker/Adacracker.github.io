About Christmas Capital Letters

Fancy a brain-teaser? You can play Christmas Capital Letters, a puzzle game from our Puzzle category, free in your browser. It works on phones, tablets and computers. You play it with taps and touch. On a phone, turn it sideways for the wide landscape screen.

In this Christmas game your job is to touch only capital letters. Be focused and fast. You can miss ten letters and you can make only 3 mistakes. Get the best score for this game and enjoy in holidays.

Why you might like it: Christmas Capital Letters has a lot in common with Atomic Puzzle XMas and Christmas Memory Match - they share the “christmas” tag.
