About Chubby Duck Offroad

Chubby Duck Offroad is a driving game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the space bar.

One descent down a freshly dealt mountain — gravity is the engine, and your air rotation is the only resource you cant take back. Follow the speed signs, land on the back of every hill, hop the boulders, ride the logs like rails. One wipeout ends the run: finish for a star, ring every cone for two, beat par time for three. How fast can you get the duck down?

Why you might like it: Fans of Magline Moto and Fat Duck Frenzy may like this one too, since it shares their “racing” and “physics” tags.
