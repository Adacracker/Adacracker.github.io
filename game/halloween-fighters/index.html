About Halloween Fighters

Halloween Fighters is a free action game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the arrow keys and W A S D.

Enter a wild Halloween arcade showdown! Choose from 11 monsters and battle through 12 haunted arenas to face three giant bosses: Iron Revenant, Hexwrap and Witchpump. Mix ten directional punches and kicks, chain combos, break guards and unleash glowing supers. Hand-drawn comic animation, elastic reactions, character voices, a themed soundtrack and dramatic slow-motion knockouts bring every clash to life. Three difficulty levels and an escalating arcade ladder keep the challenge fresh.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Spooky Boxing and Grave Blast, give Halloween Fighters a try: they share the “monster” and “zombie” tags.

How to play Halloween Fighters

Move with Arrow keys or WASD Press Z or J to punch X or K to kick Hold forward for a strong attack back for a medium attack down for a low attack or sweep and up for an uppercut or high kick Use no direction for a light attack Press up without att.
