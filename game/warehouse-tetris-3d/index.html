About Warehouse Tetris 3D

Warehouse Tetris 3D is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the space bar, the mouse and dragging.

Warehouse Tetris 3D is a relaxing 3D packing puzzle where you load boxes, crates, barrels, and cargo into warehouses, trucks, and shipping containers. Rotate pieces, plan every move, avoid empty gaps, and try to achieve the highest packing efficiency. Complete dozens of levels, earn stars, master special orders, and challenge yourself in Endless and Time Attack modes.

Why you might like it: Fans of Sand Blocks and TideRoll: Block Puzzle may like this one too, since it shares their “logic” and “relaxation” tags.
