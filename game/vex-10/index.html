About Vex 10

Pack your bags: Vex 10 is a free adventure game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. It is played with the arrow keys, W A S D and taps.

Vex 10 is here - and this time, Vex takes a different turn! Take on brand-new Acts filled with deadly traps, tricky obstacles, and game-changing mechanics. Walk on ceilings with upside-down gravity, switch between characters, rotate platforms, and avoid invisible booby traps. Freeze time to safely bypass obstacles, or take on the all-new Challenge Mode where you control multiple Vex characters at once. Can you master every Act and become the ultimate Vex champion?

Why you might like it: Vex 10 has a lot in common with Trap Step and VEX 3 Xmas - they share the “jumping” and “platformer” tags.

How to play Vex 10

On keyboard, use the Arrow keys or WASD to move, jump, and crouch your stickman. On mobile, tap the on-screen buttons to run, jump, slide.
