About Pixel Nono

Pixel Nono loads straight into this page as a free puzzle game. It is best played on a computer.

Pixel Nono turns simple number clues into a quiet, rewarding moment of focus. Fill the grid, mark the empties, and watch a pixel-art picture appear, one logical step at a time.
