About VEX 3 Xmas

Pack your bags: VEX 3 Xmas is an adventure game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the arrow keys, W A S D and the mouse.

Vex 3 is back! This time, jump, run and maneuver between candy canes, snowy landscapes and Christmas trees. Clear every zone by reaching the end of the trapped and treacherous zones. How to play VEX 3 Xmas

Use the WASD or arrow keys to move around and brave the traps and obstacles. When standing still, you can use the mouse to look ahead. Safely reach the end of the level to move on to the next. However, be careful, as even the level menu is boobytrapped!
