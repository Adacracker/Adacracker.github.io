About Winter Tiles

Winter Tiles is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the mouse, taps and touch.

Enjoy 'Winter Tiles' with a festive theme! Match and collect pairs of identical winter-themed tiles. Click or tap two tiles with a clear path between them with no more than two turns. Collect all the winter-themed tiles within the time limit to advance to the next level.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Santa Puzzles and Snowman Couples, give Winter Tiles a try: they share the “christmas” and “winter” tags.
