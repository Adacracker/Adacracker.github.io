About Cellar

Cellar loads straight into this page as a free simulation game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the mouse and taps.

Run a cheese cave as its mouse cellarmaster. Wheels age in real time: sell them young, or wait and they are worth up to five times as much. Nothing ever spoils, so leaving is always safe. Hire apprentices, hand over cheeses for the notes at the grating, dig rooms for cheeses that take an afternoon, a night or three days, win rosettes at the County Show, and listen to 1910s gramophone records while you wait. Drawn in pen and ink, like a 1920s magazine.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Wild Fusion and Steal Memerot: Zoo Tycoon, give Cellar a try: they share the “animal” and “idle” tags.

How to play Cellar

Tap or click a shelf to make, sell or turn its batch; tap a wheel for its card; tap the cellarmaster for caps. Keys (changeable in Options): 1-4 pages, Q W E the bench cards, S sell every ripe wheel, L next Book page, G hand over a note, R change the record, M sound off, O or Esc options.
