About Verity Block Survival

Fast hands help here. Verity Block Survival loads straight into this page as a free action game. It is best played on a computer. It is played with W A S D, the space bar and the mouse.

Enter a mysterious block-based world where survival is your only goal! Explore a dark and dangerous environment, gather resources, craft useful items, and build a safe place to survive. But be careful—strange creatures and unexpected dangers may appear when you least expect them. Explore abandoned areas, discover hidden secrets, and find a way to survive the night.
