About Theme word search

Fancy a brain-teaser? Theme word search loads straight into this page as a free puzzle game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it.

"Theme word search" is a free family game where you have to find hidden words on letter boards. Ideal for seniors who want to practice language, vocabulary and train their brain.

How to play Theme word search

Find mixed words of different themes. Words can be hidden diagonally, horizontally, vertically or in the opposite direction. Develop your visual dexterity and find all the encrypted words!

Theme word search: FAQ

Is Theme word search free?

Yes. Theme word search is free to play in your browser on Arcade Cabin. It is supplied by GameDistribution, and the provider may show ads before or during the game.

Can I play Theme word search on my phone?

GameDistribution does not list Theme word search as mobile-friendly, so it is best played on a computer with a keyboard and mouse.

No. Theme word search runs in your browser right on this page - press Play to start. There is nothing to install and no account to create.
