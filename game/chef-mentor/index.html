About Chef Mentor

Ready to run the show? Chef Mentor loads straight into this page as a free simulation game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the mouse, swipes and touch.

Chef Mentor is a match-3 cooking journey through the kitchens of the world. Swap and match fresh ingredients to cook 251 authentic dishes across Greece, Italy, Mexico, France and Japan, from moussaka and souvlaki to carbonara, tacos, croissants, sushi and ramen. Every country has its own map, music and new tricks, and each dish you finish unlocks its real recipe in your cookbook. Earn three stars on every level and collect stamps in your chef's passport.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Pizza Now! and Falafel Master, give Chef Mentor a try: they share the “cook” and “chef” tags.

How to play Chef Mentor

Swipe an ingredient (mouse or touch) to swap it with its neighbour and make 3 or more of the same in a row. Collect the ingredients shown at the top before you run out of moves. Match 4 or 5 in a row to create special pieces that clear many ingredients at once. Break obstacles such as ice, plates and flour by matching next to them. Finish a level with moves to spare to earn up to three stars.
