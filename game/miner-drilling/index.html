About Miner: drilling

Drill deep. Risk everything. Escape with the loot.

A fast-paced 3D mining arcade game built around short, intense runs.

Every descent takes you into a new mine, where your choices in the moment decide how far you go — and how much you bring back.

How to play

Use arrow keys, WASD, or the virtual joystick to move. On mobile, tap or drag on the screen. Drilling starts automatically when you push into a block.

Drill ore, earn money, and buy upgrades for your miner and mines. Glowing blocks are harder to break but give bigger rewards.

Each mine is procedurally generated, so every run is different. Upgrade to find richer ore, bonus blocks, cards, oxygen, cooling supplies, and new gameplay features.
