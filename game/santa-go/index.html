About Santa Go

Fancy a brain-teaser? You can play Santa Go, a puzzle game from our Puzzle category, free in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the mouse.

Santa Go is a cozy Christmas puzzle game where you help Santa reach his sleigh!

Draw a line for Santa to slide along and watch him glide under a cheerful holiday melody.

Be careful — with each level, more obstacles appear, and they can easily knock Santa down.

Your goal is to guide him into the sleigh and keep him there for 3 seconds to complete the level!

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Jewel Christmas Mania and Santa Puzzles, give Santa Go a try: they share the “christmas” and “santa” tags.

How to play Santa Go

Draw a path with your finger or mouse to set Santa’s movement.

He will follow the exact line you create.

Avoid all obstacles — hitting any of them will make Santa fall, and you’ll have to restart.

Reach the sleigh and keep Santa inside for 3 seconds to finish the level.

Experiment with different paths, find safe routes, and help Santa get back to his holiday mission!
