About Frontline Strike

Steady your aim. You can play Frontline Strike, a shooting game from our Shooting category, free in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with W A S D, the mouse and touch.

Frontline Strike is a 3D first-person military shooter. Hold your ground against wave after wave of enemy squads: riflemen who reposition, crouch to fire and lob grenades when you hide, heavy troopers who soak up damage and marksmen who pick you off from range. Maps: Desert Outpost, a burnt-out city compound at dusk, and Harbor Night, a container port at night with tight lanes between stacked containers (unlocks at the rank of Private). Weapons: assault rifle, pistol and shotgun, plus grenades. Earn credits every run and upgrade damage, magazine size and reload speed in the Armory. Career: climb 10 military ranks from Recruit to General. Daily missions: three new missions every day, each with a credit reward and a bonus for completing all three. Features: headshots, kill streaks, explosive barrels, health regeneration, ammo drops, keyboard and mouse or touch controls, English and Arabic.

Why you might like it: Frontline Strike has a lot in common with Cold Iron: Last Extraction and Robot Strike: FPS Shooter - they share the “shooter” and “first person shooter” tags.
