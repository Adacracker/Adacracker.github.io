About Lumberjack Santa Claus

You can play Lumberjack Santa Claus, an arcade game from our Arcade & Casual category, free in your browser. It works on phones, tablets and computers. It is played with taps. On a phone, hold it upright: the game is made for portrait mode.

Chop chop chop, Santa Claus needs your help! Control Santa Claus or a helper Elf in the role of the Christmas Lumberjack and try to cut the tree into piles of wood, while avoiding the tree sticks. Assist Santa Claus and the Elf timberman with their quest!

Why you might like it: Lumberjack Santa Claus has a lot in common with Santa Christmas Run and Santa Run Challenge - they share the “christmas” and “winter” tags.

How to play Lumberjack Santa Claus

Tap on screen either to the left side of the tree or to the right side and the character will chop the tree from that side. You must avoid that branches as you chop the tree.
