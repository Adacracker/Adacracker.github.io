About Apex Prime

Apex Prime is a free arcade game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use W A S D and the space bar.

Step into a prehistoric wilderness where giant beasts rule the land! Master survival skills, craft primitive weaponry, and battle formidable apex predators across uncharted ancient frontiers. Explore dense, untamed landscapes and prove your dominance in a high-stakes quest to claim the ultimate primal throne.
