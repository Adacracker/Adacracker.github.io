About Hoops & Fruits

Got a minute? Hoops & Fruits is a free arcade game that runs right in your browser. You can play it on a computer or a touch screen. You play it with dragging. On a phone, hold it upright: the game is made for portrait mode.

Dive into the whimsical world of Hoops & Fruits, where your hoop-tossing skills meet colorful, fruity targets. Engage in addictive drag-and-drop gameplay with charming graphics and a captivating soundtrack, challenging you to master levels and score big by hooping multiple fruits at once.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Graffiti Time and Red Coloring Book, give Hoops & Fruits a try: they share the “drawing” tag.
