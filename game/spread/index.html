About Spread

Fancy a brain-teaser? Spread is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. You play it with taps and touch.

Spread is a strategic color conquest puzzle game where every move expands your territory across the board. Choose colors carefully to absorb connected cells, outsmart your rival’s expansion, and control the largest area before the board fills completely. With simple mechanics but deep tactical gameplay, Spread challenges your planning, timing, and strategy in every round.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Brix Blast and Ant Moving Simulator, give Spread a try: they share the “logic” and “colormatch” tags.

How to play Spread

Tap a color to spread your territory across connected cells.

Your zone absorbs all touching tiles of the selected color.

The rival player expands from the opposite corner.

You cannot choose your current color or the rival’s current color.

Plan your moves carefully to control more territory.

Capture the largest area before the board is completely filled.

Outsmart your opponent using smart color strategy.

Progress and rounds are saved automatically.
