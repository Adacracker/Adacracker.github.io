About Obby Highest Jump Ever

Obby Highest Jump Ever is a free adventure game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the arrow keys, the space bar and the mouse. It can also be played by two people on one device.

Obby Highest Jump Ever is a click-based jumping and climbing game where every click makes you stronger. Start from a small 10M building and push your way up to massive 2500M towers as your jump power grows step by step. Each new height feels like a new milestone, and every rooftop trophy proves you earned it. Your goal is simple: jump higher, reach the top, and collect trophies. The higher the building, the bigger the reward.

Why you might like it: Obby Highest Jump Ever has a lot in common with Escape School Duel and Brainrot Mega Parkour - they share the “2 player” and “2 player games” tags.
