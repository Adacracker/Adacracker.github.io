About Taiga: Expedition

Taiga: Expedition is a free driving game that runs right in your browser. It runs on desktop and mobile alike. Controls use the space bar, the mouse and touch. On a phone, turn it sideways for the wide landscape screen.

Taiga: Expedition is a cozy 3D physics offroad delivery simulator. Drive vintage trucks through mud, deep fords and forest forks of the wild taiga and deliver logs and cargo to the sawmill. Your truck bed carries living cargo: logs, fuel barrels, blocks, pipes, hay bales and fragile containers. They sway and fly off on bumps, so feather throttle, brake and steering to keep them on board. 5 trucks: Zubr-130 Veteran, Leshy-66 4x4, Kedr-255 6x6, Utes-4320 and Cayman-6522 Taiga Beast 6x6. Upgrade engine, mud tires, suspension, high sides and fuel tank, and paint the body in six colors. 50 handcrafted routes from dirt roads to the Great Swamp, with risky shortcuts and safe detours, fords, sticky mud and boulders. Earn up to 3 stars per level for cargo kept and time.

Why you might like it: Fans of Warehouse Wobble 3D and Monster Truck Offroad 2 may like this one too, since it shares their “driving” and “offroad” tags.
