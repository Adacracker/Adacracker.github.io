About Football Pinball

Game on! Football Pinball is a sports game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the arrow keys, the space bar and taps.

Football Pinball is a pinball table shaped like a football pitch, seen from your own end. Two flippers are all you get. The ball rolls down at you, and whatever drains between them is a goal for the other side — there is a net down there too, so you get to watch it happen. Kick it back up the table, past three midfielders and a keeper who tracks it along his own line, and put it in the goal at the far end. Ninety seconds, more goals wins, level is a draw.

Why you might like it: Football Pinball has a lot in common with Rocket Car Football Championship and Pro Football Eleven 2027 - they share the “soccer” and “football” tags.

How to play Football Pinball

Left arrow A or Z works the left flipper right arrow D or works the right one Space works both On a phone or tablet tap the left half of the screen for the left flipper and the right half for the right one.
