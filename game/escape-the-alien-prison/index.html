About Escape the Alien Prison

You can play Escape the Alien Prison, an adventure game from our Adventure category, free in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with W A S D, the space bar and the mouse. It can also be played by two people on one device.

You have suddenly woken up trapped inside a massive, high-tech spaceship deep in the cosmos, and there is only one objective: break out! Escape the Alien Prison is an epic sci-fi survival and parkour adventure where you must fight your way to freedom. Navigate dangerous obstacle courses, hack complex security systems, and take down extraterrestrial threats.

The complete story campaign, puzzles, collectibles, and progression are available in Single Player Mode.

Why you might like it: Fans of +1 Speed Keyboard Lucky Escape and Snow Rider Obby Parkour may like this one too, since it shares their “roblox” and “2players” tags.

How to play Escape the Alien Prison

Move: Use the W, A, S, D keys.

Jump: Press the Spacebar (Press twice for a Double Jump!).

Sprint: Hold Left Shift to run fast.

Attack / Shoot: Press F or the Right Mouse Button.

Interact: Press E to hack panels and open doors.

Change Weapons: Use the 1, 2, 3, 4 number keys.
