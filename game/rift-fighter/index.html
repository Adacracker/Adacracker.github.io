About Rift Fighter

Fast hands help here. You can play Rift Fighter, an action game from our Action category, free in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. It is played with the arrow keys, W A S D and touch.

Type your skills. Survive the horde. A survivors-like where combos are fought out on the keyboard, not picked from a menu.

Why you might like it: Rift Fighter has a lot in common with Voxhalla and Dreamland Survivors - they share the “survival” and “pixelart” tags.

How to play Rift Fighter

Move WASD or Arrow keys · Techniques J K L I (rebindable) · Confirm J · Cancel K · Inventory Tab.

Techniques are typed as sequences. Charge (J→K) is yours from the start; the rest drop as manuals from elites and bosses. The bar at the bottom of the screen always shows how far into a sequence you are.

Touch is supported: a floating stick on the left, four action buttons on the right.
