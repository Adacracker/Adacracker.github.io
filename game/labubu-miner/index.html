About Labubu Miner

You can play Labubu Miner, a simulation game from our Build & Simulate category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the arrow keys, the mouse and taps.

Dig n Click: Labubu Miner is a relaxing pixel-style clicker where the cute Labubu swings his pickaxe and breaks blocks for you! Tap the block to crack it and collect coins and rare crystals. Spend your coins on upgrades: stronger arms, extra miners, drills, explosives and a laser drill that keep digging even while you are away. Sell the crystals you find, descend deeper into the mine to discover 30+ ores and gems, and unlock prestige crystals that multiply everything you earn. Come back every day for daily rewards, catch the golden apples for random bonuses and watch Labubu dig his way to the center of the earth!

Why you might like it: Fans of Phone Call Simulator: Monsters and Needle Rush may like this one too, since it shares their “clicker” and “casual” tags.

How to play Labubu Miner

Tap or click the block to break it Use the buttons on the screen to open Upgrades Inventory Rewards and Settings Tap a crystal in your inventory and press Sell to earn coins Use the arrows on the left to go deeper or return to previous levels.
