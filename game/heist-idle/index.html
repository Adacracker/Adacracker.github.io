About Heist Idle

Ready to run the show? Heist Idle is a free simulation game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the arrow keys, W A S D and the mouse.

Build your criminal empire in a fun 3D idle heist adventure! Rob jewelry stores, break into banks, infiltrate museums, dodge security cameras and lasers, and escape with valuable loot. Dig through secure areas, smash displays, carry stolen treasures, unlock new locations, upgrade your character, and recruit crew members to help with bigger heists. Each new area introduces tougher security, better rewards, and new ways to steal. Collect cash, improve your operation, and become the ultimate master thief!
