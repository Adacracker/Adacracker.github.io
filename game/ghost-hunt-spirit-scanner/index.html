About Ghost Hunt: Spirit Scanner

Ghosts hide among people and objects inside each room. Examine your surroundings, aim the scanner at suspicious targets, and search for paranormal traces. A correct reading reveals the hidden spirit.

Find the ghost, choose your weapon, and destroy the threat. Earn rewards for clearing rooms, then upgrade your hand, scanner, and weapon to complete new assignments faster.

How to play

1. Goal: investigate each room, scan people and objects, find hidden ghosts, and destroy them. After each level, upgrade your hand for interacting with objects, your scanner for searching, and your weapon for attacking.

2. Controls:

— Desktop: hold the left mouse button and guide the pointer to look around, aim the scanner, and select targets.

— Mobile and tablet: swipe across the screen to move your view, scan the room, and interact with objects.
