About Santa Run

Quick fun ahead. Santa Run is a free arcade game that runs right in your browser. It runs on desktop and mobile alike. You play it with the mouse. On a phone, hold it upright: the game is made for portrait mode.

Join Santa's thrilling rooftop dash in Santa Run! Master quick jumps to dodge deadly drops and tricky obstacles like water towers and icicles. With simple clicks, keep Santa sprinting as you leap over chimneys to rack up a high score and ensure every present is delivered on time.

Why you might like it: Santa Run has a lot in common with Santa Games and Wizard Santa Jump - they share the “arcade” tag.
