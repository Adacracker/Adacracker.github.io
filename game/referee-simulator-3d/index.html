About Referee Simulator 3D

Referee Simulator 3D is a simulation game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the arrow keys, W A S D and the mouse.

Referee Simulator 3D puts the whistle in your hands. Run with play, stay close to the action and make the calls that decide the match: foul or clean tackle, penalty or free kick, offside or goal, yellow or red card. Not sure? Sprint to the pitchside VAR monitor and review the incident from multiple camera angles in slow motion. Features: - 13 incident types: penalties, offsides, handballs, goal-line decisions, second yellows, elbows, corners, throw-ins and more - Real VAR reviews with offside lines and goal-line camera - Career mode: rise from the regional league to the Super League, derbies and international matches.
