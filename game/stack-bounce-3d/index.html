About Stack Bounce 3D

Got a minute? You can play Stack Bounce 3D, an arcade game from our Arcade & Casual category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the arrow keys, the mouse and touch.

Experience the ultimate satisfying 3D tower smash! Control a bouncing ball and spiral down a floating helix tower. Shatter vibrant platform rings, unleash blazing fireball power-ups, activate dynamite blasts, and dodge deadly red hazard traps. Collect stars, unlock unique ball skins, and enjoy dynamic procedural music that intensifies with your combo streaks!

Why you might like it: Stack Bounce 3D has a lot in common with Chaindrop and Bongo Bounce - they share the “arcade” and “ball” tags.

How to play Stack Bounce 3D

Desktop / Laptop: Click and drag with mouse, or use A / D or Left / Right Arrow keys to rotate the tower.

Mobile / Tablet: Touch and drag horizontally anywhere on the screen to rotate the tower.

Break the platforms to descend and build combos.

Avoid landing on red spiked hazard platforms to keep your 3 lives.

Break multiple rings in a single drop to charge Fireball mode and blast through any hazard!

Press P or click the Gear icon to pause/resume.
