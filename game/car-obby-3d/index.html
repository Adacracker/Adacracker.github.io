About Car Obby 3D

Start your engine. Car Obby 3D loads straight into this page as a free driving game. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the space bar, taps and dragging.

Drive a tiny car across 12 floating obstacle courses in Car Obby 3D. Dodge swinging hammers, spinning obstacles and moving platforms, collect coins and reach checkpoints above the clouds. Unlock eight car designs or buy boosts: Ghost for 3 seconds, Nitro for 5 seconds, Jump and Flight for 5 seconds. Tap your stored boosts while driving. Recover from falls at checkpoints and improve your best times. Play with keyboard controls on desktop or one-hand steering and automatic acceleration on mobile. Includes 11 languages and saved progress.

Why you might like it: Car Obby 3D has a lot in common with Car Smash Obby 3D and Speed Evolution - they share the “car” and “driving” tags.
