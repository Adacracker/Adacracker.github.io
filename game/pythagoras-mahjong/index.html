About Pythagoras Mahjong

Pythagoras Mahjong is a free puzzle game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the mouse and taps.

Pythagoras Mahjong is a classic tile-matching puzzle with a mathematical twist. Instead of pictures, every tile shows a number or an expression: 3, √9, 1+2, 6/2 – all of them are equal, so they make a pair. Clear the board by matching free tiles with the same value and train your mental math while you relax.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Sort Right and Bend the Dawn: Light Bending Puzzle, give Pythagoras Mahjong a try: they share the “relaxing” and “math” tags.

How to play Pythagoras Mahjong

Click or tap a free tile, then click another free tile with the same value to remove the pair. A tile is free when nothing lies on top of it and at least one of its left or right sides is open. Numbers and expressions with equal results match, for example 4, √16 and 2+2. Clear all tiles to win. Stuck? Use a hint or shuffle the free tiles.
