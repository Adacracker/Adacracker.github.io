About Halloween Match

Puzzle time. Halloween Match loads straight into this page as a free puzzle game. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. Controls use the mouse and taps.

Match spooky creatures and build your own Halloween collection.

Click or tap on the creature and it will float upwards. Two matching creatures merge and disappear.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Adopt a Monster and Twilight Onet Connect, give Halloween Match a try: they share the “halloween” and “matching” tags.

How to play Halloween Match

Tap or click on creature bubbles to float them up.

Stack 2 of the same type next to each other and they will pop! The goal is to pop all of them.

Watch out, they can get stuck on the way up. But you will have tools to help in tough situations.
