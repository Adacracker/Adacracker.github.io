About Steal Animal Eggs Online

Pack your bags: Steal Animal Eggs Online is a free adventure game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar. It can also be played by two people on one device.

Steal Animal Eggs Online is a fast collection adventure where you raid nests, steal eggs, and escape guardians before returning to your base. Hatch 120+ animals across 12 rarity classes, train with 14 exercise machines to run faster, and explore 12 unique areas. Play online in 1 Player mode or locally with 2 players. Discover rare Gold, Diamond, Toxic, Lava, Rainbow, and Candy animals while building a valuable collection.

Why you might like it: Steal Animal Eggs Online has a lot in common with +1 Speed Keyboard Lucky Escape and Escape the Alien Prison - they share the “roblox” and “2players” tags.
