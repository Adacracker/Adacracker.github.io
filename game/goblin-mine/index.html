About Goblin Mine

Goblin Mine is a 2D mining simulator where you explore underground zones, break valuable ores, fill your backpack, and sell your haul for coins. Upgrade your pickaxe, backpack, speed, and power to reach deeper mines. Unlock 15 zones, hatch pets, complete achievements and daily quests, collect Gems, and rebirth for permanent bonuses. How to play

WASD or Arrow Keys Move.

Left Mouse Button Mine.

E Interact.

Hold the Mine button on touch devices.

Return to camp to sell ores and upgrade your equipment.
