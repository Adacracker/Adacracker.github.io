About Christmas Slide Puzzle

Fancy a brain-teaser? You can play Christmas Slide Puzzle, a puzzle game from our Puzzle category, free in your browser. You can play it on a computer or a touch screen. It is played with the mouse, taps and touch. On a phone, hold it upright: the game is made for portrait mode.

Christmas Slide Puzzle is a simple HTML5 classic puzzle game. Move pieces of christmas images between tiles to arrange them in correct order to reassemble the image. Touch or click a block to shift it on adjacent blank place. Have fun.
