About Dead City: Zombie Survival

Ready for some action? Dead City: Zombie Survival is a free action game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. You play it with W A S D, the space bar and the mouse.

The city fell three days ago. Survive the night in a ruined, zombie-infested city, scavenge weapons and supplies, fight off hordes of the dead and find a way out before its too late. Explore abandoned streets, a forest camp and an industrial zone, use your flashlight in the dark and drive vehicles to escape. How long can you survive?
