About BlockCrafter

BlockCrafter is a free simulation game that runs right in your browser. It is best played on a computer. You play it with W A S D, the space bar and the mouse.

An infinite sandbox world powered by hyper-realistic graphics and advanced physics. Explore lifelike biomes, gather resources, build without limits, and survive a harsh, dynamic environment. Key Features: Next-gen visuals: HD textures, dynamic weather, and real-time lighting. Survival & Crafting: Resource management, advanced crafting, and building. Total freedom: A fully destructible, infinite world to explore solo or with friends. Ready to experience the ultimate survival sandbox?
