About Cafe honey heist

Ready to run the show? Cafe honey heist is a free simulation game that runs right in your browser. A computer with a keyboard and mouse is the best way to play it. You play it with the arrow keys and the space bar.

Fly through a warm, bustling café, sip delicious nectar from coffee cups and treats resting on tables, and carry your loot to the hive outside. But stay on your toes—the giant café owner is always listening! Buzzing too loud will alert him, sending him on a hunt to swat you right out of the sky. Land on table surfaces to rest, lower your noise level, and plan your next move. Collect enough nectar, unlock powerful abilities, and complete 5 hive deposits to win!

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Monster Café: Idle Tycoon and Burger Restaurant Simulator 3D, give Cafe honey heist a try: they share the “restaurant” tag.
