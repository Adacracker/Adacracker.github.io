About DarkHood

Got a minute? DarkHood loads straight into this page as a free arcade game. You can play it on a computer or a touch screen. You play it with the arrow keys, the space bar and touch. On a phone, turn it sideways for the wide landscape screen.

Step into a world where the only light is your own aura. Guide a brave boy in a dark hoodie through candle-lit caves, sleeping monsters and hidden passages. Collect every glowing gem, avoid bombs, pits and traps, and unlock the exit gate of each level. A cozy dark platform adventure designed for short relaxing sessions at home or on the go.

Why you might like it: Fans of Snake Block Rush and Chicken Yeet may like this one too, since it shares their “hypercasual” and “action” tags.

How to play DarkHood

Desktop arrow keys or A D to move Space W Up to jump Mobile use the on-screen touch buttons to run jump and interact.
