About Typeblade: Shadow Dojo

Defend the Shadow Dojo in a fast-paced typing action game! Use your keyboard as a weapon: type the first letter above an enemy to lock on, then complete the word to unleash a deadly sword strike. Build combos, increase your score multiplier, earn lifelines, and survive waves of Shadow Warriors, Night Runners, armored Oni Guards, and powerful Oni Lords. Fight through 20 waves and 2 boss battles in Dojo Trial, or test your skills in Endless mode. Collect coins to unlock blades and skins.

How to play

Desktop:

Use your keyboard to type the letters above enemies. Type the first letter to lock onto an enemy, then complete the word to attack. Press Backspace or Tab to cancel the current lock-on.

Mobile:

Use the on-screen keyboard to type. Tap an enemy to lock onto them, then type the displayed word to attack. Tap DEL to cancel the current lock-on.

Defeat enemies, build your combo, collect coins, and survive as many waves as possible.
