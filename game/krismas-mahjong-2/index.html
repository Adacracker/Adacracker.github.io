How to play KrisMas Mahjong 2

How to play this online mahjong game? KrisMas Mahjong uses the connect-2 rules; match two identical tiles together by clicking them. You can only match the items if they can be connected by a line with a maximum of three 90-degree corners. Make sure you empty the entire board before the timer ends to complete every level.

Use the mouse or touchscreen to play.
