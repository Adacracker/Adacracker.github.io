About Mini Golf 3D - Island Course

Warm up first: you can play Mini Golf 3D - Island Course, a sports game from our Sports category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer. You play it with the arrow keys, the mouse and touch.

Mini Golf 3D - Island Course is a 51-hole putting course on a small island. Bank shots off wooden rails, roll up ramps, time spinning rotors and drop through wormholes. Race the ghost of your best run, beat a live rival, and come back daily for a fresh one-hole challenge. Five game modes, 153 stars to earn, 13 balls and 6 weather themes. No sign-up needed.

Why you might like it: If you enjoyed Sky Golfer and Big Smash, give Mini Golf 3D - Island Course a try: they share the “physics” and “golf” tags.

How to play Mini Golf 3D - Island Course

Mouse: press the ball, pull back and let go to putt. The arrow shows aim and power, so pull further for a harder hit. Drag anywhere else to look around and press Esc to pause. Touch: pull back from the ball and lift your finger to putt, then drag anywhere else to move the camera. Sink the ball in as few strokes as you can.
