About Block Slide

You can play Block Slide, a puzzle game from our Puzzle category, free in your browser. It is best played on a computer. Controls use the arrow keys, W A S D and the space bar.

Block Slide is a minimalist sliding block puzzler. Swipe or press an arrow key and every block slides until it hits a wall or another block get each onto its matching target to clear the level. 130 levels introduce mechanics gradually: teleporters, arrow tiles, sticky blocks, pins, pressure traps, rotators, conveyors, and multi-board levels solved in parallel. Play on desktop or mobile portrait and landscape adapt automatically.

Why you might like it: Fans of Prism Blast 3D and PrismRoll 3D may like this one too, since it shares their “logic” and “block” tags.

How to play Block Slide

Swipe (touch) or Arrow Keys / WASD (keyboard) to slide all blocks in that direction. Press R to restart a level, ESC or the menu button to return to level select, Spacebar for next level.
