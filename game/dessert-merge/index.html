About Dessert Merge

Puzzle time. Dessert Merge is a puzzle game you can play for free on Arcade Cabin, with nothing to download. It is best played on a computer.

Dessert Merge is a fun and relaxing merge puzzle game where you combine matching desserts to create bigger and more delicious treats. Drop desserts onto the board, merge identical ones, and discover new recipes as you progress. Plan your moves carefully, unlock all the desserts, and see how far you can go!

Why you might like it: Dessert Merge has a lot in common with Jewel Christmas Mania and Candy Sort - they share the “candy” and “matching” tags.

How to play Dessert Merge

Drop desserts onto the board and merge two identical ones to create a new dessert. Keep combining matching treats to unlock higher-level desserts.

Plan each move carefully and avoid filling up the board. Use boosters when you need help and try to unlock every dessert in the collection!
