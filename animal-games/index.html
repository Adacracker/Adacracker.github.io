What are animal games?

Animal games put a creature at the centre of the game. You might care for a pet - feeding, washing, dressing up - run a farm or a zoo, play as a shark, wolf or duck in a simulator, or guide cute animals through puzzles and runners. It is one of the most varied collections on the site.

What makes a good one?

The best animal games make the animal feel alive with expressive animations and reactions to what you do. In care games, a variety of tasks keeps it from becoming repetitive; in simulators, a big world to explore matters most.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

For young children, pet care and animal dress-up games are a gentle start. Older players may prefer animal simulators where you survive and grow, or animal-themed puzzles and runners. Look for 'Yes, touch-friendly' on the game page if you are on a tablet.
