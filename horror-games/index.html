What are horror games?

Horror games try to make you nervous. Some are first-person chase games where a monster hunts you through a house or a maze of empty rooms (the 'backrooms'); others are escape games with creepy puzzles. This collection also includes lighter Halloween games - ghosts, pumpkins and friendly monsters - that are more silly than scary.

What makes a good one?

Good horror is about atmosphere: sound, darkness and the feeling that something could appear at any moment. The best games give you a way to fight back or hide, rather than only jump scares, and save often so that being caught is not too punishing.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

If you are easily spooked, start with Halloween puzzles and dress-ups or cartoon monster games. Horror fans can go straight to the first-person escape and chase games - play with headphones for the full effect. Several horror games are meant for teenagers and adults, so check the description first.

Are these horror games suitable for kids?

Not all of them. Halloween and cartoon monster games are fine for most ages, but first-person chase and escape games can be genuinely scary. Read the game page before playing with children.
