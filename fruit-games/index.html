What are fruit and candy games?

Fruit and candy are favourite themes for casual games because they are colourful and easy to tell apart. Here you will find watermelon merge games, fruit slicing, candy match-3, sweet stacking games and juice and dessert makers.

What makes a good one?

A good fruit or candy game is satisfying to look at and to hear - pops, splats and crunches - and has a simple goal you understand at once.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

For quick fun, try fruit slicing. For a puzzle, choose a fruit merge (Suika-style) game or a candy match-3. Most of these work well with one finger on a phone.

Fruit & Candy Games: questions and answers

What is a watermelon merge game?

A puzzle where you drop fruit into a box; two of the same fruit merge into a bigger one, and the goal is to make a watermelon without overflowing.
