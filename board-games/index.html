What counts as a board or card game?

This category is for games you could also play at a table: chess and checkers, solitaire in its many forms (Klondike, Spider, FreeCell, TriPeaks, Pyramid), mahjong tile matching, ludo, dominoes, Connect 4, tic tac toe, battleship and card games like Uno-style colour matching. Strategy and tower defense games now have their own category.

What makes a good one?

A good digital board game gets the rules exactly right and makes legal moves obvious. Useful extras are an undo button, hints, a choice of computer difficulty and clear animations, so you always see what the other side just did.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

If you just want to relax, solitaire and mahjong are perfect: no opponent, no timer, and you can stop any time. To be challenged, play chess or checkers against the computer on a harder level, or pick a multiplayer version. Ludo, dominoes and tic tac toe are good choices for playing with family on one device.

Board & Cards Games: questions and answers

Can I play chess and checkers for free?

Yes. All board and card games here are free to play in your browser. Several chess and checkers games let you play the computer or another person.

Do I need to know the rules?

Most games show a short tutorial or the rules in their menu, and the classics like solitaire and mahjong highlight the moves you are allowed to make.

Can I play board games with a friend on one device?

Ludo, tic tac toe, Connect 4-style and checkers games often have a two-player mode on the same screen - look for it on the game's start menu.

Do these games work on phones?

24 of the 59 games are marked touch-friendly; card and tile games are especially easy to play with taps.
