What are games like Tetris?

Block puzzles are about fitting shapes into a grid. In falling-block games pieces drop from the top and you rotate and slide them to complete rows. In the calmer 'block blast' style you drag pieces onto a board in any order and clear full rows and columns. Both reward thinking one or two pieces ahead.

What makes a good one?

Good block games show the next piece, let you rotate precisely and never hide a piece behind the interface. In drag-and-drop versions, a clear shadow showing where a piece will land avoids frustrating mistakes.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

If you want to relax, choose a drag-and-drop block puzzle - there is no clock. If you miss the arcade feeling, choose a falling-block game and work up to higher speeds. Leave a column open on one side and save long pieces for clearing several lines at once.
