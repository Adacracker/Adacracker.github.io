What are adventure games?

Adventure games send you somewhere and ask you to find a way through. Platformers are about running and jumping across gaps and traps; parkour and obby games are 3D obstacle courses; escape games lock you in a room or a building until you solve your way out; and quest games mix exploring, collecting and small puzzles into a story.

What makes a good one?

Good adventure games have controls you can trust on every jump, checkpoints that are close together, and worlds that change often enough to make you curious about the next level. In escape games, clues should be fair - hard to spot, but logical once you see them.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

For a gentle start, choose a 2D platformer with short levels or a cosy hidden-object quest. If you like a challenge, try 'rage' platformers, obby courses with few checkpoints or escape games with dozens of rooms. Platformers are easiest with a keyboard; many escape and quest games are fine with taps.

Adventure Games: questions and answers

Are these adventure games free?

Yes, all 312 adventure games are free to play online, with no download or sign-up.

What is an obby game?

Obby is short for obstacle course. You run and jump through a 3D course of moving platforms and traps, usually with checkpoints along the way.

Can I play adventure games on my phone?

203 of the 312 games are marked touch-friendly. Escape and point-and-click games work well on phones; precise platformers are easier on a keyboard.

Do I lose my progress if I close the game?

Many level-based games remember which levels you finished in your browser on this device, but not all do - finishing a level before you close the tab is the safest bet.
