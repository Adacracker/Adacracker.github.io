What are memory and hidden object games?

Memory games ask you to remember where things are - most often by flipping cards two at a time to find matching pairs. Spot-the-difference games show two nearly identical pictures, and hidden-object games give you a detailed scene and a list of items to find.

What makes a good one?

Good games in this group use clear, detailed pictures, give a hint when you are stuck and do not rely on a harsh timer. In hidden-object games, items should be cleverly placed but always fairly visible.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

For young children, start with memory card games with few pairs. Adults will enjoy hidden object and difference games with many levels. These games are calm and are a good choice on a tablet.
