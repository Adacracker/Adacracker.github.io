What are simulation games?

Simulation games let you run something and watch it grow. That might be a farm, a restaurant, a supermarket or a factory, a city you build block by block, a pet or a patient you care for, or a blocky crafting world. Idle and clicker tycoons belong here too: you set things up, and income keeps coming in while you take a break.

What makes a good one?

The best simulation games give you a steady stream of small goals - a new machine, a bigger shop, a new recipe - so there is always a reason to do one more day. Clear numbers, upgrades that change how you play and progress that is saved in your browser make a big difference.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

If you are new, start with a cooking or shop game where each day is short, or with an idle tycoon where you cannot really lose. If you like to plan, look for city builders, factory games and management games with deeper upgrade trees. Many simulation games are long, so check that they save progress before you invest an evening.

Build & Simulate Games: questions and answers

Are these simulation games free?

Yes. All 251 simulation games are free in your browser with no download. Some offer optional ads in exchange for in-game bonuses.

What is an idle or tycoon game?

A game where you build up a business or machine that earns money by itself; you spend the money on upgrades so it earns even faster. Our Idle & Clicker collection has more of them.

Do simulation games save my progress?

Most save automatically in your browser on this device. Progress is lost if you clear browser data or switch to another device.

Do they work on phones?

175 of the 251 games are marked touch-friendly by their providers.
