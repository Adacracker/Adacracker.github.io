What are match 3 games?

In a match-3 game you swap neighbouring pieces to line up three or more of the same kind, which clears them and drops new ones from above. Level-based versions give you a goal - collect 20 red gems, clear the jelly - in a limited number of moves. Tile-match games are a newer cousin: you pick tiles from a pile into a tray and clear them in threes.

What makes a good one?

A good match-3 game is generous with special pieces - bombs, line clears, colour bombs - and shows clearly what each combination does. Goals that change from level to level and a fair number of moves keep it from feeling random.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

If you are new, start with games that have no move limit or timer; you can learn how special pieces work without pressure. Experienced players will prefer level-based games with obstacles and tight move counts. Tile-match games are a good choice for a slower, calmer puzzle on a phone.
