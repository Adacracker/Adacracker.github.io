What are shooting games?

Shooting games put a weapon in your hands and ask you to hit targets before they hit you. That covers a lot of ground: first-person shooters (FPS) where you see through the hero's eyes, sniper games about one careful shot, top-down and side-scrolling blasters, tank battles and classic space shoot 'em ups where the screen fills with bullets.

What makes a good one?

Good shooters have honest aim - the shot lands where the crosshair was - and enemies that telegraph what they are about to do. Weapon upgrades between levels add a reason to keep going, and a clear radar or mini-map stops you getting lost in bigger maps.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

Beginners usually have the most fun with sniper games and arcade blasters: they are slower, or the game aims for you, and one level takes a minute or two. If you already play shooters, try the FPS and multiplayer arena games that use W A S D plus mouse aim. Most first-person shooters here are made for a keyboard and mouse.

Shooting Games: questions and answers

Are these shooting games free?

Yes, every shooting game here is free in the browser with no download or account. Ads from the game's provider may show between rounds.

Can I play shooting games on my phone?

124 of the 195 shooting games are marked touch-friendly, usually with virtual sticks. First-person shooters work best with a mouse and keyboard.

How do I aim in browser FPS games?

Most use W A S D to move and the mouse to look and aim; click to fire. Click inside the game first so it captures the mouse, and press Esc to get the cursor back.

Is there a difference between Shooting and Action games here?

Yes. Games where shooting is the main thing live in this category; fighting, survival and stickman games without guns are in Action.
