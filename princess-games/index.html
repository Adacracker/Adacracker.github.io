What are princess games?

Princess games mix dress-up and fairy tales. Most let you design royal outfits, hair and makeup for a ball, a wedding or a seasonal party. Others are princess makeovers, castle decorating, puzzles and adventures with a royal theme.

What makes a good one?

A good princess game has a big wardrobe of gowns, crowns and accessories, a theme for each level and a final scene that shows off your work.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

Younger players will enjoy simple princess dress-ups where you tap to change outfits. Older players may like style challenges with themes to match. Almost all of these games work with touch.
