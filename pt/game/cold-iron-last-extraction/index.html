Sobre Cold Iron: Last Extraction

Você pode jogar Cold Iron: Last Extraction, um jogo de tiro da nossa categoria Tiro, de graça no navegador. Funciona no celular, no tablet e no computador. Ele é jogado com W A S D, o mouse e toques. No celular, vire de lado para aproveitar a tela larga na horizontal.

Descrição de GameMonetize (em inglês)

Cold Iron is a third-person tactical shooter on a rain-soaked city street at night. Pick two guns and a special ability, then hold the plaza against waves of armed and armoured soldiers until the extraction helicopter arrives. Lock on to targets, land headshots, take cover and use slow motion, rage or regeneration when the fight turns. Game modes: - Survival: hold the plaza until the helicopter comes. Easy, Normal and Hard. - Sniper Only: a sniper rifle and a silenced pistol against snipers on the rooftops. - Plant Bombs: plant charges at three sites under fire, then get clear. - Stealth: slip past patrolling guards to the last bomb site. - Training: a short guided tutorial, played automatically the first time. Features: - Earn IRON coins and unlock pistols, SMG, shotgun, M416, AKM, a marksman rifle and a sniper rifle. - Three abilities: Slow Motion, Rage and Regenerate. - A daily challenge with a coin reward. - Touch controls on phones and tablets.

Por que você pode gostar: Quem gosta de Frontline Strike e Grave Blast pode curtir este também, já que ele compartilha as tags “action” e “shooter”.

Como jogar Cold Iron: Last Extraction

WASD - move Mouse - look Left mouse - shoot Right mouse - aim down sights E or middle mouse - lock on hold R - reload 1 2 or mouse wheel - switch guns Shift - run double tap and hold to sprint C - crouch X - use ability V - switch shoulder F - inter.
