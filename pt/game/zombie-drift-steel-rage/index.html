Sobre Zombie Drift: Steel Rage

Você pode jogar Zombie Drift: Steel Rage, um jogo de carro da nossa categoria Carros e Corrida, de graça no navegador. Funciona no celular, no tablet e no computador. Os controles usam a barra de espaço, o mouse e o toque na tela. No celular, vire de lado para aproveitar a tela larga na horizontal.

Descrição de GameMonetize (em inglês)

Zombie Drift: Steel Rage is a post-apocalyptic 3D action game that mixes arcade drifting with arena survival. Drive an armored battle car into a ruined city arena full of zombie hordes and mutant giants. Speed and controlled slides are your weapons: the sharper the drift, the faster your nitro rage builds and the harder your spiked bumper hits - up to 2.5x ramming damage. Mow down walkers and runners, blow up barrels and collect scrap gears. Campaign of 100 levels in 10 chapters with 20 titan bosses, plus endless survival mode. Unlock the Iron Fang, the Dune Reaper buggy and the heavy War Titan, upgrade armor and turbo, and pick weapon modules between waves: miniguns, buzzsaws, flamethrowers and rocket mortars.

Por que você pode gostar: Se você curtiu Nitrovenant 2 e Last Garage: Zombie Road, experimente Zombie Drift: Steel Rage: eles têm as tags “car” e “racing” em comum.

Como jogar Zombie Drift: Steel Rage

Keyboard W Up - throttle S Down - brake reverse A D - steer Space E - nitro Shift F - handbrake drift Esc P - pause Mouse menu upgrade cards drag to rotate the car in the garage Touch left side - floating stick DRIFT and NITRO b.
