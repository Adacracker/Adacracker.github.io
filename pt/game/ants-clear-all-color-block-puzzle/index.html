Sobre Ants Clear All : Color Block Puzzle

Que tal um desafio para a mente? Ants Clear All : Color Block Puzzle é um jogo de quebra-cabeça grátis que roda direto no seu navegador. Funciona no celular, no tablet e no computador. Ele é jogado com o mouse, toques e o toque na tela. No celular, segure na vertical: o jogo foi feito para o modo retrato.

Descrição de GameMonetize (em inglês)

Send out your ants and carry every colorful block away! Ants Clear All : Color Block Puzzle is a single-player puzzle game about matching colors and planning your moves. Click or tap a box in the front row to deploy an ant team. Your tiny helpers automatically collect reachable blocks of their own color. With limited deployment slots, choosing the right order is the key to keeping your teams moving. Clear exposed blocks to open paths to new colors, free your slots, and work through colorful pixel-art boards until the last block is gone. Enjoy cheerful ants, satisfying block-carrying animations, and simple mouse and touch controls. Can you clear them all?

Como jogar Ants Clear All : Color Block Puzzle

Mouse click a front-row box to deploy ants Keys 1-5 select a column Escape pause back Mobile tap boxes and on-screen buttons Ants automatically collect matching blocks Clear the board to win retry if stuck.
