What are parking games?

Parking games come in two flavours. Driving parking games put you behind the wheel and ask you to steer into a marked bay without touching anything. Parking jam puzzles show a crowded lot from above, and you work out the order in which cars can leave.

What makes a good one?

In driving parking games, a good camera and gentle steering matter most. In parking jam puzzles, clear arrows on each car and an undo button make the logic fun rather than fiddly.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

If you do not like time pressure, start with parking jam puzzles. If you like driving, try 3D parking games - start slow, use the brake and look at the camera angles. Puzzles work well on phones; driving games are easier with arrow keys.

What controls do parking games use?

Driving parking games use arrow keys or W A S D (or on-screen pedals). Parking jam puzzles only need a tap or a swipe on each car.
