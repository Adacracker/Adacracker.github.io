What are .io games?

.io games are named after the web address ending that games like Agar.io and Slither.io made famous. The idea is simple: you drop into a big arena, start small, eat, collect or knock out others to grow, and try to reach the top of the leaderboard before someone bigger gets you. Some are true online multiplayer games; others fill the arena with computer players.

What makes a good one?

A good .io game explains itself in five seconds, puts you into a round immediately and gives a small player a real chance against a big one - by being faster, sneakier or smarter. Fair respawns and short rounds keep the 'one more go' feeling going.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

Everyone starts at the bottom in an .io game, which makes them great for beginners. Start with snake or hole-swallowing games that only need the mouse or one finger. Experienced players can try arena shooters and team modes. Multiplayer games need a steady internet connection.

.io Games: questions and answers

What does .io mean in game names?

It started as the web address of early hits like Agar.io. Today it stands for a style of quick arena game where you grow by collecting or defeating others.

Are .io games really multiplayer?

Some connect you to real players online; others use computer-controlled opponents. The description on each game page usually says which.

Do I need an account to play?

No. You can play every .io game here without signing up; at most you type a nickname.

Are .io games free?

Yes, all 17 .io games here are free in your browser. Ads from the provider may show between rounds.
