What are obby games?

An obby is an obstacle course game: you run and jump across moving platforms, lava floors, spinning bars and towers, and every fall sends you back to the last checkpoint. The style became hugely popular on block-building platforms, and these browser obbies bring the same blocky parkour, noob escapes and +1 speed challenges without installing anything.

What makes a good one?

Good obbies have fair jumps, frequent checkpoints and a steady climb in difficulty, so every fall teaches you something. Variety matters too: ice, lava, conveyor belts and moving platforms in the same course keep it fresh.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

New to obbies? Pick one with lots of checkpoints and short stages. If you want a challenge, try the tower climbs and speed-run obbies. Many have 3D camera controls, so a keyboard and mouse is easiest, though several work with touch.
