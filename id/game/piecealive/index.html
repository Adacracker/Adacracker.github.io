Tentang PieceAlive

Saatnya main puzzle. PieceAlive adalah game puzzle yang bisa kamu mainkan gratis di Arcade Cabin, tanpa perlu unduh. Kamu bisa memainkannya di komputer atau layar sentuh. Mainkan dengan mouse, ketukan, dan sentuhan. Di HP, miringkan ke samping agar layarnya lebar (mode lanskap).

Deskripsi dari GameMonetize (dalam bahasa Inggris)

PieceAlive is a relaxing visual puzzle adventure where beautiful scenes come together piece by piece. Choose a world, select Easy, Moderate, or Hard, then study the picture and rebuild it by dragging every piece into its correct position. Complete scenes to bring them to life, earn mastery stars and coins, unlock new locations, and return for a fresh Daily Challenge. Explore 50 scenes featuring famous landmarks, wildlife, nature, and breathtaking places. Use hints when you get stuck and try to master every scene across all difficulty levels.
