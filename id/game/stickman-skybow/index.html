Tentang Stickman Skybow

Stickman Skybow adalah game aksi gratis yang langsung jalan di browser kamu. Kamu bisa memainkannya di komputer atau layar sentuh. Mainkan dengan tombol panah, mouse, dan sentuhan. Di HP, miringkan ke samping agar layarnya lebar (mode lanskap).

Deskripsi dari GameMonetize (dalam bahasa Inggris)

Stickman Skybow is a fast-paced 2D archery game with two modes: Duel and Defense. Draw, aim and release to defeat distinctive bosses across changing floors, or hold back waves of enemies. Unlock bows, special arrows, heroes and permanent upgrades with coins earned through play. Day and night scenery alternates through the Duel floors. Play with mouse on desktop or touch in landscape on mobile.

Kenapa kamu mungkin suka: Stickman Skybow punya banyak kesamaan dengan Bow or Die dan Crumple Rumble - sama-sama bertag “stickman” dan “action”.

Cara main Stickman Skybow

Desktop drag left to charge the bow drag down to aim higher then release to shoot Touch use the same drag and release gesture Choose arrows with the bottom buttons or number keys 1-8 Use Pause and Sound buttons at the top.
