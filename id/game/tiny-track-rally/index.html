Tentang Tiny Track Rally

Pasang sabuk pengaman: kamu bisa main Tiny Track Rally, game mobil dari kategori Mobil & Balap, gratis di browser. Jalan di desktop maupun ponsel. Game ini dimainkan dengan tombol panah dan tombol spasi. Di HP, miringkan ke samping agar layarnya lebar (mode lanskap).

Deskripsi dari GameMonetize (dalam bahasa Inggris)

Race tiny toy cars in this colourful 3D arcade racer! Drift through corners, grab pickups, fire your nitro on the straights and beat up to 7 rival drivers to the finish line. Features: - 10 tracks in 2 cups. Cup 2 is bigger and harder, with crates, barrels, cones, narrow walls and moving barriers - Rain, fog, sunset and night races with headlights - 5 modes: Race, Time Trial, Elimination, Checkpoint and Daily Challenge - 6 camera views, from close chase to top-down - Garage: unlock cars and a bike, pick from 8 paint colours, and upgrade speed, acceleration, handling and drift - Coins, nitro refills and boost pads on the track.

Kenapa kamu mungkin suka: Penggemar Zombie Drift: Steel Rage dan Extreme Gear Shift Racing mungkin suka yang ini juga, karena punya tag “racing” dan “drift” yang sama.
