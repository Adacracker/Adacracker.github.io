Deskripsi dari GameMonetize (dalam bahasa Inggris)

NovaBrick Rush is a fast-paced arcade brick breaker game set in a futuristic space world. Break colorful bricks, keep the ball in play, collect powerful power-ups, and complete challenging levels. Features: Multiple challenging levels Different brick types Exciting power-ups Extra Life & Bottom Shield Expand & Shrink Paddle Slow Ball & Multi-Ball Smooth mobile touch controls Keyboard controls for desktop Score and lives system Futuristic neon visuals How to Play: Break all the bricks to complete each level. Keep the ball from falling, collect power-ups, and unlock new levels as you progress.

Kenapa kamu mungkin suka: NovaBrick Rush punya banyak kesamaan dengan Mag Roller dan Color Brick Battle - sama-sama bertag “ball” dan “arcade”.
