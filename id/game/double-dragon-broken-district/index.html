Tentang Double Dragon: Broken District

Tangan yang cekatan sangat membantu di sini. Double Dragon: Broken District adalah game aksi gratis yang langsung jalan di browser kamu. Bisa dimainkan di HP, tablet, dan komputer. Deskripsi dari GameMonetize (dalam bahasa Inggris)

Double Dragon: Broken District is a martial arts story through four missions. Choose Mina or Ken and fight beside an AI sibling to find a missing trainer after your dojo burns. Chain punches and kicks, dodge enemy windups, throw foes, carry staffs and unleash Twin Spirit attacks.
