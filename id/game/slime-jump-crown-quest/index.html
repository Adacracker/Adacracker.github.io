Tentang Slime Jump: Crown Quest

Punya waktu semenit? Kamu bisa main Slime Jump: Crown Quest, game arcade dari kategori Arcade & Kasual, gratis di browser. Kamu bisa memainkannya di komputer atau layar sentuh. Mainkan dengan mouse dan sentuhan. Di HP, miringkan ke samping agar layarnya lebar (mode lanskap).

Deskripsi dari GameMonetize (dalam bahasa Inggris)

Jump toward the crown in Slime Jump: Crown Quest! Guide a cheerful slime through 150 colorful platform levels. Hold, aim, and release to launch your slime, land on platforms, and plan your next bounce. Short stages and simple mouse or touch controls make every attempt easy to start, while precise jumps reward timing and skill.
