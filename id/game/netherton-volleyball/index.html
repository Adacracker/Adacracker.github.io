Tentang Netherton Volleyball

Pemanasan dulu: Netherton Volleyball langsung dimuat di halaman ini sebagai game olahraga gratis. Jalan di desktop maupun ponsel. Game ini dimainkan dengan tombol spasi, mouse, dan ketukan. Di HP, pegang secara tegak: game ini dibuat untuk mode potret.

Deskripsi dari GameMonetize (dalam bahasa Inggris)

Tap on green, spike over the block, win the cup! Netherton Volleyball is a quick pixel-art volleyball game where you call the shots for a whole 6v6 team. Time your taps to serve, pass, dig and block, then pick your hitter: each rings color shows the chance to score. Your players level up after every match and learn new moves, from quick sets and pipes to jump serves and diving saves. Beat 18 rival teams, each with its own style, and lift the cup in a best-of-five final. Short matches, real volleyball rules, and the first match teaches you as you play. Game features: - Campaign: an 18-team ladder, best-of-three matches to 11 points, a best-of.
