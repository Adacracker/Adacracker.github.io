GameMonetize açıklaması (İngilizce)

Rope Chef is a cute physics puzzle about cooking. Ingredients hang on ropes above a pot. Cut the ropes at the right moment so each one swings, bounces or floats into the pot in the order the recipe card shows. Get the order right and the dish is served. Miss, or drop the wrong one first, and Nibbs the steamed-bun chef swoons dramatically. 50 short levels across 14 cuisines: Singapore hawker, Japan, Italy, Mexico, India, Korea, France, USA, Thailand, Middle East, China, Vietnam, Spain and Peru. Every world adds a twist: bounce pads, bubbles you pop, fans, a moving pot, a hot pan, portals, conveyor belts, flippers, wind, a rival chefs decoy pot, burning ropes and slippery ice. Earn up to 3 stars per dish: serve it, grab the spice, beat par time. Unlock worlds with stars, keep a daily streak, use the ghost line to preview a swing, and skip a dish you are stuck on. Nibbs cheers, winks, sneezes and juggles along the way. Works on desktop and mobile.

Neden hoşuna gidebilir: Slime Buffet ve Merge the Presidents hayranları bunu da sevebilir, çünkü onların “puzzle” ve “brain” etiketlerini paylaşıyor.
