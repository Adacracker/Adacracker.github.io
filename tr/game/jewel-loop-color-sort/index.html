GameMonetize açıklaması (İngilizce)

Sort colorful jewels around a looping conveyor and fill matching spaces to reveal sparkling pictures. Plan each move around limited belt capacity and a ticking clock, use helpful boosters, and explore 1,478 puzzle levels. Progress is saved automatically.
