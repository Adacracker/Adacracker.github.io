What are cat games?

Cat games put a cat in the lead role. Some are puzzles where you guide a kitty to a goal, others are pet care and dress-up games, cat cafes and simulators where you play as a mischievous cat, plus runners and merge games with a feline theme.

What makes a good one?

Cats are funny because they are unpredictable, and good cat games lean into that with playful animations, sound effects and a bit of chaos. Care and cafe games should have plenty of cats to collect and decorate.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

For a calm game, pick a cat puzzle or a cat care game. For something sillier, choose a cat simulator where knocking things over is the goal. Most cat games here are simple enough for younger players.
