What are games like Subway Surfers?

Endless runners move your character forward automatically; your job is to switch lanes, jump and slide to avoid obstacles while collecting coins and power-ups. The run ends when you crash, and the goal is to beat your best distance. This collection also includes level-based 3D runners and 'crowd' runners where you collect people as you go.

What makes a good one?

A good runner speeds up gradually, makes obstacles readable from far away and has power-ups that feel worth grabbing. Missions and unlockable characters give you a reason to come back after a bad run.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

Runners are easy to start: swipe on a phone or use the arrow keys on a computer. If you are new, choose a lane-based runner with three lanes - it is the most forgiving. For more variety, try level-based runners with gates and upgrades.

Games like Subway Surfers: questions and answers

Is Subway Surfers on this page?

No. These are running games that play in a similar way, made by other developers, and all of them are free in the browser.

How do you play runner games on a computer?

Use the arrow keys or W A S D: left and right to change lanes, up to jump and down to slide.
