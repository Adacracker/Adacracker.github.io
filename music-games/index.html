What are music and rhythm games?

Rhythm games ask you to press, tap or swipe in time with music. Piano tile games scroll notes towards you, dance battles show arrows to hit on the beat, and rhythm runners sync jumps to the song. Some games here are music makers where you build your own beats.

What makes a good one?

Tight timing is everything: a good rhythm game registers your tap exactly when you made it and gives feedback such as 'perfect' or 'good'. Songs that start easy and get busier help you learn.

How to choose: beginners and experienced players

New players should start with piano tiles on the slowest speed. Rhythm fans can try dance battles with arrow keys. Use headphones if you can, and play on a device without lag.
